8. FINANCE, INNOVATION AND INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY 
(i) 
Longford County Council Digital & ICT Strategy  
Head of IT, Danny Lynch made a detailed presentation to the members on the final 
update on Longford’s Digital & ICT Strategy. The presentation included the 
following:- 
➢ Dream Space 

➢ Stem Passport Inclusion  
➢ Post Primary Tech Awards 
➢ Working with industry and school network 
The members thanked Danny and his team for the excellent work they are doing 
across the county to promote computer science among young students in our 
schools. 
(ii) 
Midlands STEM EUJT Project – From Classroom to Community: Ready for 2030 
Christine Collins, Digital Broadband Officer, and Siobhan Grealy, STEM 
Engagement Officer made a detailed presentation to the members on Midlands 
Ireland EUJTF Project.  The presentation included the following: 
 
➢ Developing STEM in our Schools  
➢ Key STEM Programmes  
➢ Dream Big TY Programme  
➢ Longford Dream Space  
➢ VEX Robotics  
➢ Other STEM iniatives including, Lego League, Hydrogen Cars, Drones and VR 
Headsets 
➢ Supporting: Ericsson’s TY Infuse Programme & STEM Passport for Inclusion  
➢ Midlands Showcase  
➢ STEM Passport for Inclusion 
➢ Awards and Recognition  
➢ Looking forward  
➢ The results and impact  
➢ Alignment with Ireland’s national and EU digital policies  
 
The members thanked Christine and Siobhan for their presentation and for their 
continued dedication to the work they are doing in the community.
