Draft minutes of the Meeting of  
Westmeath Local Community Development Committee, (LCDC) held 
 on Friday 16th December 2022 at 10.00am (Hybrid Meeting).   

2 
 
Hugh Farrell welcomed all LCDC members, Westmeath County Council staff and Westmeath Community 
Development staff to the meeting.  He also extended a special welcome to Teresa Kennedy (HSE) to her 
first LCDC Meeting. Hugh thanked Pat Gallagher for stepping in as chairperson for the last meeting.   
 
Hugh Farrell reminded members of the confidentiality of the items on today’s agenda and asked that 
they declare any conflicts of interests that they may have.   
 
 
1.  
Draft Minutes of LCDC Meeting held on Friday 2nd December 2022 
 
On the proposal of Noel Maguire and seconded by Margaret Kiernan the minutes of the LCDC meeting 
held on Friday 2nd December 2022 were approved.   
 
2.  
Matters Arising 
Pat Murtagh updated members in relation to the Expression of Interest (EOI) for the LEADER Programme 
2023 – 2027, informing them that it has been agreed to submit a joint EOI between the LCDC and 
Westmeath Community Development Ltd, the implementing partner. This submission is prepared and 
will be sent to the Department of Rural and Community Development before close of business today. It 
is anticipated that a DRCD appointed board will examine submitted EOIs and respond in early 2023.  
 
3. 
Community Climate Action Programme 2021 -2024. 
John Jackson, Community Climate Officer WCC, gave a presentation on the Community Climate Action 
Programme. Maria Fox asked John for indicative grant amounts. John said it would be in the region of 
€10,000 to €20,000 per project. Pat Gallagher asked how much of each project will be grant funded. John 
replied that approved projects will be 100% grant funded. Pat Gallagher stated that 100% grant aid is 
very welcome and will be a big help when trying to promote the programme. Annette Barr Jordan 
informed the members that John Jackson will be attending the funding workshops early in the new year 
and will be available to discuss the Programme with community groups. Hugh Farrell thanked John 
Jackson for his presentation.  
 
 
4.  
 SICAP  
a) Annette Barr Jordan gave the LCDC Members an update on the SICAP Programme. There is a 3% 
increase in funding for the programme for 2023.  The SICAP Subcommittee have met and continue 
to finalise the Annual Plan. The plan must be submitted on the IRIS System by Westmeath 
Community development Ltd on or before 13th January 2023 and brought back before the LCDC 
for consideration by 28th February 2023.

6.  
AOB  
Pat Murtagh informed the members that we now have a full complement of LCDC Members. Training will 
be arranged for new members in January 2023. Pat added that any of the members who wish to attend 
as a refresher course are more than welcome to join in the training.  
 
 
7.  
Schedule of Meetings for 2023 
Pat Murtagh discussed the proposed Schedule of Meetings for 2023.  He is hoping to have two meetings 
in community settings (locations/ facilities previously funded through the LEADER Programme). It would 
be beneficial for members to see the finished projects while also promote the upcoming LEADER 
Programme. It was suggested that both the March and October meetings in 2023 will be held in 
community settings. This was agreed by all members.  
 
8.  
Date of Next Meeting 
The next LCDC meeting is scheduled for the 17th February 2023 at 10am. Frank Murtagh suggested that 
a short meeting be held in January if needs to be to facilitate LEADER Projects which may be ready for 
LCDC consideration. Members agreed to this suggestion if the need arises.  
 
Hugh Farrell thanked everyone for attending and for their work all year. He wished the members a Happy 
Christmas and a safe journey home. He also complimented Westmeath County Council on the condition 
of the roads. They are in very good condition considering the freezing conditions and bad weather.
