ITEM NO. 1 
 
To consider the variation of the basic rate of Local Property Tax and the setting of the Local Adjustment 
Factor in accordance with the Finance (Local Property Tax) Act 2012 (as amended) to apply for the period 
as specified from the next local property tax liability date 1st November 2023. 
 
Elected members were circulated with: 
 
1. Report of Chief Executive dated 4th October 2023 to Members on the matters to which Wicklow 
County Council shall have regard to in the setting of the Local Adjustment Factor from the Chief 
Executive, Ms. Emer O’Gorman. 
 
Mr. B. Gleeson, Head of Finance gave a presentation on Local Property Tax (LPT) and the setting of the 
Local Adjustment Factor under the following areas: 
 
 
Summary. 
 
2024 Budget process. 
 
LPT Baseline review. 
 
LPT 2024 allocation. 
 
LPT (Local Adjustment Factor) Regulations 2014. 
 
Estimate of income and expenditure 2024. 
 
2024 Budget adjustments. 
 
The financial position of Wicklow County Council. 
 
Financial effect on a liable person. 
 
Impact on Wicklow County Council revenue budget and service delivery for 2024. 
 
Public consultation process. 
 
Local Property Tax 2024 in summary.  Recommendation to retain the LPT variation rate at 6%; no 
increase to householder LPT bills in 2024; generates €1.2m to be ring-fenced for discretionary 
funding to Municipal Districts; €297,000 for Bray Municipal District and€223,000 for other 4 
Municipal Districts; and supports local public realm projects and community/voluntary 
organisations. 
 
  
In concluding, Mr. Gleeson recommended that the Local Property Tax rate is maintained at 6% which will 
generate revenue of €1.2m to be ring-fenced for discretionary funding for the Municipal Districts, but 
ensures households face no increase in their Local Property Tax charge for 2024. 
 
Elected Members made the following contributions: 
 
 
Number of projects midstream – important to have a level of discretionary spending. 
 
Concerns with the concept of LPT. 
 
Flawed system that restricts what counties like Wicklow can retain in property tax. 
 
Baseline needs to be addressed. 
 
Discretionary monies should not be allocated to general footpath maintenance. 
 
Increase in cost of living. 
 
People want to know where LPT is being spent in their local communities when they see services 
being reduced. 
 
A lot of positivity on the ground – provision of defibrillators; footpath repairs; maintenance of 
carparks; roads resurfaced; provision of Christmas lights. 
 
 
 
 

The following proposals were put forward: 
 
It was proposed by Cllr. P. Fitzgerald and seconded by Cllr. S. Bourke:  That the Local Property Tax be 
retained at the 2023 level, which is an upward variation of the baseline rate by 6% for 2024. 
 
It was proposed by Cllr. P. Leonard and seconded by Cllr. M. Kavanagh: That the Local Property Tax 
remains at the baseline rate of 0%. 
 
The proposal put forward by Cllr. P. Fitzgerald and seconded by Cllr. S. Bourke:   
 
‘In accordance with the provisions of Section 20 of the Finance (Local Property Tax) Act 2012, as amended, 
that the basic rate of Local Property Tax should stand varied upwards by 6% for 2024 in respect of relevant 
residential properties situated in the administrative area of Wicklow County Council.’ 
 
This was put to a vote and following a roll call was passed by a margin of 20 votes for, 7 against and 5 not 
present viz:-
