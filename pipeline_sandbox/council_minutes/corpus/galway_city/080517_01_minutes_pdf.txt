Standing Order No 2 (Prayer/Reflection at Commencement)
Two options were considered and debated by Members as included in the document.
a) A Prayer recited by the Mayor or Deputy Mayor. Such prayer may be recited in Irish or
English.
b) A statement of intention to conduct the business of the Meeting in line with agreed Standing
Orders followed by moment of silent reflection

Standing Order the chair of the Meeting Deputy Mayor, Cllr. P. Flannery used his casting vote in
favour of Option (b). Option (b) was carried.
