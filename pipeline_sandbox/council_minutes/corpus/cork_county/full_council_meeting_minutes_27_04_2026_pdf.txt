NOTICES OF MOTION 
 
FOGRAÍ RÚN 
 
MARINE PROTECTED AREAS 
 
  13/4-1 
 
That this Council would write to the Minister for State at the Department of Agriculture, Food 
and the Marine Timmy Dooley TD, requesting that Cork County Council be formally 
recognised and included as a key statutory stakeholder in the consultation, planning, 
designation, and management processes for all Marine Protected Areas within or adjacent to 
the County and to request, structured engagement mechanisms to ensure ongoing consultation 
with Cork County Council throughout the development and operational phases of such Marine 
Protected Areas, particularly where Council-owned slipways, piers, harbours, or marine 
facilities may be affected in our Coastal and Island communities, and that we circulate this 
motion to all coastal local authorities seeking their support.” 
 
At the agreement of the Members, it was decided that this motion would be deferred to the next 
meeting of Full Council on 11/05/2025. 
 
CORE FUNDING MODEL 
 
  14/4-1 
 
To write to the Minister for Children, Disability & Equality, Norma Foley to increase funding 
for staff wages & operational overheads under the Core Funding Model for Childcare 
providers.” 
 

At the agreement of the Members, it was decided that this motion would be deferred to the next 
meeting of Full Council on 11/05/2025. 
 
ACCESS OFFICER  
 
  15/4-1 
 
That Cork County Council would clarify the following: 
o Does Cork County Council have an Access Officer? 
o If so, what is the defined role and parameters of the Access Officer 
 
And will ensure the following: 
o That at least one Access Officer is in a full-time dedicated position (not spread 
across other roles) 
o The Access Officer will engage closely with the Housing Directorate, 
personally assist those seeking housing support, train staff to recognise when 
someone requires more assistance and how to give that assistance. 
o The Access Officer’s contact details will be shared with disability groups, 
councillors and the public. 
 
At the agreement of the Members, it was decided that this motion would be deferred to the 
next meeting of Full Council on 11/05/2025. 
 
BARRYROE OIL AND GAS 
 
  16/4-1 
 
That Cork County Council write to the Minister for Climate, Environment and Energy and 
request that the development of Barryroe Oil and Gas field be prioritised as a matter of energy 
security for the Irish state.”  
 
At the agreement of the Members, it was decided that this motion would be deferred to the next 
meeting of Full Council on 11/05/2025. 
 
 
FAMILY RESOURCE CENTRES 
 
  17/4-1 
 
That Cork County Council call on the Minister for Children, Disability and Equality, Norma 
Foley to: Recognise and formally acknowledge the ongoing pressures faced by Family 
Resource Centres and commit to a long-term funding model that reflects their central role in 
community wellbeing and social inclusion. 
 
1. Provide a minimum level of core funding of €250,000 per annum to all Family 
Resource Centres nationally, ensuring sustainable and equitable service delivery. 

2. Introduce dedicated capital and stand-alone funding streams to enable Family 
Resource Centres to secure, develop, or upgrade premises that are fit for purpose. 
3. Support and promote the integration and co-location of Family Resource Centres 
with other key community and statutory services, including Citizens Information, 
youth services, and family supports, to enhance accessibility and outcomes for 
service users.” 
At the agreement of the Members, it was decided that this motion would be deferred to the next 
meeting of Full Council on 11/05/2025. 
 
THE COMMON CONDITIONS SERVICE 
 
  18/4-1 
 
That this Council would write to the Minister for Health, Jennifer Carroll MacNeill, and the 
Health Service Executive, seeking an urgent update on the rollout of the Common Conditions 
Service in community pharmacies, and calling for the expedited national expansion of 
pharmacists’ prescribing powers to include a wider range of minor ailments.” 
 
At the agreement of the Members, it was decided that this motion would be deferred to the next 
meeting of Full Council on 11/05/2025. 
 
ACCESS AND INCLUSION OFFICER

At the agreement of the Members, it was decided that this motion would be deferred to the next
