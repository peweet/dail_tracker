Agenda 
 
1. 
Fógra um (a) leasanna coinbhleachtaí nó (b) leasanna airgeadais nó leasanna 
tairbhúla faoin alt 177 den Acht Rialtais Áitiúil 2001, arna leasú.        
Declarations of (a) conflicts of interest or (b) pecuniary or beneficial interests under 
section 177 of the Local Government Act 2001, as amended. 
 
2. 
Chun miontuairiscí cruinniú speisialta an 15ú Meán Fomhair 2025 a faomhadh (cóip 
ceangailte). 
To adopt the minutes of the Special Meeting held on 15 September 2025 (attached). 
 
3. 
An Comhairleoir - Councillor T. McDonnell 
Seconded by - Councillors N. Heavey and B. O’Loughlin 
That Kildare County Council resolves to nominate Maria Steen of 31 Avoca Avenue, 
Dublin, A94 T4A3, as a candidate for the forthcoming election for the Office of 
President of Ireland pursuant to Article 12 of Bunreacht na hÉireann and the 
Presidential Election Act 1993 as amended.
