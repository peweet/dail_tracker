The Meeting commenced with a statement of intention to conduct the business of the
Meeting in line with Standing Orders followed by a moment of silent reflection. Mayor
Flannery welcomed all to the Meeting.
Ms. N. Moggan, A/Head of Finance referred Councillors to the draft Budget 2018 Report
issued in advance of the Meeting and presented an overview of the process and the
significant issues for consideration.
Councillors were advised of the initial presentation of the Budget Strategy to the Corporate
Policy Group on the 8th September 2017 and the decision not to vary rate of Local Property
Tax for 2018 taken at the Ordinary Meeting of the City Council on 11th September 2017. An
outline Capital & Revenue Budget was submitted to Department Housing, Planning,
Community & Local Government on the 22nd September 2017 and consultations with
Corporate Policy Group were held on the 18th October and 7th November 2017.
Ms. Moggan advised Councillors that decisions were now required on the determination of
percentage of refund to apply in respect of commercial rates on vacant properties for 2018,
Adoption of Annual Budget 2018, the determination of Annual Rate on Valuation to be
levied for 2018, and approval of Rates Payment Incentive Scheme for 2018.
1. Consideration of Draft Annual Budget 2018 and adoption of Annual Budget 2018
Ms. Moggan presented an overall Summary of 2018 Budget noting proposed budgeted
expenditure of €77,966,575, no increase to ARV for Commercial Rates, no increase in Local
Property Tax, an assumption that the commercial rates rebate on vacant premises will
continue at 50% for 2018 and no increase in local charges. Additional expenditure is

Ms. Moggan noted that this Draft Budget supports the ongoing implementation of the
Council's Corporate Priorities into 2018, will drive the economic development of Galway
City, ensure provision of funding to progress European Capital of Culture Designation,
continued projects under the City Centre Transport Plan, Capital works under the
Designated Urban Grant Scheme, continuation of the Marketing & Promotion Fund and
increased resources to Economic Development and Tourism, drive the inclusive and
equitable socio-economic development of the City including increased funding towards
Homeless Services, continued improvement of our Social Housing stock, ongoing funding for
Traveller Accomodation and ongoing significant funding for Arts and related activities,
refocus on legal and regulatory responsibilities with provision in 2018 for the
implementation of the General Data Protection Regulations and our obligations and
responsibilities under Climate Change and revitalise the organisation by provision for filling
of vacant posts, provision for ICT development projects and ensuring a healthy and safe
work environment.
Queries, clarifications and questions were invited by Mayor Flannery. Councillors offered
compliments to Ms. Moggan and her team on the presentation and her availability to meet
Councillors in advance of the meeting to discuss the Draft Budget. A number of Councillors
sought specific clarification on expenditure items in the Draft Budget and this information
was provided by the A/Head of Finance and relevant Director of Services. Councillors noted
the positive achievements in 2017 and the allocation of funding across the programme
divisions for 2018.
