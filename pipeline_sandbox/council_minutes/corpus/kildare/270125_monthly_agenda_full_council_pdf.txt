21. 
Councillor Noel Connolly 
Go dtabharfaidh Comhairle Contae Chill Dara tuairisc do na Comhaltaí ar an ngníomh 
atá á dhéanamh chun rialáil an mhargaidh cíosa ghearrthéarma sa Chontae a fhiosrú 
agus a fhorghníomhú; líon na maoine i gCill Dara atá ar fáil faoi láthair ar cíos trí 
Airbnb agus/nó Booking.com a áireamh; líon na maoine sin atá á ligean ar cíos ar 
feadh níos lú ná 90 lá agus atá cláraithe le haghaidh díolúine; líon na réadmhaoine 
seo atá á ligean ar cíos ar feadh níos mó ná 90 lá agus a bhfuil iarratas déanta acu ar 
chead pleanála le haghaidh athrú úsáide; agus má tá aon áitribh i gCill Dara faoi 
láthair atá ar ligean gearrthéarmach ar feadh bliana. 
 
That Kildare County Council reports to the Members on the action being taken to 
investigate and enforce the regulation of the short term rental market in the County; to 
include the number of properties in Kildare that are currently available to rent through 
Airbnb and/or Booking.com; the number of these properties that are being rented for 
less than 90 days and have registered for an exemption; the number of these 
properties that are being rented for more than 90 days and have applied for planning 
permission for a change of use; and if there are currently any properties in Kildare that 
are year-long short-term lets.

variable positioned speed cameras and other technical advances used in other 
countries, to Transport SPC to interact with Road Safety Authority and An Garda 
Síochána a sustainable means of enforcing compliance to speed limits.

Councillor Nuala Killeen 
That the council establishes a pilot to trial the High Street Rental Auctions in one

25. 
Councillor Chris Pender 
That Kildare County Council acknowledges the critical role of Homeless HAP 
(Housing Assistance Payment) in addressing and preventing homelessness and 
recognises that its application in Kildare appears to differ significantly from other local 
authorities, where it is often used as a preventive tool. To address this, the council is 
requested to provide a comprehensive report detailing the current criteria and 
processes for awarding Homeless HAP, the rationale behind these criteria, and how 
they compare to other counties, alongside relevant statistics on its use and outcomes. 
Furthermore, the council is urged to immediately review the existing criteria with a 
view to expanding its use as a homelessness prevention measure, supporting 
individuals and families at risk of losing their homes, and aligning with national best 
practices.
