Notices of Motion
Cllr. S. Maher
Offaly County Council calls on the Government to implement the necessary measures impelling the highly
profitable insurance sector, pass on premium reductions to the hard pressed SME and Transport sector.
Cllr. C. Murray 
This Council condemns corporations that profit from protracted armed conflicts and
systematic human rights violations, particularly in relation to Israel’s assault on Gaza,
escalating settler violence in the West Bank, Russia’s ongoing war in Ukraine, and other
global conflicts. In response, the Council resolves to adopt a rights-based Ethical
Procurement Policy (EPP) that adheres to international standards, ensuring human rights
are respected throughout the procurement process. The policy encourages companies to
meet their obligations to avoid contributing to human rights abuses and to mitigate any
negative impacts linked to their operations.
