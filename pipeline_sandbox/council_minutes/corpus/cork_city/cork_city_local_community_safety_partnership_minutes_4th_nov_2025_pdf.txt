5. Approval of the Terms of Reference and Standing Orders;

approval of such. Proposed by Cllr Joe Kavanagh, seconded by Cllr Margaret McDonnell.

Sandy read out the Terms of Reference related to the election process.

Cllr Terry Shannon drawn to make the second presentation.

*Elections results corrected to Cllr Shannon= 14, Ms O Connell= 10, as an error had  been 
made during the tallying of votes. 
 
Cllr Shannon duly elected as Chair and Chaired the rest of the meeting.

Cllr O Connor duly elected as Vice Chair and took her seat.
