The Meetings Administrator commenced the meeting by quoting Standing Order 3, as per the Local
Government Act 2001, which requires that in the case of an Annual Meeting in an election year and until
the election of the Mayor at such meeting, the Chair for this period shall be taken by a Councillor or an
employee selected by the meeting for this purpose. Any Councillor selected to Chair an Annual Meeting
under this subparagraph shall not have a second or casting vote and any employee of the Local Authority
if so selected in accordance with this subparagraph shall not have any vote.
Cllr. P. Keane proposed that the outgoing Mayor take the Chair, to give a synopsis of the year and take
contributions. This was seconded by Cllr. J. Connolly.

such a time as the new Mayor is elected. This was seconded by Cllr. T. O'Flaherty.

Motion is taken, then the Motion, and proceeded to take a vote, as instructed by Standing Orders and the
Local Government Act 2001.

Cllr. M. Cubbard, having received the Majority of the votes of the Members present, was formally
elected Mayor of Galway. He proceeded to take the Chair for the remainder of the meeting.
On taking the Chair, Mayor Cubbard thanked Cllr. N. Larkin and Cllr. P. O'Reilly for their nomination. He
acknowledged Cllr. P. Keane, and welcomed the new Councillors and congratulated all Members on their
election. The Mayor acknowledged the work of those Members who were not re-elected, those who had
retired, and all those who had put their names before the electorate. The Mayor thanked his family and
campaign team.
The Mayor advised Members that he would have an open and fair working manner, to support
productive and progressive Council Meetings. He welcome suggestions from Members to improve
meetings. The Mayor acknowledged the importance of the role, when Galway City will be on a world
stage as part of Galway2020. The Mayor went on to set out his priorities as follows:
•
Housing - to address the housing crisis and bring about an improvement; to get children in the
city out of hotels and B&Bs;
Mental Health — to reduce lives lost, without hope. The Mayor wishes to use his term to highlight
positive mental health, and to work with the previous Mayor to build on achievements during his
term;
Traffic Congestion — to improve public transport, increase provision of bus shelters and connect
cycle lanes. The Mayor is committed to an accessible city, which is safer for those with
disabilities;
Sports - To support sports facilities in the pipeline, to deliver participation in sport;
Climate Change — to deliver flood defences for the city;
Policing — to address drug culture and advocate for community policing;
The Mayor highlighted the importance of engaging with schools and young people, and the
importance of installation of safety measures on the River Corrib to prevent tragedies involving
young people.
The Mayor went on to say that he wished to send a message to young people that if he could become
Mayor of the City of Galway, you can do it too.
The Mayor advised that he wished to put an end to unacceptable behaviour which had taken place in the
Chamber in the past, in order to make it productive and progressive for the people of Galway.
Councillors congratulated the Mayor on is election, and wished him well in his period as Mayor.

Item 5. Appointment of Chairpersons to Five (5) Strategic Policy Committees (SPC)
The Meetings Administrator summarised the process of appointments of Chairs to the Strategic Policy
Committees. The allocation of S.P.C. Chairs should reflect equitably the spread of elected representation
on the Council. However, where agreement in this regard is not possible the group of S.P.C. Chairs to be
appointed should be treated for appointment purposes as if it constituted a separate committee, i.e.
group system applying. Councillors are also advised that if they choose to proceed with appointments at
this meeting they could select five (5) Councillors as Chairpersons without selecting the S.P.C.s to which
they would be appointed, which could be decided at a later date when the S.P.C. scheme is completed.

Item 8. To Fix Dates for Meetings of Galway City Council
The Chief Executive advised Members that the practice has been that Ordinary Meetings are held on the
second Monday of each month, except for the month of August.
Cllr. D. McDonnell proposed that meetings take place at 3.00pm on the second Monday of each month,
excluding June and August 2019. The June Meeting is proposed for 24th June 2019.
The motion was seconded by Cllr. M. O'Connor and agreed.
