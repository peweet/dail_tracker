Evidence methodology

Keep every commercial signal tied to its data grain.

PublicSignal is a presentation layer over reviewed Dáil Tracker procurement contracts. It is designed to help a person find and inspect evidence, not manufacture a commercial conclusion.

Every current opportunity should link to its originating notice. If a source link or field appears wrong, report it to hello@publicsignal.ie.

Current opportunities

The opportunity desk uses a private, public-safe snapshot of active forward notices. Filters operate over published buyer names, stated CPV divisions, deadlines and advertised values. Missing values remain missing; PublicSignal does not infer them.

Sectors and buyers

Sector rows are grouped by stated CPV division. Buyer rows use the exact published buyer display name. Spelling variants are not silently merged in the public view. Counts describe the loaded snapshot, not the whole addressable market.

Suppliers and awards

Supplier rows describe historic national award records. TED award-notice activity is attached only when both sources resolve to the same unique CRO company number. A name resemblance is not treated as an entity match.

Money fields

  • Advertised notice estimates, awarded contract values and disclosed public-body payments are different measures.
  • They are never unioned into one total.
  • TED notice values are not summed because a notice value may be a framework ceiling or cover multiple lots.
  • Where award values are explicitly sum-safe, that status is shown separately.

Freshness and continuity

The beta targets a weekday snapshot refresh. The build time and age are shown in the product. Once the snapshot is older than 48 hours, the interface labels it overdue and scheduled email digests are suppressed. The last-known-good copy remains visible for research so an upstream failure does not replace evidence with invented or partial records.

How to use the result

Treat PublicSignal as a discovery and triage tool. Open the source notice, inspect the complete procurement documents, confirm the deadline and ask the contracting authority where clarification is required. PublicSignal does not provide a probability of success, legal advice or a bid/no-bid decision.
