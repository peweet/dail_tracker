Eddie Fitzpatrick raised concerns regarding the recent severe weather
conditions and the significant damage being caused to the road network. He
highlighted several locations now requiring urgent repair work, including: The
Edenderry to Clonbullogue Road, Bracknagh Village and Sections along the R420 in
Gracefield, Portarlington. Cllr. Fitzpatrick also expressed disappointment at the
continued lack of recognition from the Department regarding the impact of
peat-based ground conditions on the deterioration of local roads. In addition, he
raised concerns about speed through Daingean, emphasising the issue from a public
safety perspective and noting that measures are urgently required.
Cllr. Fergus McDonnell also raised the issue of road safety in Daingean, expressing
ongoing concern for public safety. He noted that the previous Active Travel proposal
for Daingean had created significant worry locally, However, he stressed that
measures still need to be taken to address safety issues in the town. He also raised
concerns regarding the maintenance and management of trees, highlighting that
many residential estates designed by OCC require ongoing upkeep. He noted that the
taking-in-charge process for estates can take considerable time, and in the
meantime, trees are not being properly maintained. Cllr. McDonnell requested that
the matter receive in-depth review by the SPC, given its wider implications for estate
safety and management.
Cllr. Fergus McDonnell and Cllr. Noel Cribbin both queried whether Cokery Lane
could be considered under an Active Travel Scheme, noting its potential to link the
lane with the Greenway. They highlighted concerns regarding the low-lying sections
of the lane, which are prone to flooding, as well as the lack of adequate public
lighting, both of which present safety issues for pedestrians and cyclists.
Cllr. Claire Murray raised further concerns regarding road safety in Daingean,
particularly at the junction heading toward Croghan, noting that action is required to
address safety risks. She also highlighted the condition of footpaths in Monasteroris,
