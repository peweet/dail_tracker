At the commencement of the meeting a roll call was taken.  The Meetings Administrator 
advised of meetings etiquette for online meetings and that the press and some members of 
the public were also present. 
 
 
Congratulations were extended to all those who participated in the General Election.  
Special congratulations were extended to Cllr. E. Timmins on his election to Dáil Éireann.

Monday 4th November 2024.

confirm and sign minutes of ordinary meeting of Wicklow County Council held on Monday 
4th November 2024.

ITEM NO. 10

a presentation outlining the following: 
 
 Energy, progress and targets. 
 Introduction. 
 Energy in Wicklow. 
 ISO 50001 – Wicklow County Council Energy Users. 
 Leisure Centre Pathfinder 2025+. 
 2030 Energy Efficiency Targets. 
 2030 total CO2 targets. 
 2030 CO2 targets (heating and transport). 
 Project impacts on targets. 
 Energy Office – Wicklow County Council. 
 Public lighting. 
 Transport and machinery. 
 District heating opportunity? 
 Arklow decarbonisation zone stakeholder engagement. 
 Decarbonisation case study – Bray Fire Station. 
 Clermont Campus case study. 
 Community Recognition Fund. 
 Old Bray Courthouse, Boghall Road. 
 

The Elected Members thanked Mr. Keogh and Mr. Martin for their presentation.  The 
Elected Members raised queries which were responded to by Mr. Keogh and Mr. Martin. 
 
ITEM NO. 11 
 
Ms. L. Gallagher, Director of Services advised the Elected Members that due to the 
Christmas break and the closure of County Buildings, it would be necessary to re-schedule 
the next meeting from Monday 6th January 2025 to Monday 13th January 2025. 
 
It was proposed by Cllr. J. Snell, seconded by Cllr. S. Langrell and agreed by all to hold the 
next meeting on Monday 13th January 2025. 
 
 
NOTICES OF MOTION 
 
Notice of motion No. 1 
 
Notice of motion in the name of Cllr. S. Stokes (proposer), seconded by Cllr. P. O’Brien 
(17th October 2024) 
 
‘Wicklow County acknowledges the phenomenal population growth in County Wicklow.  This 
has resulted in school place shortages.  Wicklow County Council call on the Department of 
Education to provide a secondary school in Newtownmountkennedy as a matter of urgency’. 
 
 Following a short discussion, it was agreed by all to write to the Department of Education.  
It was also agreed to write to the newly elected TDs in the Wicklow and Wicklow/Wexford 
constituencies inviting them to a meeting on the matter.
