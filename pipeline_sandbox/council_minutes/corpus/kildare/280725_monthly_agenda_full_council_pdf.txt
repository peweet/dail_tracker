Tairisceana na Comhairle Contae 
Motions Kildare County Council 
 
 
10. 
Councillors Chris Pender, Nuala Killeen, Pat Balfe, Peter Melrose, Paula Mulroe, 
Claire O'Rourke, Aoife Breslin, Angela Feeney, Rupert Heather, Noel Connolly, 
Bill Clear, Ger Dunne, Pádraig McEvoy and Seamie Moore 
That Kildare County Council writes to the Minister for Housing, Local Government and 
Heritage to oppose the proposed changes to apartment standards, which reduce local 
authority discretion and risk undermining the proper planning and sustainable 
development of housing, as well as public confidence in local and national democracy, 
and seek a meeting to ensure that any future standards reflect a locally informed and 
community-led approach to housing delivery.  
 
11. 
Councillors Peggy O’Dwyer, William Durkan and Brendan Wyse 
That the council writes to the Department of Climate, Energy and the Environment for 
a policy on the disposal of large nitrous oxide canisters.  
 
12. 
Councillors Evie Sammon and Tracey O'Dwyer 
That the council outlines how the Local Government Rates and Other Matters Act 
2019 could be used to vary the annual rate across the county to support town centres.  
 
13. 
Councillors Mark Leigh and Anne Breen 
That the council adopts a county-wide strategy for improving road junction sightlines. 
 
14. 
Councillor Bernard Caldwell 
That in preparation of the budget for next year, the total spend specifically for the 
provision of speed ramps in 2025 be extrapolated from current budget and this 
amount be doubled for allocation in 2026.  
 
 
15. 
Councillor Donna Phelan 
That the council establishes a working group or committee consisting of a 
representative from each municipal district and external stakeholders with relevant
