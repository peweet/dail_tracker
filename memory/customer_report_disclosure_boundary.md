# Customer report disclosure boundary

Confirmed: 2026-10-16.

Removing a renderer's direct implementation notes does not establish a customer disclosure
boundary. Source-authored caveats can repeat acquisition, custody and processing details through
a second path.

Use explicit, validated customer copy where internal and customer explanations differ. Preserve
the original internal record. Customer copy must retain source identity, editions, citations,
dates, rights, uncertainty and applicability limits; do not remove arbitrary clauses or imply that
unassessed material was checked. Describe an automated source review honestly without exposing
its internal component name or presenting its timestamp as a publisher update.

Verify both retained and unavailable source cases, with positive evidence markers and negative
disclosure markers. Exercise the real PDF generation and download path, inspect rendered pages
and links, and refresh dependent browser fixtures. Version changed presentation; keep previously
sealed artifacts intact and test legacy readers separately.

Evidence: focused red/green renderer and publication tests, independent read-only review, and
authenticated customer-PDF propagation diagnostics. This is a general lesson, not external release
clearance or a claim that every possible disclosure has been excluded.
