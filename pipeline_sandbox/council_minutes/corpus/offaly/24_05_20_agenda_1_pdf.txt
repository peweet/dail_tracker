Notices of Motion
Cllr. E. Dooley
This Council calls on the relevant Government Departments to recognise the
existing delivery of renewable energy projects on a county by county basis

when setting Regional Renewable Electricity Targets. If existing county delivery
is not recognised, this Council will recommend an immediate review of the
County Development Plan to allow for reasonable and proportionate
development of other objectives including economic development, community
development, amenity and biodiversity development etc. which are not
subservient to the energy targets.
