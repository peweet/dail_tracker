At the commencement of the meeting a roll call was taken.  The Meetings Administrator 
advised of meetings etiquette for online meetings and that the press and some members of 
the public were also present. 
 
 
Votes of Sympathy 
 
Elected Members passed a vote of sympathy to the families of the late Mr. Patrick Fleming, 
Ms. Victoria Leonard, Mr. Bill Dickenson, Mr. Adam Slater, Mr. Cathal Mac Oireachtaigh, Mr. 
Pat O’Donnell and Ms. Lizzie Lott.  A minutes silence was observed for the deceased. 
 
Congratulations were extended to Bray Emmets Senior Hurling Team who defeated Carnew 
and have won six hurling championships in a row and also to the Garden County Football 
Club who have secured both the league and the Harding Cup.  The success of the Wicklow 
Pride on their nomination of the Gala Awards was also acknowledged.  Cllr. P. O’Brien was 
also congratulated on his appointment as Chairperson of KWETB. 
 
 
Request for Suspension of Standing Orders:- The Meetings Administrator advised that two 
requests for suspension of standing orders had been submitted in advance of the meeting.   
In accordance with the protocol adopted the request was read to the meeting as proposed 
and seconded:- 
 
1. Cllr. D. Alvey, seconded by Cllr. M. Barry requested a suspension of standing orders to 
discuss the following motion:  
 
‘That this Council agrees to write to An Taoiseach and the Minister of Heritage and Electoral 
Reform asking the State to examine purchasing 197 acres of land currently for sale in 
Kindlestown, Delgany that connects Kindlestown Wood to the Glen of the Downs Nature 
Reserve, in line with our obligations under the Nature Restoration Law; and thereby bringing 
this land into the ownership of the State as is the preference of this Council’. 
 
2. Cllr. P. Leonard, seconded by Cllr. J. Behan requested a suspension of standing orders 
to discuss the following motion: 
 
‘That this council request the Chief Executive write to the EPA to request an emergency 
environmental & human impact assessment be carried out on Ballinclare Quarry, Kilbride 
and environs due to concerns from the local community around the presence of asbestos and 

the quarry’s recent water drainage operations and the risk of contamination to nearby lands 
and water and air quality’.  
 
It was agreed by all to have a discussion on the topics at 4.30 p.m.

Suspension of Standing Orders

to discuss the following motion:  
 
‘That this Council agrees to write to An Taoiseach and the Minister of Heritage and Electoral 
Reform asking the State to examine purchasing 197 acres of land currently for sale in 
Kindlestown, Delgany that connects Kindlestown Wood to the Glen of the Downs Nature
