Draft minutes of the Meeting of  
Westmeath Local Community Development Committee, (LCDC) held 
 on Friday 2nd December 2022 at 10.00am (Hybrid Meeting).   

2 
 
In the absence of Chairman Hugh Farrell, Pat Gallagher (vice-chairman) welcomed all LCDC members, 
Westmeath County Council staff and Westmeath Community Development staff to the meeting.  Annette 
Barr Jordan acted as Chief Officer for the meeting in the absence of Pat Murtagh.  
 
Pat Gallagher reminded members of the confidentiality of the items on today’s agenda and asked that 
they declare any conflicts of interests that they may have.   
 
1. Draft Minutes of LCDC Meeting held on Friday 21st October 2022 
 
On the proposal of Cllr. Bill Collintine and seconded by Margaret Kiernan the minutes of the LCDC 
meeting held on Friday 21st October 2022 were approved.   
 
2. Matters Arising 
No items under matters arising. 
 
 
3. LEADER PROGRAMME 2023-2027 
Annette Barr Jordan discussed the upcoming LEADER Programme. Annette asked the LCDC 
members to consider putting in an Expression of Interest to become the LAG for the 2023-2027 
Programme. On the proposal of Christine Charlton and seconded by Noel Maguire, it was agreed 
to submit an EOI.  
 
4. Sláintecare Healthy Communities  
 
a. Seed Funding Proposal  
Brigid Geoghegan gave members an update on the Seed Funding Proposal. Pat Gallagher 
thanked the members of the subcommittee for all their work. The funding proposal was 
proposed by Margaret Kiernan and seconded by Noel Maguire.  
 
b. Programme Launch 
Brigid Geoghegan reminded the LCDC Members of the Programme Launch taking place on 
Friday 9th December at 2.30pm in Athlone. She invited all LCDC Members to attend.   
 
 
 
5. Healthy Ireland Update 
Pat Gallagher welcomed Sarah McManus (Healthy Ireland Coordinator) to the meeting. Pat 
acknowledged Dermot Egan’s work and support during his time as coordinator. It was agreed that 
the Healthy Ireland Subcommittee members would remain unchanged namely Margaret Kiernan, 
Maria Fox and Frank Murtagh. Teresa Kennedy, HSE, was not at the meeting to reconfirm her 
position. This will be followed up before the next meeting.   
 
Proposed by: Frank Murtagh 
Seconded by: Marie Flynn.  
 
 
 

3 
 
6. Community Support Fund 2022 
Annette Barr Jordan discussed the recent announcement of the Community Support Fund 2022. 
Closing date set by the Department is 31st March 2023.  Closing date for applications within the 
county will be 10th March 2023. Online applications and hardcopy applications will be accepted. 
Annette also informed members that Funding Workshops & Clinics will be held for community 
groups on 30th January 2023 and 1st February 2023.  Vinny McCormack asked if Residents 
Associations will be eligible for this funding? Annette confirmed that it will be open to all 
community groups. Pat Gallagher welcomed the news of the additional funding for the county.  
 
7. Correspondence 
None  
 
8. AOB  
None.  
 
9. Date of Next Meeting 
 
The next LCDC meeting is scheduled for the 16th December 2022 at 10am followed by Seasonal Lunch.  
Pat Gallagher thanked members for attending today’s meeting and wished them all a safe journey home.
