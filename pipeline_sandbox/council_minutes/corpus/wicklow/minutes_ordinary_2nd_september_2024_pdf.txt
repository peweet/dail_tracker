ITEM NO. 31 
 
To consider the preparation of Wicklow County Council Corporate Plan 2024 – 2029 in 
accordance with Section 134 of the Local Government Act 2001, as amended 
 
Ms. L. Gallagher, Director of Services advised the Elected Members that local authorities are 
obliged to prepare a corporate plan to be adopted within six months of the annual meeting 
of the new Council.   
 
The plan will serve as the Council's strategic framework over the duration of the plan, which 
is five years.  The plan has been prepared on an inclusive basis with an organisational wide 
strategic approach.  It is proposed to carry out consultation with staff and other external 
stakeholders within the Council and to consult formally with the Corporate Policy Group and 
Strategic Policy Committees now that they have been put in place.  The Local Community 
Development Committee, external stakeholders, such as the Public Participation Network, 
representatives groups, social partners at local level, the community voluntary and 
environmental interests, and other public bodies operating locally will all be consulted as 
part of the wider consultation process.  A questionnaire will also be available on the 
Council's Public Consultation Hub. 
 
The Elected Members discussed the plan and in particular, the inclusion of ‘democracy’ in 
the overarching themes.  The importance of annual review and monitoring of performance
