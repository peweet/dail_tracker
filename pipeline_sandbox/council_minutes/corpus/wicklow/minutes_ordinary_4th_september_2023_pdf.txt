ITEM NO. 7 
 
To consider the disposal of 1.82 Ha or thereabouts in the townland of Lathaleere, 
Baltinglass, Co. Wicklow comprised in Folio No. 5663 Co. Wicklow being the property 

known as Lathaleere, Baltinglass, Co. Wicklow by lease for a term not exceeding three 
years.  The Lease commencement date will be the date of the Lease Agreement but 
ending no later than 31st March 2027 to The Minister for Children, Equality, Disability, 
Integration and Youth. 
 
It was proposed by Cllr. P. O’Brien, seconded by Cllr. R. O’Connor ‘to consider the disposal of 
1.82 Ha or thereabouts in the townland of Lathaleere, Baltinglass, Co. Wicklow comprised in 
Folio No. 5663 Co. Wicklow being the property known as Lathaleere, Baltinglass, Co. 
Wicklow by lease for a term not exceeding three years.  The Lease commencement date will 
be the date of the Lease Agreement but ending no later than 31st March 2027 to The 
Minister for Children, Equality, Disability, Integration and Youth’  
 
A long discussion on the proposed disposal followed.  Some of the issues raised included: 
 
 Referral back to Baltinglass Municipal District. 
 Lack of communication. 
 Opposition of Baltinglass Municipal District Members. 
 Implications of refusal. 
 Community infrastructure. 
 Need for social housing. 
 High levels of foreign nationals in the area. 
 Lack of geographical spread. 
 Land zoning, possible sports centre. 
 Availability of other suitable sites. 
 Site selection process. 
 Use of lands/units after 3 years. 
 Possibility of extension of duration. 
 Site restoration. 
 Options in relation to the disposal including possible deferral. 
 Displacement of Ukrainian people due to war. 
 
Ms. H. Purcell, Meetings Administrator outlined the legislation. Section 183 of Local 
Government Act 2001, as amended:  ‘At a meeting of the Council to be held after the 
expiration of ten clear days from the date of the sending of this notice the Council may 
resolve as follows: 
(a) That the disposal shall be carried out in accordance with the terms specified in such 
notice or, 
(b) That the disposal shall be carried out in accordance with the terms specified in the 
resolution or, 
(c) That the disposal shall not be carried out’. 
 
The Chief Executive and Management Team responded to the issues raised and reiterated 
the need for this disposal. 
 
Following a roll call it was defeated by a margin of 9 for, 19 against and 4 not present viz:-

ITEM NO. 10 
 
To receive a presentation:  Comhairle Na nÓg 
 
Katie MacAdam, Oisin Nunez and Oscar Kavanagh, Comhairle Na nÓg, gave a presentation 
covering the following area: 
 
 What is Comhairle Na nÓg? 
 Snapshot Report – contribution to food poverty research; presenting at drugs 
conference; national youth assembly of Ireland; and national events – showcase 
2023. 
 Bringing young voices to decision maker spaces. 
 Benefits of listening to the youth voice. 
 Annual youth summit. 
 9 topics from 2022 – mental health, vaping, drugs, discrimination, public transport, 
pollution, peer pressure, bullying and cost of living. 
 Survey – Youth attitudes towards drugs. 
 
There followed a short discussion in which the Elected Members thanked the members of 
Comhairle Na nÓg for their presentation and for all the work that they do.  They stated the 
presentation challenged preconceived beliefs held by the Elected Members and was 
extremely uplifting. They wished them well in the future.
