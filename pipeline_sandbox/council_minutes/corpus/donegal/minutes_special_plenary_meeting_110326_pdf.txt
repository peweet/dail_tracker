C/66/26 
 HOUSING CAPITAL UPDATE 
 Mr John Gallagher, Senior Engineer updated members in relation to 
the following: 
 
• Updates on the Housing Capital Programme since the previous 
meeting on the 11th February 2026. 
• Overview of projects completed from 2022-2025. 
• Projects currently on site. 
• Projects at Tender stage. 
• Projects at Planning & Detailed Design Stage. 
• Competitive Design Stage. 
• Current status of overall social housing delivery pipeline. 
 
He confirmed that competitive dialogue was a mechanism aside from 
inhouse delivery that allowed Donegal County Council to deliver social 
housing schemes on lands owned by the Council and relied on 

 
4 
 
expressions of interest from interested developers with the aim of 
speeding up housing delivery. 
 
A lengthy debate ensued during which members raised a variety of 
issues.  
 
Responding Mr Gallagher confirmed that the Housing Capital team 
attended Municipal District meetings on a regular basis and could also 
facilitate workshops at that level where many of the issues highlighted 
could be addressed. A number of the more specific items referenced, 
he stated, could be discussed at SPC and at workshop level. 
 
There was, he advised, an ongoing open expression of interest for land 
being promoted on the Council’s website and that a focus was being 
maintained on all aspects of land acquisition. He confirmed that there 
was ongoing interaction with Uisce Éireann in terms of the service 
needs for social housing delivery.  
 
It was confirmed that 15 units had been completed at Windy Hall and 
that others were now on site. He said that information could be 
provided to members in relation to the listings of schemes that had 
Uisce Éireann approval and those that were awaiting same.  
 
An update on Part V approvals could, he advised, would be provided at 
the next appropriate Special Meeting.  
 
The projects at Malin, Gleneely and Barrack Hill, were expected to be 
completed in June or July of this year. Further detail would be provided, 
he added, with regard to Cluain Darach and the role of the Approved 
Housing Body in that project.  
 
With regard to the turnkey acquisition in Moville, he said, that the Uisce 
Éireann issue had been fully resolved and the contract signing was at 
an advanced stage.  
 
Members would, he said, be updated in relation to the completion dates 
for a number of the projects listed.  
 
Archaeological tests, he said, were ongoing at Ballymacool with the aim 
of completing same preconstruction, though nothing of significance had 
been encountered to date. 
 
Mr Patsy Lafferty, Director Housing, MD Services and Capital Projects  
confirmed that it would be possible to arrange a series of MD 
workshops to go through some of the finer details raised by members.  
 
On the proposal of Cllr J Brogan, seconded by Cllr Mc Gowan it was 
resolved that representatives from Uisce Éireann and the ESB be 
invited to brief members in relation to infrastructure and supply issues.

C/68/26 
DRAFT DEVELOPMENT CONTRIBUTION SCHEME 2025-2030 
Members considered the report circulated with the agenda in relation to 
the above. 
 
Mr Killian Smith, Executive Planner, provided a detailed presentation on 
the Draft Development Contribution Scheme as presented together with 
an overview of the Chief Executive’s response in relation to a number of 
the submissions. He referred also to several modifications recommended 
by the Planning Authority. 
 
Cllr C Brogan said that a number of items required clarification and that 
additional discussion was needed as members needed information on 
what income was derived from development charges and the rationale 
behind the proposed increases. 
 
An overview of the spend per service and the rationale for same was 
provided by Mr Richard Gibson, Director Finance, Corporate Services  & 
Information Systems and by Mr Eunan Quinn, Senior Planner. It was 
confirmed that the overall aim was to strike a reasonable balance whilst 
maximising the overall income potential.  
 
A lengthy debate took place during which members suggested proposed 
amendments to the Schedule of DCS Charges for Classes of 
Development. It was thus unanimously agreed to defer consideration of a 
number of specific charges to later in the meeting and to facilitate further 
consultation. 
 
Members proceeded to agree the following:- 
 
Parking  
On the proposal of Cllr Blaney seconded by Cllr Mc Monagle it was 
resolved to accept the recommendation in the Chief Executive’s report 
and to apply a charge of €3,500 per parking space. 
 
Refuse or Waste Disposal. 
On the proposal of Cllr C Brogan, seconded by Cllr A Farren  
it was resolved to it was resolved to accept the Chief Executive’s 
recommendation and levy a charge of €2,065.20 per 0.1 Ha of surface 
area. 
 
Bed & Breakfast/Guest House/Hotel Rooms(including guestrooms 
associated with public houses) 
On the proposal of Cllr Blaney, seconded by Cllr C Brogan it was resolved 
to accept the Chief Executive’s recommendation and implement a no 

 
6 
 
charge policy in respect of the first four bedrooms with €688.40 per 
bedroom appliable where there are in excess of four bedrooms.  
 
Car Wash 
On the proposal of Cllr Blaney, seconded by Cllr C Brogan it was resolved 
to accept the Chief Executive’s recommendation and apply a €1500 flat 
rate (in addition to commercial rate for petrol  forecourt area where 
applicable) 
 
Advertisements 
On the proposal of Cllr Mc Gowan, seconded by Cllr C Brogan it was 
resolved not to accept the Chief Executive’s recommendation and to apply 
a charge of €27.54 per m2 subject to a minimum charge of €55.07 
 
Golf Course/Pitch & Putt 
It was unanimously agreed to accept the Chief Executive’s 
recommendation and apply a charge of €1,115.21 per ha. (Commercial 
buildings/clubhouses to be charged at commercial rate , based upon floor 
area.  
 
Wind Anemometer Mast 
It was unanimously agreed not to accept the Chief Executive’s 
recommendation and to the leave the charge at €3442 currently in place. 
 
Agriculture Forestry Buildings (Garden Centres under Retail) 
Cllr Blaney declared an interest in this aspect. 
 
It was unanimously agreed to not accept the Chief Executive’s 
recommendation and maintain the existing charge of €3.44 per m2 subject 
to a minimum charge of €100. 
 
Use of Land for Intensive Agricultural Purposes (e.g. battery farming of 
livestock) 
On the proposal of Cllr Mc Gowan, seconded by Cllr Blaney it was 
resolved not to accept the Chief Executive’s recommendation and to apply 
the currently prevailing charge of €826.08 per Ha.  
 
Sheds, Stores and Garages Within the Curtilage of a House or Ancillary to 
Use of a Residence or Non-Commercial Hobbying 
It was unanimously agreed to accept the Chief Executive’s 
recommendation and apply a charge of €10 per m2 subject to a minimum 
charge of €100. 
 
Domestic Extensions  
It was unanimously agreed to accept the Chief Executive’s 
recommendation. and apply a charge of €5 per m2 subject to a minimum 
charge of €100.
