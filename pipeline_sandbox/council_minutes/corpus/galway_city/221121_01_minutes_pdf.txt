Councillors discussed the draft budget and thanked the Chief Executive and staff for their
contribution during the year.
Mr. McGrath, Chief Executive expressed his appreciation to Declan Smyth, Head of Finance
and Nepta Moggan, Financial Management Accountant, Laurena Mitchell, SEO and the
Finance Department team and wished a speedy recovery to Mr. D. Smyth, Head of Finance.
Mayor. C. Connolly suggested that the meeting break at 5pm for a ten minutes and that the
meeting conclude at 6.30pm. This was agreed.
Ms. R. McNally, Dos; Ms. P. Philbin, Dos; Mr. D. Mahon, SEO, Mr. B. Barrett, SEO; Ms. C.
Kilcoyne, SE and Ms. E. Naughton, Head of ICT responded to queries raised.
At 5.05pm, the meeting took a break and resumed again at 5.23pm.
Councillors discussed the current meeting arrangements which are in a hybrid format with
both online and physical attendance. Mayor C. Connolly proposed that in light of current
COVID figures that the Council meet as a body online for Council meetings. This was seconded
by Cllr. E. Hoare.
Cllr. M.J. Crowe advised that he was not in agreement and that the Council were entitled to
meet in person. He added that if Government advice changes that this can be considered at
this stage.
Mr. P. Greene, DoS and Mr. McGrath, Chief Executive continued to respond to queries raised
by Councillors.
3
Cllr. M. Cubbard queried if Local Improvement Scheme allocations could be spent in the Parks
area as well as Recreation. This was confirmed and agreed by Council.
Cllr. O. Hanley proposed an amendment to the motion submitted by Cllr. F. Fahy.
Mr. B. McGrath advised that the City Council provided for family grave plots only.
In line with Standing Orders the amendment is taken first. Ms. R. McNally proceeded to take
a Roll Call Vote as follows:
Proposed by: Cllr. O. Hanley Seconded by: Cllr. M.J. Crowe
That Galway City Council approve the budget as proposed by Cllr. F. Fahy but amend the
ICT cut to pay the ICT €60,000 from reserves and cut the Tourism fund by €55,000 and
Introduce a 100% waiver for standard baby grave openings.
In favour: Cllr. l. Byrne; Cllr. A. Cheevers; Cllr. J. Connolly; Cllr. M.J. Crowe; Cllr. O. Hanley
& Cllr. P. Keane.
Against: Cllr. C. Connolly; Cllr. M. Cubbard; Cllr. F. Fahy; Cllr. C. Higgins; Cllr. E. Hoare;
Cllr. N. Larkin; Cllr. D. Lyons; Cllr. D. McDonnell; Cllr. N. McNelis; Cllr. N. Murphy;
Cllr. M. O'Connor & Cllr. T. O'Flaherty
In Favour: 6 Against: 12 Abstain: O Present: 18
The amendment was defeated.
Ms. R. McNally proceeded to take a Roll Call Vote on the original Budget proposal motion
submitted as follows:
Proposed by: Cllr. F. Fahy Seconded by: Cllr. D. Lyons
That Galway City Council approve the Draft Budget 2022 subject to the amendments
outlined by Cllr. F. Fahy.
In favour: Cllr. l. Byrne; Cllr. A. Cheevers; Cllr. C. Connolly; Cllr. J. Connolly;
Cllr. M. Cubbard; Cllr. F. Fahy; Cllr. O. Hanley; Cllr. C. Higgins; Cllr. E. Hoare; Cllr. P. Keane.
Cllr. N. Larkin; Cllr. D. Lyons; Cllr. D. McDonnell; Cllr. N. McNelis; Cllr. N. Murphy;
Cllr. M. O'Connor & Cllr. T. O'Flaherty
Against: Cllr. M.J. Crowe
In Favour: 17 Against: 1 Abstain: O
The Proposal was passed and approved.

2. Determination in accordance with the Annual Budget adopted of the Annual Rate
on Valuation to be levied for the several purposes specified in the Annual Budget
for the financial year ending December 2022.
Proposed by: Cllr. D. McDonnell Seconded by: Cllr. D. Lyons
That the Annual Rate on valuation to be levied for the several purposes specified in the
Annual Budget for the financial year ending 315t December 2022 be €67.40.
The Annual Rate on Valuation was adopted.
3. The decision to determine the proportion of refund to apply in respect of Rates on
vacant properties in each local electoral area in Galway City.
Proposed by: Cllr. D. Lyons Seconded by: Cllr. D. McDonnell
That a refund of 36.5% apply in respect of Commercial Rates on vacant properties in each
Local electoral area in Galway City
The rates of 36.5% refund on vacant properties was adopted.
A Roll Call Vote was taken to amend the Schedule to the 2009 Parking Byelaws on the proposed
increase in off-street parking from €5 to €6.50 as follows:
Proposed by: Cllr. D. Lyons Seconded by: Cllr. M. O'Connor
That the Schedule to the Parking Byelaws provide for an increase in off-street parking
from €5 to €6.50 at the Dyke Road; Cathedral and College Road carparks.
In favour: Cllr. C. Connolly; Cllr. M. Cubbard; Cllr. F. Fahy; Cllr. C. Higgins; Cllr. E. Hoare;
Cllr. N. Larkin; Cllr. D. Lyons; Cllr. N. McNelis; Cllr. N. Murphy; Cllr. M. O'Connor & Cllr. T.
O'Flaherty
Against: Cllr. l. Byrne; Cllr. A. Cheevers; Cllr. J. Connolly; Cllr. M.J. Crowe; Cllr. O. Hanley;
Cllr. P. Keane & D. McDonnell.
In Favour: 11 Against: 7 Abstain: O Present: 18
The increase on off-street parking from €5 to €6.50 on the Schedule of the Carparking
Byelaws was adopted.
Proposed by: Cllr. C. Connolly Seconded by: Cllr. O. Hanley
That Galway City Council calls on the Government to introduce legislation to impose levies
on homes that are purchased as investment properties.
It was agreed to leave this proposal on the agenda
The meeting ended at 18:28 pm
