## Priority Issues

### P0: Do not describe operational guarantees as PDF capabilities

**Why it matters:** A downloaded PDF cannot revoke access, prove who opened it, enforce immutability, or keep a queue current. Claiming otherwise creates false assurance.

**Fix:** Print only the archive, delivery, and audit identifiers plus a dated status. Point to the authoritative external record.

**Suggested command:** `impeccable harden`

### P0: Keep the land-control matrix evidential, never determinative

**Why it matters:** A table headed `ownership verified` could be mistaken for a title opinion or sufficient legal-interest conclusion.

**Fix:** Name the evidence received, its scope, source, review state, and unresolved professional action. Do not infer owners or legal control from parcels or proximity.

**Suggested command:** `impeccable clarify`

### P1: Separate exact-site history from nearby context

**Why it matters:** The Ballyboggan pack demonstrates that point proximity can become an unsupported `same parcel` assertion.

**Fix:** Use explicit states: exact boundary match, partial overlap, adjoining or shared infrastructure, nearby only, and unresolved. Require a matching basis and source for every non-nearby claim.

**Suggested command:** `impeccable clarify`

### P1: Do not add all operational data to the main report

**Why it matters:** Queue state, correction logs, and economics would bury the planning evidence and expose internal commercial data to clients.

**Fix:** Keep the client report focused on planning evidence. Generate a separate internal assurance snapshot for workflow and pilot metrics.

**Suggested command:** `impeccable distill`

### P2: Make provenance and staleness visible on every page

**Why it matters:** Superseded PDFs will continue to circulate after a new report is issued.

**Fix:** Repeat report ID, review state, confidentiality status, and supersession state in the footer. Put the full identity block on the cover and in the provenance appendix.

**Suggested command:** `impeccable harden`

## Persona Red Flags

**Planning consultant:** Nearby applications can read as comparable precedent unless relationship and non-equivalence are explicit.

**Solicitor or land agent:** A land-control matrix can be over-read as title verification unless every status is framed as evidence received and reviewed.

**Development director:** Economic metrics inside the planning report can resemble an investment recommendation or hidden mandate score.

**QA reviewer:** A printed confirmation queue becomes stale immediately without a snapshot time and a pointer to the authoritative workflow.

**Client recipient:** `Named-user access` wording may imply protection that disappears when the file is downloaded or forwarded.
