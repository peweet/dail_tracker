ITEM NO. 10 
 
To receive a presentation: Irish Farmers Association (IFA) 
 
Mr. T. Byrne, Irish Farmers Association (IFA) gave a presentation outlining the following: 
 
 Farmers across mainland Europe have been voicing their anger and frustration at the 
ever increasing administrative burden being placed on farmers, which have had a 
major impact on farm family incomes.   
 
 The EU, the Irish Government, and local government, are devising policies far from 
the farm gate, with little or no consideration of direct impact on farmers. 
 
 Transformation of CAP away from supporting food production towards actually 
reducing farm output.   
 
 The failure of the EU to properly compensate farmers under CAP.   

 
 The proposed Mercosur trade deal being pushed by the European Commission has 
the capacity to devastate and, in particular, the Irish    in particular, the Irish beef 
sector.   
 
 Overcomplicated schemes, which have been poorly implemented by the Irish 
Department of Agriculture.   
 
 There has also been a complete systems failure on farm investment grant aid, 
otherwise known as TAMs scheme.   
 
 The reduction of nitrates derogation from 250kgs of organic N to 220 organic N 
imposed by the European Commission, with absolutely no flexibility offered.   
 
 The nature restoration law, which places further restrictions on farmers, particularly 
on designated lands with no compensation.   
 
 The lack of flexibility being shown to tillage farmers within the new regulations.   
 
 It is imperative that any measurement of emissions and removals is based on 
research that reflects Irish conditions.   
 
 Delays in planning process, which is hindering on farm investment.   
 
 County Councils will be doing an increasing number of inspections on farm.  . 
 
 The lack of dog wardens in many counties to deal with the dog attacks on sheep 
flocks.   
 
Following the presentation the Elected Members expressed views and raised queries which 
were responded to by Mr. Byrne 
The following motion was put forward:- 
‘Wicklow County Council resolves to write to the President of the EU Commission, the Taoiseach and 
the Minister for Agriculture advising them that the Council has passed the following resolution. 
Wicklow County Council supports the IFA ‘enough is enough’ campaign.  
The Council recognises the significant contribution that farming, and the Agri-sector makes to the 
Irish and local economy. The Council acknowledges the income challenge on Irish farmers arising 
from the significant increases in the cost of doing business, regulatory costs and cuts in Basic Income 
support for farmers. 
  
The Council calls on the Government to introduce no further regulations on farmers or any measures 
that may increase costs on farms without full negotiation and agreement with the Irish Farmers 
Association (IFA). The Council calls on the European Union and the Irish Government to provide 

additional stand-alone funding, separate to the Common Agricultural Policy (CAP), to support 
farmers to undertake climate and biodiversity measure”  
  
The motion was proposed by Cllr. S. Cullen, seconded by Cllr. S. Bourke and agreed by all.
