At the commencement of the meeting a roll call was taken. The Meetings Administrator
advised all present they are bound by the Code of Conduct for Councillors and Employees.
Also, of meetings etiquette for online meetings and that the press and some members of the
public were also present.
Votes of Sympathy.
Elected Members passed a vote of sympathy to the families of the late Mr. Bryan Doyle, Ms.
Marian Canavan, Ms. May Nolan and Mr. Jim Fitzmaurice. A minute's silence was observed
for the deceased.

ITEM NO. 1
Election of the Cathaoirleach. (Section 36 of the Local Government Act 2001, as amended)
Prior to calling for nominations for the position of Cathaoirleach, Cllr. M. Corrigan, outgoing
Cathaoirleach, said that representing Wicklow County Council had been a fantastic
experience and that she enjoyed the privilege of travelling across every corner of County
Wicklow meeting communities, organisations, volunteers and residents. She thanked the
Elected Members and the staff of Wicklow County Council for their support and kindness
throughout the year.
The Elected Members and the Chief Executive paid tribute to Cllr. Corrigan on the
exceptional manner in which she carried out her duties as Cathaoirleach. It was
acknowledged that meetings were carried out in a fair manner and that she was always fair,
open and approachable.
Cllr. M. Corrigan called for nominations for the position of Cathaoirleach of Wicklow County
Council for the forthcoming year.
1. Cllr. C. Winstanley was proposed by Cllr. M. Barry and seconded by Cllr. D. Alvey
2. Cllr. J. Behan was proposed by Cllr. P. Leonard and seconded by Cllr. M. Duddy.
Both Cllr. Winstanley and Cllr. Behan accepted the nomination.
Following a roll call Cllr. C. Winstanley was elected as Cathaoirleach of Wicklow County
Council by a margin of 20 votes for Cllr. C. Winstanley, 11 votes for Cllr. J. Behan and 1 not

Before calling for nominations for Leas-Cathaoirleach, the newly elected Cathaoirleach
addressed the Council.
She thanked the Elected Members in electing her to the position of Cathaoirleach of Wicklow
County Council and paid tribute to Cllr. M. Corrigan for her exceptional service to the county
during her role as Cathaoirleach over the past year.

ITEM NO. 2
Election of Leas-Cathaoirleach
The Cathaoirleach called for nominations for the position of Leas-Cathaoirleach for the
forthcoming year.
1. Cllr. A. Cronin was proposed by Cllr. S. Langrell and seconded by Cllr. P. O'Brien.
Cllr. Cronin accepted the nomination.
As there were no other nominations Cllr. A. Cronin was elected as Leas- Cathaoirleach of
Wicklow County Council.
ITEM NO. 3
To agree a schedule of Ordinary/Special meetings for the coming year
It was proposed by Cllr. J. Snell, seconded by Cllr. S. Langrell and agreed by all to the
schedule of meetings for the coming year as circulated.
