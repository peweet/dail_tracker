1.0 To consider the proposed material alterations and the report of the Chief Executive on the 
Draft Maynooth & Environs Joint Local Area Plan 2025 – 2031 
 
An Cathaoirleach, Councillor Sharon Tolan thanked the Elected Members for their attendance. 
Mr Alan Russell, A/Senior Planner, provided a presentation and overview of the LAP process and 
proposed material alterations and the report of the Chief Executive on the Draft Maynooth & 
Environs Joint Local Area Plan 2025 – 2031.  Mr Russell also informed the members that at a meeting 
of the Clane Maynooth Municipal District on 17th February 2025, their members made the Joint LAP, 
and in doing so, did not accept the Chief Executive’s recommendation in relation to a number of 
material alterations relating to permeability measures and a zoning objective that related solely to 
the administrative area of Clane Maynooth Municipal District. 
 
Mr Russell then read the following resolution: 
 
“Having considered the Proposed Material Alterations to the Draft Maynooth and Environs Joint Local 
Area Plan 2025 – 2031 and the Chief Executive’s Report on submissions and observations dated 10th 
January 2025 in relation to the Draft Maynooth and Environs Joint Local Area Plan 2025 – 2031 and 

 
 
        
Miontuairiscí / Meeting Minutes 
 
pursuant to Section 20 of the Planning & Development Acts 2000 (as amended) the Elected Members 
of Meath County Council resolved, at a meeting on 19th February 2025, that the Maynooth and 
Environs Joint Local Area Plan 2025 – 2031 be made” 
 
 
There were interventions of support from Councillors Brian Fitzgerald, Caroline O’Reilly and Maria 
Murphy. The potential for the future development of the County was noted.  The importance of the 
provision of critical infrastructure was also noted including lands for economic development and 
housing, as well as the inclusion of a road to facilitate cycling and walking initiatives into Maynooth.  
Councillor Caroline O’Reilly noted the importance of providing critical infrastructure such as housing 
and lands for economic development, however, she expressed concern on the impact of the zoning 
on the rural community and the lack of schools and social infrastructure to cater for the increased 
demand. 
 
Councillor Brian Fitzgerald proposed to make the material alterations of the Maynooth & Environs 
Joint Local Area Plan 2025- 2031. The proposal was seconded by Councillor Nick Killian.
