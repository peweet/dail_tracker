NOTICES OF MOTION 
FOGRAÍ RÚN    
 
15. CLLR BRENDAN MCCARTHY 
 

“This Council calls on the GAA to ensure that all inter-county senior football and hurling games 
from 2027 onwards are available for all to view on free-to-air television and, where required, 
through accessible online streaming platforms. Everyone should have the opportunity to watch their 
county team compete in the inter-county Championship.”  
           [18-06-2026] 
16. CLLR AILEEN BROWNE 
“That Cork County Council develops and implements a County Cork Ryder Cup 2027 
Opportunities Strategy, in partnership with Fáilte Ireland, Tourism Ireland, local business 
organisations, community and sporting groups to maximise the economic, tourism and cultural 
benefits arising from the hosting of the Ryder Cup in Adare, Co. Limerick, in September 2027.”  
           [18-06-2026] 
17. CLLR PAT HAYES 
“That Cork County Council would investigate the feasibility of purchasing all or some of the Old 
Sugar site in Mallow.” 
           [19-06-2026] 
18. CLLR DOMINIC FINN 
“That Cork County Council reviews its current policies relating to tenant alterations to Council-
owned social housing and develops a framework to permit tenants (subject to appropriate 
technical, safety, insurance and regulatory requirements) to install solar panels on their homes at 
their own expense, and that Cork County Council develops clear guidelines outlining the 
application process, installation standards, maintenance responsibilities, insurance requirements, 
and arrangements in the event of a change in tenancy.” 
           [23-06-2026] 
19. CLLR WILLIAM O’LEARY 
“That Cork County Council calls on the Minister for Finance and the Minister for Housing to 
amend the Help to Buy Scheme to ensure the full amount available under the scheme is accessible 
to young Irish people and couples returning home from countries such as Australia, Canada and 
the United States. The current eligibility criteria, which are linked to recent Irish tax 
contributions, can leave many returning first-time buyers with a reduced grant or no grant at all 
simply because they have been living and working abroad but now wish to return home” 
     [25-06-2026] 
20. CLLR DEIRDRE KELLY 
“That this Council would write to the Minister for Education and Youth, Hildegarde Naughton to 
provide a viable opportunity for the 626 children that are currently being educated in 
Gaelscoileanna in Clonakilty, Bandon, Skibbereen and Cape Clear to continue their education in 
the Irish-medium in Secondary education. As it stands currently, students have to travel distances 
that are not realistic, to Gaelcholáistí in Ballyvourney, Ballincollig or Carrigaline.” 
     [25-06-2026] 
21. CLLR GER CURLEY 
“That we write to Housing minister James Browne looking for update on social housing threshold 
as many young families are gone over the threshold and are in limbo with no possibility of getting 
a home.” 
    [02-07-2026] 

22. CLLR PATRICK DONOVAN 
“To request that Cork County Council would write to the CEO of Uisce Eireann highlighting its 
concerns around the high number of water mains breaks in Cork County over the last number of 
weeks. I am asking that we outline our concern over the number of breaks and request that Uisce 
Eireann bring forward a plan to address these breaks as a matter of urgency, given some breaks 
are reoccurring multiple times at one location.” 
    [03-07-2026]
