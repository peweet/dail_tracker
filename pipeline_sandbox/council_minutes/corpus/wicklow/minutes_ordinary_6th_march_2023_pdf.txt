Votes of Sympathy 
 
Elected Members passed a vote of sympathy to the families of the late Mr. Myles Kavanagh, 
Ms. Eileen Ivory and Ms. Joan O’Reilly. A minutes silence was observed for the deceased. 
 
The Leas Cathaoirleach welcomed Ms. Emer O’Gorman, recently appointed Chief Executive 
of Wicklow County Council to County Wicklow wishing her every success in her tenure as 
Chief Executive. 
 
The Elected Members joined together in collectively welcoming Ms. O’Gorman to Wicklow 
County Council and that they looked forward to working on the priorities for County 
Wicklow over the coming years.   
 
The Chief Executive thanked the Elected Members for the warm welcome. She said that she 
looked forward to working with the Elected Members to achieve what is best for Wicklow to 
drive the county forward and to continue the great work that Mr. Brian Gleeson has led 
over the past year.

ITEM NO 8.  
 
To receive a presentation from Wicklow Cheviot Sheep Owners Association 
 
Mr. P. Behan and Mr. P. Dunne, Wicklow Cheviot Sheep Owners Association gave a 
presentation outlining the following: 
 
 
o 155,209 breeding ewes in Wicklow about to lamb this Spring. 
o 233,000 lambs born in Wicklow every year. 
o The fifth largest country in Ireland in terms of sheep numbers. 
o Savage attacks are a huge animal welfare issue.  
o Want Minister for Agriculture to deliver what farm organisations have asked for –  
o Would like this council to write to relevant ministers – Minister Charlie McConalogue 
and Minister Heather Humphreys to ask for: 
o A national database for all dogs in the country for microchipping and licensing. 
o Extra dog wardens working flexible hours – to ensure there are dog wardens working 
weekends. 
o Dog wardens need to be out seen enforcing the law. 
 
A broad discussion followed in which the Elected Members raised queries to which Mr. 
Dunne responded. There were four proposals put forward by the Elected Members. 
 
It was proposed by Cllr. P. Kennedy, seconded by Cllr. R. O’Connor and agreed by all to write 
to Minister for Agriculture and to the Minister for Rural & Community Development to ask 
for: 
o A national database for all dogs in the country for microchipping and licensing. 
o Extra dog wardens working flexible hours – to ensure there are dog wardens working 
weekends. 
It was proposed by Cllr. S. Cullen, seconded by Cllr. M. Corrigan and agreed by all to request 
the Minister for Rural & Community Development to review the wording ‘under effectual 
control’ in the Control of Dogs Act 1986 Section 9 (1). 
 
It was proposed by Cllr. J. Mullen, seconded by Cllr. P. Kennedy and agreed by all to write to 
Coillte and that a copy of the  letter to be send to all other councils, suggesting that Coillte 
add on and develop their open access policy to include that dogs should be on leashes when  
out and about in the countryside. 
 
It was proposed by Cllr. S. Bourke, seconded by Cllr. I. Winters to establish a Working Group 
within the Council to review the existing bye-laws if any, or to establish new bye-laws.
