CONSIDERATION OF CHIEF EXECUTIVE’S REPORT ON VARIATION NO. 1 
TO THE LAOIS COUNTY DEVELOPMENT PLAN 2021-2027 
 
Ms. Angela McEvoy, Director of Services referred to the Chief Executive’s Report on Variation 
No. 1 to the Laois County Development Plan 2021-2027 which had been circulated to them on 
the 21st of April 2026.   
 
Ms. McEvoy also advised the Members of their obligations in relation to the Code of Conduct 
and referred to correspondence which had issued to them with their meeting pack in this 
regard.    Any Member who has any interest in any item before the meeting must disclose the 
nature of that interest and withdraw fully for the meeting for as long as the matter is being 
discussed or considered. 
 
This Variation has been prepared to align the Plan with Ministerial Guidelines published in 
accordance with Section 28 of the Planning and Development Act 2000 (as amended). The 
relevant guidelines are: 
1. NPF Implementation: Housing Growth Requirements Guidelines for Planning 
Authorities (2025), which were published to give effect to the National Planning 
Framework First Review (2025), and 
2. Sustainable Residential Development and Compact Settlements Guidelines for 
Planning Authorities (2024). 
The proposed variation is also responding to Pillar 1: Activating Supply as set out in the 
Government Action Plan on Housing Supply and Targeting Homelessness – Delivering Homes, 
Building Communities, 2025-2030 (November 2025). 
The zoning of sufficient land is seen as a key enabler in the delivery of housing. 

 
  
The proposed variation to the Plan is intended to address specific, immediate policy updates 
that are needed in the short term and proposes that the following maps of the Portarlington 
Joint Local Area Plan (2025-2031) are included in the Laois County Development Plan (2021-
2027): 
• 
Map 1 - Land Use Zoning 
• 
Map 2 - Objectives 
• 
Map 3 – Land Use Zoning, SAC and Flood Zoning 
• 
Map 4 – Green Infrastructure Adopted 
• 
Map 5 – Schematic Layout of Transport Elements 
and then include: 
• 
Mapping changes setting out: 
• 
Proposed rezonings and the addition of residential zoned lands in response to 
the Ministerial Guidelines. 
The Proposed Variation is accompanied by:  
• 
A Residential Audit 
• 
A Site Assessment Report 
• 
A Strategic Flood Risk Assessment of the proposed zoning amendments 
• 
An Appropriate Assessment (AA) Screening Report and associated AA Screening 
Determination, pursuant to the EU Habitats Directive (92/43/EEC) and Planning and 
Development Act 2024 (as amended), 
• 
A Strategic Environmental Assessment (SEA) Screening Report and associated SEA 
Screening Determination pursuant to the European SEA Directive (2001/42/EC) 
European Union (Land Use Planning – SEA) Regulations 2025 
 
The Proposed Variation No.1 was on public display from 25th February 2026 to 27th March 
2026 (inclusive of both dates) Submissions received were formulated into a CE report which 
was circulated to the members on the 21st April 2026.

Mr. Nathan Smith, Senior Executive Planner advised as follows:- 
AA AND SEA 
The Variation to be made has been screened for the need to undertake SEA and Stage 2 AA and 
it has been found that these assessments are not required. All related documents have been 
made available to Members. We are making final Screening for SEA and AA determinations 
that these assessments are not required now, in advance of the Variation being made. The 
written resolution to make the Variation will refer to the SEA and AA Screening documents. 
 
Conclusion 
In making and adopting Variation No.1 of the Laois County Development Plan 2021-2027  the 
Elected Council, acting in the interests of the common good and the proper planning and 
sustainable development of the area, must, in accordance with the “Code of Conduct for 
Councilors” prepared under the Local Government Act 2001,as amended carry out their duties 
in this regard in a transparent manner, must follow due process and must make their decisions 
based on relevant considerations, while ignoring that which is irrelevant within the 
requirements of the statutory framework. 
In accordance with the relevant provisions of Part 3 (plan preparation) and Part 6 
(Environmental Assessments) of the Planning and Development Act 2024, members are 
advised that when performing their functions under this subsection, members of the planning 
authority shall be restricted to considering the proper planning and sustainable development 
of the area, the statutory obligations of any local authority in the area and any relevant policies 
or objectives for the time being of the Government or of any Minister of the Government. 
“In accordance with the relevant provisions of Part 3 of the Planning and Development Act 
2024 relating to the preparation, reporting and adoption of plans, the ‘statutory obligations’ 
includes, in relation to a local authority, the obligation to ensure that the local area plan is 
consistent with: 
• 
The objectives of the development plan, 
• 
Regional Spatial Economic Strategy 2019 - 2031; and 
• 
Specific Planning Policy Requirements specified in guidelines issued by the Minister 
under Section 28 of the Act. 
Equally, Local Authority employees should perform their duties objectively, should have no 
vested interest in the contents of the matter and should be in accordance with the Council’s 
Code of Conduct for Employees. 
Members are reminded of the requirement of Part 15 of the Local Government Act 2001, as 
amended in relation to Disclosure of Interest. Any member who has a pecuniary or other 
beneficial interest on any item in the subject matter before the members must disclosure the 

 
nature of that interest and then withdraw fully from the meeting for as long as the matter is 
being discussed or considered. 
Similarly, any member who has a connected person who has a pecuniary or other beneficial 
interest on any item in the subject matter before the members must disclose the nature of that 
interest and then withdraw fully from the meeting for as long as the matter is being discussed 
or considered. A connected person means a brother, sister, parent or spouse of the person or a 
child of the person or of the spouse. 
Members are also reminded that they are Designated Public Officials and should at all times 
act in accordance with the requirements of the Regulation of Lobbying Act 2015.

NOTICE OF MOTIONS  
Notice of Motion No: 29/2026 
 
Councillor John King  proposed the following Notice of Motion:- 
 
“That Laois County Council advance plans for the design of a new library for Rathdowney 
town on the newly acquired property in the Square which was previously Council Offices 
and also a Garda Station” 
 
This Notice of Motion was seconded by Councillor Conor Bergin.  The Members were advised 
that Laois County Council recognises the strategic importance of enhancing library services in 
Rathdowney town.  The Council is supportive, in principle, of progressing plans for the design 
of a new library facility on the recently acquired property at The Square.  The development of 

 
a modern, accessible library in this central location would represent a valuable cultural and 
community asset for Rathdowney and the surrounding area. 
Subject to the availability of funding and alignment with the Council’s capital programme 
priorities, it is proposed that preliminary feasibility and design considerations be advanced in 
order to establish the scope, requirements, and potential delivery options for the project. An 
application will be made for EUJTF to support this.  This will also assist in identifying suitable 
funding opportunities, including potential national library capital funding streams and URDF 
funding. Advancing this project will be contingent on securing the necessary financial 
resources and approval through the Council’s budgetary process.
