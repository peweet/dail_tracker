99. 
PRESENTATION TO OUTGOING CATHAOIRLEACH  
 
Mr. Michael Rainey, Chief Executive made a presentation to outgoing Cathaoirleach, Mr. Barry 
Walsh and commended him on a busy year of events.  Councillor Barry Walsh was presented with 
a bound book of minutes for his term of office, a photograph album of various events and a listing 
of all events attended by him.    Mr. Michael Rainey, Chief Executive on behalf of the Management 
and Staff of Laois County Council thanked Councillor Walsh on an exceptional year and his 
success in representing Laois County Council. 
 
The Members complimented and extended their thanks to Councillor Walsh. 
 
 
100. 
ELECTION OF CATHAOIRLEACH  
 
On the proposition of Councillor John Joe Fennelly seconded by Councillor Elva Kelly, the 
Members agreed that Councillor Catherine Fitzgerald be elected the Cathaoirleach of Laois 
County Council for the coming year. 
 
Councillor Catherine Fitzgerald thanked her proposer and seconder and took her seat as 
Cathaoirleach of Laois County Council. 
 
101. 
ELECTON OF LEAS CATHAOIRLEACH  
 
On the proposition of Councillor John King seconded by Councillor Barry Walsh, the Members 
agreed that Councillor Paddy Buggy be elected the Leas Cathaoirleach of Laois County Council 
for the coming year. 
 
Councillor Paddy Buggy thanked his proposer and seconder and extended thanks to all for their 
support. 
 

 
102. 
SCHEDULE OF MEETINGS  
 
It was agreed that the schedule of meetings from July 2026 to June 2027 be circulated to the 
Members. 
 
 
 
This concluded the business of the meeting.
