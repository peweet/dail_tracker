At the commencement of the meeting a roll call was taken.  The Meetings Administrator 
advised of meetings etiquette for online meetings and that the press and some members of 
the public were also present. 
 
 
ITEM NO. 1 
 
Election of the Cathaoirleach (Section 36 of the Local Government Act 2001, as amended) 
 
Prior to calling for nominations for the position of Cathaoirleach, Cllr. P. O’Brien, outgoing 
Cathaoirleach, said that representing Wicklow County Council has been an honour and 
privilege of a lifetime.  He thanked the Elected Members for their support and hard work 
throughout the year. 
 

2 
 
 
The Elected Members and the Deputy Chief Executive paid tribute to Cllr. O’Brien on the 
exceptional manner in which he carried out his duties as Cathaoirleach.  It was 
acknowledged that meetings were carried out in a fair manner and that he was always fair, 
open and approachable.   
 
Cllr. P. O’Brien called for nominations for the position of Cathaoirleach of Wicklow County 
Council for the forthcoming year.  
 
1. Cllr. M. Corrigan was proposed by Cllr. S. Bourke and seconded by Cllr. L. Scott.   
2. Cllr. J. Snell was proposed by Cllr. G. O’Neill and seconded by Cllr. J. Behan. 
 
Following a roll call Cllr. M. Corrigan was elected as Cathaoirleach of Wicklow County 
Council by a margin of 22 votes for Cllr. M. Corrigan and 10 votes for Cllr. J. Snell viz:-

Before calling for nominations for Leas-Cathaoirleach, the newly elected Cathaoirleach 
addressed the Council. 
 
She thanked the Elected Members in electing her to the position of Cathaoirleach of 
Wicklow County Council and paid tribute to Cllr. P. O’Brien for his exceptional service to the 
county during his role as Cathaoirleach over the past year. 
 
 
ITEM NO. 2 
 
Election of Leas-Cathaoirleach 
 
The Cathaoirleach called for nominations for the position of Leas-Cathaoirleach for the 
forthcoming year.   
1. Cllr. C. Winstanley was proposed by Cllr. D. Alvey and seconded by Cllr. P. O’Brien.   
2. Cllr. J. Snell was proposed by Cllr. G. O’Neill and seconded by Cllr. I. Neary. 
 
Following a roll call Cllr. C. Winstanley was elected as Leas-Cathaoirleach of Wicklow County
