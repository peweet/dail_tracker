Sir,
I have to acquaint you that the Department have had under
consideration the representations, respecting the administration
of the funds available for Technical Instruction in the Rural
Districts of County Louth, which were placed before them by
the deputation which waited on them on the 7th instant. Two
proposals for the administration of these funds were put for-
ward by the deputation, but the Department regret that they
are unable to give their assent to either of these suggestions.
The first proposal was that the funds should be divided into
two portions, and handed over to the Technical Instructi011
Committees for the Borough of Drogheda and the Urban
District of Dundalk, to be devoted by them to the purposes
of Technical Instrmetion in the Rural Districts in the South
and North of the County respectively. The Department have
been advised, however, that it is not competent for one Local
Authority to hand over moneys raised by rates for expenditure
by another in the manner suggested. In this connection I 'have
to direct attention to the Department's circular letter, So. 36'
addressed to the County Louth County Council in April, 1905•
The second proposal was that the funds should be adminis-
tered by the present County Committee of Agriculture, who
would arrange with the two Urban Technical InstructiOU
Committees for the utilisation of the services of their Instrt1C-
tors in the Rural Districts. This proposal would involve an
6
in what manner the funds available for the Southern portion
of the County are to be expended, and to make arrangements
with the Technical Instruction Committee for the Borough Of
for the services of their Instructors, and the use Of
equipment, for Classes to be conducted in these Rural areas'
in cases where such arrangements are desirable. The decisions
of this Committee, subject to the general conditions governing
the scheme as a whole, and to their not involving an expendi-
ture in excess of the amount available, to be confirmed by the
County Joint Committee.. In case of difficulty the question to
be submitted to the Department, whose decision shall be final-
The Committee to meet at Drogheda or some other convenient
centre in the Southern portion of the County, and the Principal
Executive Offcer of the County Joint Committee to act as
Secretary and Convener.
The Department hope that the County Council will be able
to see their way to concur in this suggestion, Which would
seem to secure all that is desired, namely, that the Rural
Districts of Ardee and Louth should secure the full benefit Of
the funds available, and that these funds shall be expended
as economically as possible.
The Department further hope that this matter may receive
the consideration of the County Council at an early date.
I am, Sir,
Your obedient servant,
