Notice of Motions 
 
 
Notice of motion in the name of Cllr. P. Fitzgerald (proposer), seconded by Cllr. P. Kennedy 
(23rd May 2023) 
 
'Wicklow County Council calls on the Office of Public Works to carry out the necessary excavation 
works to recover the remains of Joe Brady, Daniel Curley, Michael Fagan, Thomas Caffrey and Tim 
Kelly, in what is commonly known as the Invincibles Yard at Kilmainham Gaol. The bodies of these 
five members of the Irish National Invincibles lie beneath the paving slabs of the yard where they 
were executed in 1883 for their part in the Phoenix Park assassinations. The families of the five men 
are represented by the National Graves Association and the wish of the families is for their relatives 
to be exhumed from Kilmainham Gaol and reinterred in consecrated ground at Glasnevin Cemetery.’  
 
It was proposed by Cllr. E. Timmins, seconded by Cllr. A. Flynn-Kennedy and agreed by all 
pass the motion. 
 
Notice of motion in the name of Cllr. L. Scott (proposer), seconded by Cllr. P. Leonard and 
supported by Cllr. M. Kavanagh (24th May 2023) 
 
‘That Wicklow County Council produces a county wide strategy for the development of 
community gardens and allotments’. 
 
Response: 
 

It is acknowledged that Community Gardens/Allotments can create social ties, bring about 
positive health effects and increase community involvement and help reduce negative 
environmental impacts by promoting sustainable agriculture.  All can benefit from urban 
agriculture since it creates habitats and improves the ecology of the area. 
Wicklow County Council can certainly support such community initiatives through the 
LEADER programme. 
  
There are already good Community developed allotments in Wicklow including Fassaroe at 
Little Bray Community Centre, run by the Community Centre.  
 
There is currently no provision in the Budget in any Directorate or the necessary staff 
resources in place to develop a county strategy for community gardens and allotments. 
 
Following a discussion the Elected Members requested that the Municipal Districts source 
suitable land and report back by September. Following receipt of reports from the Municipal 
Districts, expressions of interests countywide from community groups and interested parties 
may be sought.
