At the commencement of the meeting a roll call was taken.  The Meetings Administrator 
advised of meetings etiquette for online meetings and that the press and some members of 
the public were also present. 
 
 
Votes of Sympathy 
 
Elected Members passed a vote of sympathy to the families of the late Ms. Pauline Doyle, 
Ms. Margaret Timmons, Ms. Ann Fortune, Mr. William Redmond, Ms. Gabrielle Robinson, 
Mr. Sean Crammond, Ms. Teresa White and Ms. Pat McHugh. A minutes silence was 
observed for the deceased. 
 
 
Request for Suspension of Standing Orders:- The Meetings Administrator advised that a 
request for suspension of standing orders had been submitted in advance of the meeting.   
In accordance with the protocol adopted the request was read to the meeting as proposed 
and seconded:- 
 
 
Cllr. M. Kavanagh, seconded by Cllr. P. Leonard and supported by Cllr. T. Fortune and Cllr. J. 
Behan, requested a suspension of standing orders to discuss the following: 
“That the Members of Wicklow County Council object strenuously to the Coillte decision to 
partner with a UK based Investment Fund, Gresham House, in the purchase by this Fund of 
thousands of hectares of Irish forestry lands depriving the Irish people of the profits accruing 
and at the same time driving up the price of agricultural land which could be bought by Irish 
farmers. Furthermore, this Council calls on the Government to halt this arrangement 
immediately and ensure that the future ownership of Irish Forestry assets remains in the 
hands of the Irish People.” 
 
It was agreed by all to have a discussion on the topic at 4.30 p.m.

ITEM NO. 7 
 
To consider and adopt Speed Limit Byelaws for County Wicklow. 
 
Ms. L. Earls, Director of Services gave a presentation outlining the background and context 
to the speed limit bye-laws as follows: 
 
 Local authorities required to review speed limit byelaws on a 5 yearly basis. 
 Reasons- Safety, Traffic Management, Education, Enforcement. 
 Speed limits are set by default and can only be changed on a permanent basis, by the 
making of speed bye-laws. Limits can be fixed, variable or periodic. 
 Default speed limits are specified in the Road Traffic Act 2004. 
 Special Speed Limit Bye-Laws- Road Traffic Act 2004 & Local Govt Act 2001 (as 
amended) � Reserved function of Members. 
 
      Default speed limits apply to the following:- 
 
 Built up areas -50km, Regional & local roads � 80km. 
 �National roads � 100km, Motorways � 120km 
 
Special speed limits are specified in accordance with the guidelines ‘Setting & Managing 
speed limits in Ireland’. 
 
 set out in guidelines. 

 50km for built up areas, 60km, 80km for motorway, national road or built up area, 
100km for motorway/no urban or regional/local road/built up area and 120km dual 
carriageway. 
 Predraft consultation undertaken during 2021/2022 with Members, An Garda 
Siochana, District Engineers, adjoining LAs and TII. 
 A schedule of proposed amendments issued to all Members in early 2022 for their 
review and consideration, with presentations made to all districts. 
 Public consultation undertaken in Oct/Nov 2022. 
 257 submissions received. 
 Further engagement with Members in relation to the submissions in December 
2022. 
 Briefing session with Members in January. 
 Individual engagement in recent days. 
 Draft Bye-Laws circulated provided for 13 schedules:- 
 1st schedule�Default Built up Areas speed limit -50km. 
 2nd schedule- special speed limit 20km - no roads. 
 3rd schedule- special speed limit 30km. 
 4th schedule- special speed limit 40km � no roads. 
 5th schedule- special speed limit 50km. 
 6th schedule- special speed limit. 
 7th schedule-special speed limit -80km. 
 8th schedule- special speed limit 100km. 
 9th schedule- special speed limit 120km. 
 10th schedule- special periodic speed limit 20km- no roads. 
 11th schedule- special periodic speed limit 30km. 
 12th schedule- special periodic speed limit 40km- no roads. 
 13th schedule � special periodic speed limit 50km � no roads. 
 
 Consider the adoption of the draft bye-law, with or without amendment. 
 If adopted there will be two dates for them coming into operation:- 
 1st June - all speed limits with exception of periodic speed limits at school. 
 1st August - periodic speed limits at schools.
