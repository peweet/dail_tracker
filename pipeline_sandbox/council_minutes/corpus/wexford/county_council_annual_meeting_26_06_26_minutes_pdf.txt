Cathaoirleach Opening Remarks 
 
Outgoing Cathaoirleach Cllr. J. Sullivan opened the Annual Meeting of Wexford County 
Council and welcomed the attendance. Cllr. Sullivan welcomed Deputies Malcolm Byrne, 
George Lawlor, and Brian Byrne, Senator Cathal Byrne, former Deputies and former 
Councillors Hugh Byrne and Tony Dempsey, and former Councillor Willie Kavanagh. 
 
Cllr. J. Sullivan thanked Mr. E. Taaffe, the Management Team, and all the staff for their 
support. Cllr. Sullivan noted the following highlights during his term – the hosting of Fleadh 
Cheoil na hÉireann, Wexford Festival Opera, acquisition of lands at Ballynagee for a third 
level campus and educational facilities, ongoing development of Rosslare Europort with 
progress being made on the Oylegate to Rosslare motorway, the Kennedy Summer School, 
and leading the Wexford delegation to Savannah, Georgia in March. 
 
Cllr. Sullivan thanked his fellow Councillors whilst also acknowledging the challenges to his 
position and authority. Cllr. Sullivan advised that because of this he made the very hard 
decision to resign from Fianna Fáil and will serve out the remainder of his term as an 
independent Councillor. Cllr. Sullivan concluded by saying he was honoured to serve the 
people of Wexford as Cathaoirleach. 
 
Cllr.’s T. Forde, J. Codd, P. Barden, M. Farrell, L. Kelly, G. Carthy, M. Sheehan, J. Owens, O. 
Walsh, P. Kavanagh, B.A. Murphy and J. Dwyer joined in congratulating the outgoing 
Cathaoirleach on his tenure.  
  
Chief Executive Mr. E. Taaffe welcomed friends and family, Oireachtas members, and 
former Councillors to the public gallery. Mr. Taaffe thanked Cllr. Sullivan for his kind words 
and acknowledging the progress which has been made in many areas. He congratulated the 
outgoing Cathaoirleach on his year in office.  
 
Cllr. J. Sullivan welcomed Minister James Browne to the Annual Meeting.

Cllr. L. McDonald was presented with the Chain of Office by Cllr. J. Sullivan, following which 
Cllr. L. McDonald assumed the Chair. 
 
Cllr. L. McDonald acknowledged the presence of Minister James Browne, Deputies 
Malcolm Byrne, George Lawlor, and Brian Byrne, Senator Cathal Byrne, former Deputies 
and former Councillors Hugh Byrne and Tony Dempsey, former Councillor Willie Kavanagh, 
her family and friends. Cllr. McDonald thanked her proposer and seconder and all those 
who voted for her whilst also commending Cllr. Barden who contested the position. 
 
Cllr. L. McDonald paid tribute to the outgoing Cathaoirleach for his service and 
commitment to the people of Wexford. Cllr. McDonald outlined her aspirations and said 
the theme for her year in office is “delivery”. 
 
Cllr.’s B.A. Murphy, C. Walsh, O. Walsh, L. Kelly, M. Farrell, P. Barden, P. Breen, V. Barron, J. 
Fleming, A.R. O’Brien, D. Kenny, J. Codd, B. Murphy, M. Sheehan, A. Browne, T. Forde, J. 
Owens, G. Carthy, F. Staples, and G. Laffan congratulated Cllr. McDonald on her election. 
Chief Executive Mr. E. Taaffe congratulated Cllr. McDonald and her family and said he is 
looking forward to working with her over the next twelve months. Ms. L. Lacey and Mr. E. 
Hore joined the Chief Executive in congratulating Cllr. McDonald.
