CATHAOIRLEACH’S ADDRESS 
As he concluded his term as Cathaoirleach of the Council, Councillor Garry Murtagh reflected 
on what he described as a great honour and privilege to have served in the role. He expressed 
his sincere appreciation to his fellow Elected Members for their support and cooperation 
throughout the year. He also thanked the Chief Executive, the Directors of Services, the 
Meetings Administrator and all Council staff for their guidance, professionalism and 
assistance during his term of office. Councillor Murtagh acknowledged the support of the Leas 
Cathaoirleach, Councillor Niall Gannon, and thanked the local media for their continued 
coverage of the Council's work. In closing, he paid a special tribute to his family Lorraine, 

Moya and Ben for their encouragement, patience and support throughout his year as 
Cathaoirleach. 
 
 
1. ELECTION OF CATHAOIRLEACH 
 
It was proposed by Councillor Paul Ross seconded by Councillor Turlough McGovern 
that Councillor Gerry Hagan be appointed Cathaoirleach of Longford County Council.  
 
As there was no other nomination, Councillor Gerry Hagan was, thereby, elected 
Cathaoirleach of Longford County Council until the Annual Meeting in 2027. 
 
The Chain of Office was formally conferred on Councillor Hagan.  
Longford County Council Chief Executive Paddy Mahon said that it is a huge honour 
for Cllr Hagan and his family, and it is a great opportunity to build on the notable work 
being undertaken in the county.  He outlined that Councillor Hagan has set out a 
number of challenges for the year ahead and he can assure him that the Council 
Executive and employees will work with him to ensure that as much as possible will 
be achieved under his tenure.  
Councillor Hagan expressed his sincere thanks to his proposer, seconder and fellow 
Elected Members for the honour and trust they had placed in him by electing him to 
serve as Cathaoirleach of Longford County Council for the next twelve months. He 
described the occasion as one of the proudest moments of his life, noting that while 
it was a privilege to be elected by the people to represent his community, it was an 
even greater honour to be chosen by his colleagues to serve as the County's first 
citizen. He said he was delighted to share the occasion with his family and friends. 
Councillor Hagan paid tribute to his sisters for their constant encouragement and 
support throughout his political journey. He also acknowledged the support of the 
Fine Gael branch in Killoe, with particular thanks to Jim McGoldrick for his guidance 
and encouragement over the years. He took the opportunity to remember the late 
Victor Kiernan, recognising his valued contribution and influence. 
He expressed his appreciation to the Mercy Secondary School, Kilbeggan, and its 
Principal, Garrett Farrell, for their support and understanding in facilitating his leave 
from teaching to allow him to devote his full attention to the role of Cathaoirleach. 
Looking to the year ahead, Councillor Hagan outlined several priorities that he hopes 
to advance during his term of office and reaffirmed his commitment to working 
collaboratively with the Elected Members, the Chief Executive, Council staff and the 
wider community to deliver on these objectives. He concluded by acknowledging the 
dedication and leadership shown by the outgoing Cathaoirleach, Councillor Garry 
Murtagh, and thanked him for his service and stewardship of the Council over the past 
year. 

Cathaoirleach Councillor Gerry Hagan wished to acknowledge Councillor Mick Cahill’s 
absence from the meeting and he wished him a speedy recovery, hoping to see him 
back in the Chamber soon.  
 
2. ELECTION OF LEAS CATHAOIRLEACH  
It was proposed by Councillor Paul Ross seconded by Councillor Turlough McGovern 
that Councillor Mark Casey be appointed Leas Cathaoirleach of Longford County 
Council.  
 
As there was no other nomination, Councillor Mark Casey was, thereby, elected Leas 
Cathaoirleach of Longford County Council until the Annual Meeting in 2027. 
 
Councillor Mark Casey thanked his proposer Councillor Paul Ross and his seconded 
Councillor Turlough McGovern for nominating him for the position of Leas 
Cathaoirleach for the next 12 months.  
Councillor Casey expressed that he is delighted to be Leas Cathaoirleach of Longford 
County Council for the next 12 months and he is looking forward to working closely 
with Cathaoirleach Councillor Gerry Hagan.  
 
Chief Executive Paddy Mahon extended his congratulations to Councillor Hagan and 
Councillor Casey and he wished them both the best of luck in their roles for the year 
ahead.
