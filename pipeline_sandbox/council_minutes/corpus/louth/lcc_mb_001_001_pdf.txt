County Surveyor's Report to the County Council at the Second Quarterly
Meeting held on August 30th, 1900.
MR. CHAIRMAN AND GENTLEMEN,
The New Road through Glack, in Ardee Electoral Division, has been
completed and handed over in good order, but an application for a Maintenance
Contract (No. 1 Ardee) was rejected. The road cost [720 to construct, and after
the first two years it might be kept in repair for about L 12 per annum.
The protection wall (No. Drogheda) is not required so far as 1 can judge.
If there is any danger at one occupier's land, where the wall is to be built,
then there is exactly the same danger in the case of three adjoining occupiers, whose
lands ought also to be fenced.
The road through Rassan Bog (Creggan, Dundalk) has been badly damaged
by an occupier who cut turf close up to the roadside where there is no fence,
leaving a vertical drop of about nine feet.

OF MOTION.
1. I hereby give Notice of Motion, that at the next Meeting of the County Council I shall
move :—That the Directors of the Great Northern Railway Company be asked by
this Council in making their next audit to take into consideration the earnings between
the unction at Dromin and the Seaport towns of Dundalk and Drogheda, accruing
to t em from the carriage of Goods, Cattle and Passengers, which come or go
over the Ardee Branch Line, and which Goods, Cattle and Passengers, were taken to and
from the Seaports by road previous to the building of the Ardee Branch Line, as vve con-
sider the present arrangement whereby the Company allows for earnings on the Ardee
Branch only, to be unfair to the ratepayers, inasmuch as less than one-third of the earnings
brought to said Company, by reason of Ardee Branch Line, is allowed by ghe Company to
the credit of the ratepayers in the making of the audit.
That copies of this resolution be sent to the Directors of the Great Northern Railway
Company, and to the Parliamentary representative of South Louth.
PATRICK MURRAY.
(Signed),
Received 23rd July, 1900.
2. I hereby give Notice of Motion, that at the next Quarterly Meeting of the Council I
shall move—That the following Four Committees be appointed in lieu of those at present
existing, i.e. : —1st, Finance; 2nd, Proposals; 3rd, Roads and Piers; 4th, Executive
under Diseases of Animals' Act, 1894, and that each Committee consist of nine members.
No Councillor to be appointed on more than one Committee, and that the Chairman, and
Vice-Chairman, for the time being, be Ex-Officio Members of all Committees. That
each Committee shall elect its own Chairman and Vice-Chairman, who shall be responsible
for its efficient working, and draft the Report of its Proceedings, and move same before the
Council, and that the Chairman, with Councillors Dickson and O'Meara, be hereby ap-
pointed as a Selection Committee to arrange the names of the Councillors who are to act
on the above Committees.
(Signed),
T. J. BYRNE.
