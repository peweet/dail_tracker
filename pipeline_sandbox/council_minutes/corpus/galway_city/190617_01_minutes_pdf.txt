Matters of Accuracy:
Cllr. M. Cubbard referred to the debate around Meeting starting times in Standing Orders. It was Cllr.
M. Cubbard and not Cllr. M. Crowe who proposed we don't start new suggestions around Meeting
times until the new council in place in 2019.

19 in the Minutes dated 8th May. Cllr. C. Ö Conchüir informed that he had abstained from the vote in
Standing Order 2.
Cllr. P. Conneely wished to have it noted that he raised the issue in relation to loss of Parking
revenue from the pay and display machines & suspension of a staff member which was not recorded
in the Minutes dated 15th May. Cllr. C. Connolly informed that there were two motions on the
today's agenda, NOM 24/4 & 11/5 which had both been passed by SPC.
Matters Arising:
The following issues were raised by Members under Matters Arising:
Cllr. P. Conneely — Under Standing Order 8 he should be entitled to correspondence at the same time
as other Members. The CE Mr B. McGrath informed Cllr. Conneely that Agenda's for Meetings would
continue to be delivered to him but City Council did not have the resources to run a delivery service
for all correspondence. Members were provided with Laptops, printers and were informed by email.
Information was also placed on the Extranet. It was up to Cllr. Conneely if he wished to avail of this
option or not.
Cllrs. O. Crowe, M. Cubbard, N. McNeilis, T O' Flaherty, M. Lohan, P. Flannery, C. Connolly & F. Fahy
raised the following issues:
Upkeep of Roundabouts, Grass-cutting, Ballinfoile Community Centre, CCTV Westside, Sunday
Parking, Cappagh Park Tender, Playgrounds, Ballymoneen Site, Sale of Tourist Office Fairgreen,
Corrib Great Southern,
Detailed responses and clarifications on matters arising were issued by Mr. B. McGrath Chief
Executive & Ms. E. Ruane, A/Director of Services. Ms Ruane expressed her regrets that Ballinfoile
Community Centre had not opened on time which was due to resource issues. She confirmed that
the centre would be opened in August. She also informed the meeting that works were due to
commence on CCTV in the Westside during July.
Mr. McGrath informed that it had been brought to his attention during the last week that the Tourist
Office, Fairgreen had been sold by Failte Ireland. He informed Members that City Council on the
advice of Blake & Kenny Solicitors, had written to Falite Ireland to advise them that they were in
breach of the terms of their lease with the Council, as they had sold the property without the
consent of Galway City Council. He would keep Members informed when we received a response
from Failte Ireland. Mr McGrath stated that there would be an update on City Playgrounds provided
to the next meeting. He also informed Members that the Corrib Great Southern was not in the
Derelict Site Register.
Director of Services, Mr T. Connell informed Members that the Parks Department were monitoring
complaints in relation to grass cutting and the number of complaints had reduced significantly. Mr
Connell also confirmed that there is upgrading of equipment taking place in existing City
playgrounds.
In relation to Sunday Parking Senior Engineer, Mr U. Finn informed Members that signs had been
changed and only stickers had to be removed to show Sunday parking times.

Kirwan Roundabout Presentation given by U. Finn Senior Engineer, Galway City
Council and D. Delaney, Project Manager, Halcrow Barry.
In the documents circulated at the Meeting Mr Finn and Mr Delaney outlined the
background to the project, the 6 Options and how they were assessed, with the results
showing Option 3B as the preferred option.
Detailed discussions took place among Councillors with the following issues raised:
Over/Underpass, Cost, Traffic Counts, Consultation with Local Community, Right hand turn
to Terryland Business Park, Provision for Bus Lane, support for Option B, More information
required & CPO process.
Detailed responses and clarifications were issued by Mr. Finn & Mr Delaney who highlighted the
closeness on Safety of the top three options, the reduction in rat running, the overpass option which
was examined but considered too expensive and had too much of an impact on private property.
They also informed that traffic surveys had been carried out, junction counts and that public
transport figures would be provided at Part VIII stage and individual access will be provided for in the
plans. Mr Finn confirmed that safety information including costs would be circulated to all Members.
The CE advised that he had met with the NTA and TII and a lot of the discussion centred on funding.
He stated that we would need to use funding very soon or we would lose it.

Tuam Road Bus Corridor
Senior Engineer Mr. U. Finn provided a verbal update to Members on the project scope, key
issues including accommodating bus lanes, cycle lanes, design issues, pinch points &
timeframes.
Detailed discussions took place among Councillors with the following issues raised:
Maintaining turns to Bothar na Mine, Terryland Business Park, McDonaghs DIY & Liaising
with Bus Eireann re Bus timetables.
Mr Finn provided responses & clarifications and highlighted that this was only the early
stage of the design process. He would take on board the comments made by Members in
maintaining the right turn. The Tuam Road Bus Corridor still has to go before Members as a
Part VIII and he would report back to members in September.
Draft Ardaun Local Area Plan Update
Executive Planner Ms D. Egan provided a non statutory update on the Draft Ardaun Local
Area Plan. In the document circulated by Ms Egan she highlighted the Background of the
Plan which is a specific objective of the City Development Plan 2017 — 2013, the recent
developments at national level (Ardaun has been designated by the Department as a Major
Urban Housing Delivery Site) and the next steps going forward which would include
completion of the preparation of the draft LAP before it goes on public display in July.
Detailed discussions took place among Councillors with the following issues raised:
Positive news for the City, City & County Council working together, Ownership of the land,
expansion of housing and the price of houses and extension of the City Boundary rather
than merger with the County Council.
Ms Egan provided detailed responses to the issues raised including the multiple ownership
of the land in question, size of the area which was 405 acres approx, the potential delivery
of 2,500 units of housing and the timeframe involved.
The CE confirmed that under "Rebuilding Ireland" Ardaun was considered a major urban
housing delivery site which had the potential to deliver thousands of housing units in a city
which currently had a huge shortage of housing units and this was affecting the city across a
number of sectors.
Cllr. P. Flannery proposed "that Galway City Council calls on the Government to extend the city
boundary and dispense with the unworkable merger of both Councils," this was seconded by Cllr.
O. Crowe and the vote went as follows:
