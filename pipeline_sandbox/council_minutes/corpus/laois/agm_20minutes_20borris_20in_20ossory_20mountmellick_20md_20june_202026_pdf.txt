Councillor Conor Bergin, Cathaoirleach extended thanks to all his colleagues and 
officials for their support during his term of office as Cathaoirleach of the Borris-in-
Ossory/Mountmellick Municipal District.  Councillor Bergin also paid tribute to the 
members of the press for their excellent coverage of the MD District. 
The Members individually complimented Councillor Bergin on his excellent leadership 
throughout the year.  Ms. Angela McEvoy, Director of Services on behalf of the 
Executive thanked Councillor Bergin also.   
 
1. 
ELECTION OF CATHAOIRLEACH  
It was proposed by Councillor Paddy Bracken and seconded by Councillor John King  
“That Councillor Ollie Clooney  be appointed Cathaoirleach for the coming year” 
Councillor Ollie Clooney having been elected Cathaoirleach, thanked his proposer and 
seconder and all his colleagues and paid tribute to the former Cathaoirleach of the 
Municipal District, Councillor Conor Bergin.  Ms. Angela McEvoy, Director of Services 
wished Councillor Ollie Clooney well on his election. 
 
2. 
ELECTION OF LEAS-CHATHAOIRLEACH  
It was proposed by Councillor Paddy Bracken seconded by Councillor Conor Bergin: 
“That Councillor John King be appointed Leas-Chathaoirleach for the coming 
year” 
 

Councillor John King having been elected Leas Cathaoirleach thanked his proposer and 
seconder and his colleagues in the Borris-in-Ossory/Mountmellick District. 
 
3. 
AGREE SCHEDULE OF MEETINGS  
The Members unanimously agreed the schedule of meetings and it was agreed that this 
would be circulated.  The next meeting was scheduled for the 8th of July 2026 at 9.30 
a.m.    It was agreed that the February Municipal District Meeting would take place in 
Bloom HQ.
