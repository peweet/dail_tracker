Item 2 (a) Draft Regional Spatial & Economic Strategy (RSES) - Material
Alterations
Ms. R. McNally reported on the Draft Regional Spatial and Economic Strategy, and the submission made by
Galway City Council on the draft Strategy and draft Metropolitan Area Strategic Plan (MASP), authored by
the North West Regional Assembly. Ms. R. McNally advised Members of the concerns of Galway City Council
that the RSES/ MASP did not reflect the importance of the city of Galway, and that there were concerns about
proposals for the airport site, which contravened national and local strategies.
Comments and questions from Members included:
Urban density; expansion of the metropolitan area; airport site; submission from landowner beside the
airport which was not intended to cover the airport site; omission of the N84 in the strategy;
Ms. R. McNally responded comprehensively to Members, and advised that there was poor consultation with
Galway City Council officials during the preparation of the report. The Galway Transport Strategy defines the
metropolitan area of Galway, or the zone of economic influence of the city, with densities set at a national
level. Ms. R. McNally advised Members of the importance of Members' voices at the Regional Assembly to
influence the final Strategy.
The Chief Executive advised Members that a meeting would take place in the NWRA with the County Council
and the Elected Representatives regarding Galway City's needs, as the Strategy did not recognise the primacy
of Galway City in the region.

Item 4 (a) LED Street Light Replacement Programme.
Mr. U. Finn reported on the LED street light replacement programme in Galway City. The Mayor invited
clarifications and questions.
Comments from Members included:
Quality of the light; timeline to do the city; scheme started in estates but not yet complete; good public
reaction to lights; cost/ benefits with number of lights increasing over time; use of less lights; repair of lights;
Merlin College; EU funding for climate change improvements; variation in cost of installation; tree canopy
blocking lights at Corrib Park, Quincentennial Bridge, and Galway Clinic to Galway Crystal; light pollution;
burners; national funding allocated to Galway City; lights in Merlin Forest and at Westside Running Track;
dimming lights at night; energy cost for city; estates complete; and lighting in Doughiska and other parks to
prevent anti-social behaviour.
Mr. U. Finn responded comprehensively to Members' queries, and advised Members on targets for the LED
programme, and variation in costs for installation. He advised that where estates were partially complete,
this may be because of the requirement to move to a metered supply as replacements were done. Where a
bulb goes, the head is replaced with an LED lantern as part of maintenance, which means there may be a
singular LED light in an estate. Trees would be trimmed outside nesting season, as part of maintenance. Mr.
U. Finn advised Members that he would revert with a programme for next year for residential estates to be
completed, once the main roads were complete.
The Chief Executive recommended that reducing lighting be brought to the JPC for consideration in terms of
impacts on safety.
