(e) Consideration of Part VIII of the Planning & Development Act 2000, as amended — Construction
of new 3 storey museum building incorporating the refurbishment & restoration of Comerford
House. Works will include general upgrade and flood protection works, remodelling of hard and
soft landscaping within 'Museum Square' between the Spanish Arch, and the existing Galway City
Museum, together with minor elements of hard landscaping in front of Comerford House and the
Spanish Arch within the Fish Market and alterations to the existing Galway City Museum building.
Presentations on the proposed extension to Galway City Museum were given by Lucy O' Connor of
Howley Hayes Architects and Eithne Verling, Museum Director who outlined the types of exhibits
planned for the new development. Mark O Donnell, Senior Executive Officer gave details to the
Meeting of the Part VIII construction proposal.
At 5.30 Cllr. D. Lyons assumed the role of Chair.
Cllrs broadly welcomed the report with credit given to the CE for driving the project forward. Cllrs
also acknowledged the work of the Architects, Museum staff and Mark O' Donnell on the project.
Concerns and queries were raised in relation to flooding, parking, funding, opening hours, timeframe,
the 2 houses on Merchants Road, anti-social behaviour in the location at night time, need for rainy
day activities and the clean-up of the blind arch.
At 5.35 the Mayor resumed the role as Chair of the Meeting.
Clarifications were given by Ms. O'Connor and Ms. Verling in relation to flood barriers, raised ground-
floor levels, wheelchair accessibility, flat roof design, ceiling height for exhibitions, the enclosing of
the City wall, cleaning and repointing of the blind arch, Claire Sheridan sculptor, visitor numbers and
increase in opening times.
Seconded: Cllr. B. Cameron
Proposed: Cllr. C. Connolly
"That Galway City Council proceed with Part VIII of the Planning & Development Act 2000, as
amended — construction of new 3 storey museum building incorporating the refurbishment &

remodelling of hard and soft landscaping within 'Museum Square' between the Spanish Arch, and
the existing Galway City Museum, together with minor elements of hard landscaping in front of
Comerford House and the Spanish Arch within the Fish Market and alterations to the existing
Galway City Museum building.
