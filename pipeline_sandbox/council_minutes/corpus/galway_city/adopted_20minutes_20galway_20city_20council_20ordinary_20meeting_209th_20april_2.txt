Mayor Flannery expressed sympathies as Mayor, to the family and friends of a Polish national
who was tragically found dead in the City on the morning of Friday 6th April 2018. The
deceased man had availed of emergency homeless services in the City over a period of time
from September 2017 including services at the Fairgreen Hostel and at the Cold Weather
Response. At this stage the cause of death is unknown and Galway City Council and its
partners in the provision of homeless services extend its deepest sympathies to the family
and friends of the deceased. While a bed in the Cold Weather Response remains available for
all rough sleepers in the City, those that do not to accept this offer are provided with a
sleeping bag and a harm minimization pack. The Council will work with the deceased man's
family, An Garda Sfochåna and homeless service providers in the City to provide whatever
supports are now required.

(a) Presentation on Draft Recreation & Amenity Master Plan for South Park
Ms. Keith Mitchell, Cunnane Stratton Reynolds, Landscape Architects and Town Planners
presented the Draft Recreation & Amenity Master Plan for South Park. He confirmed that the
Draft Plan is unchanged since February 2018 and this is the third iteration and that written
submissions have been considered. Councillors input into the Draft Plan was acknowledged.
Mr. Mitchell highlighted a number of features incorporated into the design arising from the
