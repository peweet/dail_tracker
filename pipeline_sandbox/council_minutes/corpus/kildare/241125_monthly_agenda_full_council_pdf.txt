Rúin Chomhairle Contae Chill Dara 
Motions Kildare County Council 
 
20. 
An Comhairleoir - Councillor Bernard Caldwell 
That the County Kildare Access Network flag is flown in specific agreed locations 
within the remit of the council for Kildare Disability Week (30th November until 7th 
December 2025) to incorporate International Day of Persons with Disabilities. 
 
21. 
An Comhairleoir - Councillor Donna Phelan 
That the council agrees to establish a Women’s Caucus to provide a formal, cross-
party platform for women councillors to collaborate, share experiences and advance 
gender equality within local government.  
 
22. 
An Comhairleoir - Councillor Chris Pender 
That the council supports the development of a dedicated parkour facility in County 
Kildare, and that the executive:  
a) Identifies a suitable location through engagement with young people and local 
community groups 
b) Carries out a short feasibility process to confirm design, cost, and delivery 
options, and  
c) Engages with specialist providers to ensure quality, value for money and a 
clear timeline for public consultation and delivery.

23. 
An Comhairleoir - Councillor Peter Melrose 
That this council, in accordance with its powers under Regulation 4 of S.I. No. 
264/2025 of the Planning and Development (Prescribed Newspapers) Regulations 
2025, commits to establishing and publishing a list of approved local newspapers and 
online publications for the purpose of advertising planning applications, and require 
developers to advertise planning applications in at least one such approved local 
publication, either in print or online, in addition to any national newspaper prescribed 
by the Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage. 
 
24. 
An Comhairleoir - Councillor William Durkan 
That the council outlines how many streetlamps are under Kildare County Council 
management, how many have been replaced with modern LED lamps to date, and 
what plan does the council have to replace the remaining lamps.  
 
25. 
An Comhairleoir - Councillor Noel Connolly 
Go leathnóidh Comhairle Contae Chill Dara a sruthú beo ar chruinnithe chun 
cruinnithe Ceantair Bhardasaigh a áireamh. 
That the council extends its live streaming of meetings to include municipal district 
meetings.  
 
26. 
An Comhairleoir - Councillor Pádraig McEvoy 
That the council explores options to apply a ‘climate lens’ to the Annual Budget Book 
as a tool to support informed awareness of climate action across all service areas, to 
communicate progress and improve the visibility of climate-related activities, thereby 
strengthening the monitoring and governance of measures required to reduce 
emissions and to build environmental, social, and economic resilience in line with 
national climate action objectives.  
 
27. 
An Comhairleoir - Councillor Brian O’Loughlin 
That the council calls for the limitation of single use, disposal cutlery, cups and plates 
in restaurants and cafes where food and beverages are consumed on-site (either 
inside or in designated outdoor seating areas).

28. 
An Comhairleoir - Councillor Pat Balfe 
That the council prepares a report on all recycling collection points across County 
Kildare, to include: 
a) The location and capacity of each site, along with the frequency of 
collections currently in place 
b) Identification of sites where overflowing or access issues are regularly 
reported, including parking or safety concerns, and 
c) Recommendations to address high-usage locations, improve access and 
parking, and reduce littering or maintenance problems.
