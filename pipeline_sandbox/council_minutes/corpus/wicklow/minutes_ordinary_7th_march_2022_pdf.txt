ITEM NO. 1 
 
Section 140 motion, Local Government Act 2001, as amended, in the names of Cllr. G. 
O’Neill and Cllr. J. Behan seeking to withdraw the Blessington Greenway proposals from 
consideration by An Bord Pleanala. 
 
 

Date: 25th February 2022 
To: Brian Gleeson, Chief Executive, Wicklow County Council 
In accordance with Section 140 of the Local Government Act, 2001, as amended by the Local 
Government Reform Act 2014, we, the undersigned Members of Wicklow County Council, 
direct the Chief Executive of Wicklow County Council to withdraw the Blessington Greenway 
proposals from consideration by An Bord Pleanala. 
This will allow for the removal of the elements of the Greenway Plan which will cause serious 
negative impacts on the lives of the people living in the areas surrounding the Blessington 
Lakes including the installation of traffic lights on Knockeiran, Baltyboys and Valleymount 
Bridges and the proposed new parking restrictions on Blessington Main Street which are 
widely opposed by the local community. 
The reason for the motion is the lack of meaningful consultation with Elected Members of 
Wicklow County Council and the general public before the matter was referred to An Bord 
Pleanala. 
We further direct that the matter be placed before the Members of Wicklow County Council 
at the forthcoming Council Meeting to be held at 2.00 p.m. on Monday 7th March 2022 in 
the Chamber of Wicklow County Council, County Buildings, Wicklow. 
Cllr. Gerry O'Neill 
Cllr. Joe Behan 
The Chief Executive referred to legal advice received and advised the Elected Members that 
the suggested resolution is one which if proceeded with, will be legally unsound, invalid and 
in breach of Section 140(2) and/or Section 140(10).  Furthermore, no resolution under 
Section 140 of the 2001 Act can be brought to force the Chief Executive to withdraw a 
required application under Section 177AE of the Planning & Development Act because it 
would be an attempt to force the Chief Executive to do something that the Council is not 
lawfully able to do.  Any such resolution would be void and legally ineffective. 
Cllr. G. O’Neill raised the following points and concerns: 
 
 That there was no consultation with the people, Elected Members and staff in West 
Wicklow. 
 First learned at a Town Team meeting that the application had gone from the 
Council to An Bord Pleanála. 
 An Garda Siochana and the Emergency Services were not informed. 
 Risks involved dividing bridges in two, traffic on one side and pedestrians/cyclists on 
the other side. 
 Public meetings held and submissions sent to An Bord Pleanála.  
 Advised the Elected Members that the application could be withdrawn according to 
an An Bord Pleanála official. 

 In favour of the Greenway, however,  lack of consultation unacceptable. 
 
 
 
Cllr. J. Behan paid tribute to Cllr. G. O’Neill for his persistence in raising the issue and in 
continuing to do so.  He also raised the following concerns: 
 
 That Wicklow County Council did not listen to genuine requests by Cllr. O’Neill and 
the local community for this Greenway Project to be withdrawn and made better. 
 That he is not convinced by the legal advice. 
 That no-one is against the Greenway but want a Greenway that will work. 
 That this project will damage the quality of life of many people who live around the 
lakes of Blessington. 
 The installation of traffic lights on 3 bridges will add from 5 to 10 minutes extra on 
journeys. 
 Safety concerns in respect of ambulances or fire engines to get across the bridges in 
a hurry. 
 
There followed further discussion by the Elected Members and response by the Chief 
Executive.  Ms. L. Gallagher, Director of Service read Section 140(7) and (10) (e) of the Local 
Government Act 2001, as amended, to the Elected Members:  
 
Section 140 (7) Without prejudice to any other relevant requirements, it is necessary for the 
passing of a resolution under this section that at least one-third of the total number of 
members of the local authority concerned vote in  favour of the resolution. 
 
(10) (e) to any act, matter or thing to be done or effected in the performance of the 
executive functions of a local authority in respect of its functions as a planning authority 
under the Planning & Development Act 2000. 
 
It was proposed by the Cathaoirleach Cllr. S. Cullen, seconded by Cllr. P. Fitzgerald to defer 
the item for 2 weeks until 21st March 2022 in order to seek further clarification and legal 
opinion. 
 
Motion proposed by Cathaoirleach Cllr. S. Cullen, seconded by Cllr. P. Fitzgerald to defer this

opinion. A vote was taken and following a roll call was passed by a margin of 24 votes for, 6
