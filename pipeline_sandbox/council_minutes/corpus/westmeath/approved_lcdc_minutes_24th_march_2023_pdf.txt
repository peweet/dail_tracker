Draft minutes of the Meeting of  
Westmeath Local Community Development Committee, (LCDC)  
held on Friday 24th March 2023 at 10.00am in  
Mullingar Charity Variety Group, Forest Park, Mullingar, Westmeath (Hybrid Meeting) 

2 
 
Hugh Farrell welcomed all LCDC members, Westmeath County Council staff and Westmeath Community 
Development staff to the meeting. He also thanked Ger Keena of Mullingar Charity Variety Group for his 
hospitality. Hugh reminded members of the confidentiality of the items on today’s agenda and asked that 
they declare any conflicts of interests that they may have.   
 
1.  
Draft Minutes of LCDC Meeting held on Friday 17th Feb 2023 
On the proposal of Cllr. Tom Farrell and seconded by Cllr. Bill Collintine the minutes of the LCDC meeting 
held on Friday 17th Feb 2023 were approved.   
 
2.  
Matters Arising 
 None 
 
3.  Mullingar Charity Variety Group- LEADER Presentation 
Ger Keena gave a presentation on the works completed in Forest Park by the Mullingar Charity Variety 
Group thanks to LEADER funding. Due to the improvements and upgrade works over the last number of 
years, the facility is now facilitating 100 adults & 150 children every week. Ger thanked all the committee 
and all the staff involved in the LEADER Programme for their help in delivering the funding.   
 
4. Comhairle na nÓg Presentation 
Members of Westmeath Comhairle na nÓg gave a presentation on their work this year.  
Cllr Tom Farrell praised the students for the great presentation. He acknowledged the success of the Road 
Safety Event and asked would it be possible to make it an annual event. He suggested that the 
Transportation section of Westmeath County Council consider taking it on each year. It costs 
approximately €5,000 to run the event.  
 
Pat Gallagher thanked the members for their work and for their presentation. He would be interested in 
receiving a submission from Comhairle na nÓg in relation to the Road Safety Event acknowledging that it 
would be great to make it an annual event. Pat congratulated Comhairle na nÓg on 20 years in operation 
and wished them well for the future.  
 
Cllr. Bill Collintine also thanked the members for their presentation and was happy to see they had carried 
out work on an Anti-Vaping Programme. Bill continued that we do not know the dangers/harm of vaping 
and it is great to make young people aware of this.  
 
Joe McEvoy congratulated the members on an excellent presentation. He said he is looking forward to 
seeing the Green-guide they have been working on. 
 
5. LEADER 
 
6. LCDC Annual Report 
Pat Murtagh discussed the LCDC Annual Report with the members and asked them to consider the report.  
Pat is hoping to bring the report to the Council Meeting Monday week.  
The Annual Report was proposed by Frank Murtagh and seconded by Cathryn Whelehan.

7. Local Economic & Community Plan 
Pat Murtagh gave the LCDC Members an update on the LECP. Pat said that the draft Socio-Economic 
Statement needs to be brought before the LCDC & the SPC for approval. He advised members that an 
online meeting might be required to discuss / adopt the Socio-Economic Statement.  
 
8. Empowering Communities Programme 
Frank Murtagh gave the LCDC members an update on the Empowering Communities Programme and 
their work to date. They have also organised a Meet and Greet Coffee Afternoon/ Easter Egg Hunt on 6th 
April 2023 for the Residents of Dalton Park
 
9. Community Recognition Fund 2023 
Annette Barr Jordan gave the LCDC members an update on the Community Recognition Fund 2023. The 
funding proposal has been submitted to the Department for approval and we are awaiting feedback on 
same. 
 
Hugh Farrell reminded the LCDC members of the confidentiality on this as it is still a work in progress and 
projects have not yet been approved by the Department. 
 
Cllr. Tom Farrell welcomed the projects and is delighted that its 100% funded. Tom added that he hopes 
the Department respond sooner rather than later. Tom asked who will be carrying out the works in Dun 
na Si if it is approved. Annette informed him that it will be the Municipal District if it is approved. Annette 
said she will make contact with the Department and ask when we should have an update from them.  
 
 
10. Correspondence  
Pat Murtagh informed the members that there has been an increase of €10,000 for PPN this year. Pat 
also thanked Mary Goldsberry and the Team for their help with the Moate Town Centre First Team. 
 
11. AOB  
Frank Murtagh thanked Ger Keena for his hospitality and was delighted to have held today’s meeting at 
Mullingar Charity Variety Group. 
 
12. Date and Venue of Next Meeting 
The next LCDC meeting will take place on Friday 26th May 2023 at 10am. The meeting will be held in the 
Council Chambers in Mullingar.
