The Meeting commenced with a moment of silent reflection. Mayor Cubbard welcomed all to
the Meeting.
Ms N. Moggan, A/ Head of Finance, referred Councillors to the draft Budget 2020 Report, issued
in advance of the Meeting, and presented an overview of the Budget process and the significant
issues for consideration.
Councillors were advised that the draft budget was for €99.7million, an increase on the adopted
budget for 2019, and a proposed increase of €11.8 million on the 2019 budgeted figure.
Consultations with members of the Corporate Policy Group on the preparation of the Draft
Budget, in accordance with the provisions of the Local Government Acts, took place on the 6th
September, 4th October and the 23th October 2019.
The proposed Draft Budget for 2020 was compiled on the basis of no proposed increases in
Rates or Local Property Tax. Ms. N. Moggan outlined the sources of Revenue Income, including
Commercial Rates, LPT, Goods and Services, and Grants and Subsidies.

Ms. N. Moggan thanked the previous Council for their prudence in previous budgets, which had
built up the provision on reserves. Schemes such as the development of a new cemetery, the
CFRAMS work and work in the Housing Capital Area will progress in 2020.
Ms. Moggan advised Councillors that decisions were now required on the determination of
percentage of refund to apply in respect of commercial rates on vacant properties for 2020,
Adoption of Annual Budget 2020 and the determination of Annual Rate on Valuation to be
levied for 2020.
