in the Regional Spatial and Economic Strategy as ‘towns with high levels 
of population growth and a weak employment base which are reliant on 
other areas for employment and/or services and which require targeted 
‘catch up’ investment to become more self-sustaining’1. These towns 
range in size (as per the Census of population in 2022) from c. 2,000 
to 4,500 persons. Population growth in Level 4 towns overall is 
targeted to be in the 20%-25% range between 2016 and 2031, with 
variation in future growth rates between towns in the level due to 
developments already underway in some
