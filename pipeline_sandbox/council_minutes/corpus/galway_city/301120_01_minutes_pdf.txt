The Meeting commenced with a moment of silent reflection. Mayor Cubbard welcomed all to the
Meeting.
Mr. McGrath, Chief Executive referred Councillors to the draft Budget 2021 Report, issued in
advance of the Meeting, and presented an overview of the Budget process and the significant
issues for consideration.
Councillors were advised that the draft budget is €102,540,409 million, a proposed increase of
(2.8%) on the 2020 budgeted figure of €99,696,970. Mr. McGrath advised that having no deficit
at the end of 2020 is central to being in a position to bring forward a positive and progressive
budget.
The proposed Draft Budget for 2021 was compiled on the basis of no proposed increases to the
rate of local property tax or the annual rateable value (ARV) regarding commercial rates currently
€67.40. Mr. McGrath welcomed the Government's response in relation to the nine month rates
waiver for 2020 which was beneficial to Galway businesses and a key to retaining employment
and supporting survival of businesses. He referred to the delivery of Restart Grants of c. €22m
which further invested in the local economy.

Mr. B. McGrath outlined factors influencing the preparation of the Budget which included: the
ongoing impact of COVID-19; the world economy and Ireland being in recession; delivery of water
services through a Service Level agreement with Irish Water; grant allocations; local authority
borrowings; statutory demands; impacts of Brexit; the requirement to keep the budget in balance
and the Corporate Plan 2019-2024.
