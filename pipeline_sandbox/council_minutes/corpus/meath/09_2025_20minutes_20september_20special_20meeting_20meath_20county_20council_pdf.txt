The Chief Executive noted that Meath County Council integrates social considerations into its public procurement 
policies to advance social objectives related to employment opportunities, adherence to social and labour rights, 
social inclusion, equal opportunities, and accessibility. 
 
1.0 I am calling on Meath County Council to consider all lawful sanctions it can take against the apartheid state of 
Israel in order to put pressure on them to stop the Genocide and forced starvation of the Palestinian people. 
 
Councillor Alan Lawes introduced the motion.  He noted the Council's obligation to adhere to EU and national 
procurement regulations and emphasised the requirement to take decisive action in response to the ongoing 
conflict in Gaza.  Councillor Maria White supported the motion. 
 
The Meetings Administrator noted that in accordance with the provisions of the Local Government Act, 2001 (as 
amended) a minimum of 14 members would be required to approve the motion.
