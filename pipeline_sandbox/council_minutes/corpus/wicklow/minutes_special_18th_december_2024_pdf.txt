ITEM NO. 1 
 
To consider the filling of casual vacancy arising from the election of former Cllr. Edward 
Timmins to Dáil Éireann in accordance with Section 19 of the Local Government Act, 2001, 
as amended. 
 
Ms. R. Gillespie, A/Meetings Administrator advised the Elected Members that in accordance 
with Section 19 of the Local Government Act 2001 as amended, the Elected Members were 
notified of the casual vacancy due to the election of former Cllr. Edward Timmins to Dáil 
Éireann.   
 
Legislation states that a co-option shall be made after due notice at the next meeting of the 
Local Authority, after the expiration of 14 days from the occurrence of the vacancy or as 
soon after the expiration of the 14 days, as circumstances permit.   
 
Fine Gael has nominated Mr. Pat Mahon, Knocknadroose, Valleymount, Blessington, Co. 
Wicklow to fill the vacancy and he has consented in writing by letter dated 16th December 
2024. 
 
It was proposed by Cllr. A. Cronin, seconded by Cllr. S. Bourke and agreed by all to co-opt 
Mr. Pat Mahon on to the Council. 
 
The Elected Members welcomed Cllr. Mahon, wished him well and looked forward to 
working with him. 
 
Cllr. Mahon thanked the Elected Members for their kind words and stated that he looked 
forward to working with everyone. 
 
 
Congratulations were extended to Cllr. E. Timmins on his election to Dáil Éireann.
