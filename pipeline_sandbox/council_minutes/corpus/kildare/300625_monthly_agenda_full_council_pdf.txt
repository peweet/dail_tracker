Tairisceana na Comhairle Contae 
Motions Kildare County Council 
 
13. 
Councillors Aoife Breslin, Anne Breen and Rupert Heather 
That the members immediately seek a meeting with the Minister for Housing and the 
Department of Housing and Local Government following the announcement that the 
National Development Finance Agency (NDFA) have been instructed by the 
Department not to proceed to award the contract for PPP Bundle 3. Also, the 
Department’s decision to initiate a review of PPP Bundle 4 – to 7 which will have a 
very serious impact on the delivery of social housing in Kildare.  
 
14. 
Councillor Peggy O’Dwyer 
That the council provides details on the function of the cameras on traffic lights and 
confirms if there is an alert mechanism if they are faulty or not working.  
 
15. 
Councillor Peter Melrose 
That the council support the provision of a mobile sensory unit and a mobile 
accessible toilet for future events run by or in association with Kildare County Council 
that exceed an estimated footfall of 3,000 attendees, to be run as a pilot project with a 
view to extending to events of 500 attendees or more.

16. 
Councillor Nuala Killeen 
That Kildare County Councillors agree to commence a public participation aspect to 
the Municipal District Meetings for 2026 to permit organisations to present to their 
municipal district on issues that concern them that are relating to council functions.  
 
17. 
Councillors Chris Pender 
That the council engages with relevant stakeholders, including the South Western 
Regional Drugs and Alcohol Task Force, Cuan Mhuire, Youth for Peace Hostel, the 
Peter McVerry Trust, all Approved Housing Bodies operating within the county, and 
any other appropriate organisations, to identify a suitable property or properties that 
can be used to support individuals transitioning from addiction treatment into 
independent living; a) that the council prioritise properties located near existing 
support services or accessible public transport links to ensure continued engagement 
with recovery programmes; b) that the council explore funding opportunities and 
available schemes, such as the Capital Assistance Scheme, to support the acquisition 
or adaptation of such properties; and c) that a report on this engagement, including 
any potential locations identified and barriers encountered, be brought to the Housing 
SPC within an agreed timeframe.  
 
18. 
Councillor Seamie Moore 
That the council writes to the National Development Planners and East Midland 
Regional Authority on behalf of the elected members, asking them to consider the 
creation of a new town in mid Kildare, whilst at the same time designating a new 
Economic Corridor between Naas & Newbridge, along the commuter catchment route 
of the M7/M9 and the Dublin-Cork Rail Line, on an axis between Kilcullen and 
Rathangan. This would reduce the indicative expectation of future population overload 
of Naas and Newbridge and allow community amenities and community infrastructure 
facilities to be developed in those already overpopulated towns.

19. 
Councillor Donna Phelan 
That the council clarifies the role of the County Kildare Access Network (CKAN), and 
its role in engaging with local disability and inclusion advocates operating across the 
county. In particular, does CKAN actively liaise with and support grassroots initiatives 
and groups who are working directly with communities on the ground? 
 
20. 
Councillor Tracey O’Dwyer 
That the council increases its support for the months of May and June in towns and 
villages that enter the Tidy Towns competition by prioritising road sweeping, cleaning 
of path verges, particularly on approach roads and paths to towns and villages to 
further enhance Kildare’s overall results in Tidy Towns and support the volunteers 
involved.  
 
21. 
Councillor Pat Balfe 
That the council agrees to write to the Minister for Housing, Local Government and 
Heritage requesting a review of the recent changes to the Housing Aid for Older 
People Grant scheme, specifically the exclusion of funding for the purchase of new 
fossil fuel boilers, such as oil or gas boilers, effective from 1 January 2025, and 
further: 
a. To express concern that the current grant criteria may disproportionately affect 
older people and low-income households, who may not have the financial means to 
install new heating systems, even if partially funded by a grant. 
b. To request that the Minister consider reinstating support for like-for-like boiler 
replacements in cases where full system upgrades are not financially viable or 
technically feasible for the applicant. 
c. To advocate for a balanced approach that ensures the transition to sustainable 
energy sources does not inadvertently disadvantage vulnerable populations, 
particularly during the winter months when reliable heating is essential. 
 
22. 
Councillor Mark Leigh 
That the council writes to the Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage to 
urgently request the publication of updated Wind Energy Development Guidelines.

23. 
Councillor Noel Connolly 
Go scríobhfadh Comhairle Contae Chill Dara chuig an Aire Tithíochta, Oidhreachta 
agus Rialtais Áitiúil á iarraidh air polasaí a cheapadh agus a rointt do na Comhairle

asking him to formulate and share a policy to all Councils in relation to credit for time

24. 
Councillor Bill Clear 
That the council provides an update on progress made to date to change the council 
fleet of vehicles to Electric Vehicles, confirm if tenders have been sent out and the 
expected timeframe for completion of the process. 
 
25. 
Councillor Claire O’Rourke 
That, following the welcome introduction of the 60 km/h speed limit on Rural Local 
Roads in February this year, the council provides a progress report on the introduction 
of the 30 km/h speed limit in urban areas and when is it anticipated that a definition of 
urban areas be available to enable the council to progress this.
