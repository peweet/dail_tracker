ITEM NO. 7 
 
To consider the Chief Executive’s Report in accordance with Section 179 of the Planning 
and Development Act 2000, as amended, for a bus based park & ride facility located to the 
west of M11 Junction 6 at Fassaroe. 
 
Mr. J. Bowes, Senior Engineer gave a brief outline of the Chief Executive’s report advising:  
 
 Proposed development is for a strategic bus park and ride facility located to the west 
of the M11 Junction 6 in the townland of Fassaroe comprising of a car park of 388 
spaces, including 26 spaces for mobility impaired users, 42 EV charging, 40 bicycle 
parking stands and 20 bike lockers.   
 
 The project also includes public lighting, CCTV and tow bus bays, two passenger 
shelters and a bus turning area. 
 
 The proposed park and ride is in accordance with objectives in the County 
Development Plan and the Bray Local Area Plan. 
 

 The aim of the park and ride at this location is to intercept N11 traffic that originates 
further south and to transfer these journeys to bus from this location to Dublin City 
centre and vice-versa. 
 
 The new bus service would be an express service. 
 
 Proposed development is not at any significant risk of flooding. 
 
 Plans and details for the proposed development were made available online and in 
the Council offices from 23rd August 2023. Last day for submitting 
comments/observations was 4th October 2023. 
 
 Total of 14 submissions were received. 
 
There followed a broad discussion in which the Elected Members raised questions which 
were responded to by Mr. Bowes and by Mr. Dorgan and Mr. Dalton from NTA. 
 
It was proposed by Cllr. R. O’Connor, seconded by Cllr. M. Corrigan ‘In accordance with 
Section 179 of the Planning & Development Act 2000, as amended, the development of the 
M11 Junction 6 Fassaroe Bus Park & Ride Scheme may be carried out as recommended in the 
Chief Executive’s Report’ and following a roll call was passed by a margin of 27 for and 4 not

Suspension of Standing Orders 
 
1. Cllr. P. O’Brien, seconded by Cllr. G. Dunne requested a suspension of standing 
orders to discuss the following: 
 
“The ongoing destruction and deterioration of The Murrough in Wicklow Town”.   
  
 
A broad discussion followed.  The Elected Members highlighted the following: 
 
 Acknowledge and thank Deputy Stephen Matthews T.D., who visited the site after 
the last storm.  
 Finance secured, Irish Rail to proceed with some works.  
 The Murrough means so much to the fabric of the town.   
 Irish Rail has taken the initiative and will work alongside Wicklow County Council.     
 Area of special conservation. 
 The monkey pole having to be moved again.  
 On the verge of losing something so precious to the people of Wicklow. 
 Cannot afford to lose any more of The Murrough.   
 The Murrough is a precious amenity – mental health benefits. 
 Need to speed up the process to save the Murrough. 
 
The Chief Executive responded by stating that it was a very important issue.  The Murrough 
is a beautiful natural amenity.  She advised that funding has been given to Irish Rail who 
have emergency powers and that Wicklow County Council will be encouraging them to use 
these powers to do the work.  Mr. M. Devereux, Senior Engineer advised that he was invited 
by Irish Rail to sit on a steering group alongside colleagues from the OPW and other local 
authorities to work on the project. 
 
2. Cllr. S. Cullen, seconded by Cllr. I. Winters requested a suspension of standing orders 
to discuss the following: 
 
‘The 950-bed temporary accommodation at Glendalough Estate at Annamoe.  The 
concerns are the process, scale, location and the impact on the local communities’. 
 
There followed a broad discussion in which the Elected Members raised the following 
concerns: 

 
 On the 16th of October, a briefing was sent out to the councillors and the County 
Council that 950 bed accommodation was sought for Glendalough Estate at 
Annamoe.   
 Annamoe is a small, hamlet between Roundwood and Laragh, with a population of 
about a hundred people.   
 To bring 950 people into, a rural area without any consultation, either the County 
Council, the Council representatives, or communities. 
 No facilities in this rural location, no sewerage, no mains water on this site, no 
broadband.   
 A company called Pastures New have been awarded a contract to manage this site.  
 The communities of Roundwood, Annamoe and Laragh in total shock with the scale 
and numbers. 
 32 week contract, temporary rest area.   
 In relation to the County Development Plan, Annamoe was a level 8 in our housing 
strategy. 
 Housing crisis.   
 International obligations.   
 
It was proposed by Cllr. S. Cullen, seconded by Cllr. I. Winters to write to the Minister for 
Children, Disability, Equality, Integration & Youth requesting him to consider the following 
motion:  
 
‘To discuss the 950 bed temporary accommodation at Glendalough Estate at Annamoe. The 
concerns are the process, scale, location and the impact on the local communities. 
 
Wicklow County Council are disappointed with the lack of engagement with the Council and 
also the lack of facilities in the area for the development.  We request that the process is 
stopped with immediate effect in this location. Alternative accommodation should be sought 
in consultation with the relevant local authority’. 
 
And following a roll call the motion was passed by a margin of 15 for, 3 against, 10 not 
present and 3 abstained viz:-
