Traveller Accommodation Plan
Mr. T. Connell Director of Services referred Councillors to the Report issued in February 2016 and
subsequent update issued in April 2016 and outlined in detail the content of the Report and the
proposals contained within. Mr. Connell referred to the comprehensive assessment undertaken of
Council owned lands, the requirements agreed as part of the Traveller Accommodation Programme,
the needs assessment and specific accommodation requirements to be met by the proposals in the
Report, outlined the next steps in terms of development of specific designs, local consultation and
planning process and securing necessary funding to deliver on developments approved.
A comprehensive debate on the Report followed with contributions received from Counciloors:
McDonnell, Lyons, Flannery, O'Flaherty, Farrell, Cubbard, McNelis, M.J. Crowe, Ö Conchüir, Connolly,
Cameron, Larkin and Conneely. The debate included comment, queries and requests for
clarification on a number of issues including location of existing traveller specific accommodation
across the city, the suitability of halting site accommodation, fire safety audits on existing halting site
facilities and issues arising from same, the suitability of certain sites outlined in the Report for the
type of accommodation proposed, the requirement for a balance of accommodation across each of
the City Wards, a preference for a mix of social and group housing schemes to provide
accommodation, the requirement to consult with local residents associations and groups formed
who have expressed concern in relation to certain aspects of the proposal, whether funding had
been secured to deliver on the proposals outlined, the costs of acquiring lands in Ballybane,
requirement of solutions to be acceptable to local communities and the members of the travelling
community.
Councillors also queried the Report of the meetings with Area Councillors in March and a number
noted that they had received no notification of the meeting or were unable to attend. There was
condemnation of the ongoing movement of caravans from location to location in the city in recent
months and queries on the legal process in place to deal with such illegal encampments. There was
general consensus that the halting site concept is no longer an appropriate form of accommodation
and ather options should be considered. Mr. Connell pointed out that the members had made
provision for same in the Traveller Accommodation Programme and the proposals in front of the
Councillors were bringing implementation to the Programme agreed. Site costs and suitability of the
site in Ballymoneen Road was also noted and queried. Councillors queried the CENA project and
their role in delivering on the proposals for halting site accommodation. Councillors acknowledged
the work of the Housing Department in dealing with recent issues in Doughiska and noted the
importance of compliance with the legal provisions available to deal with such matters.
There was acknowledgement and acceptance that the Council has legal obligations under national
and European legislation to meet the accommodation needs of the travelling community but
Councillors queried the particular provisions and accommodation solutions outlined in the Report
and stated a number of concerns including lack of local consultation and issues of site suitability.
Mayor N. Larkin noted that the Council are obliged to consider the accommodation requirements for
members of the travelling community and assured Councillors that a way forward could be found
though engagement and consultation with the local community.
Mr. Connell, Director of Service, provided replies on queries raised and stressed that the Report
provides an implementation route for the Traveller Accommodation Programme agreed with by the
members. It was also stressed that the location requirements of the applicants must be considered
and it was not simply a matter of designating sites evenly across the 3 City Wards. Mr. Connell
stated that the Report is an initial plan for Councillor's consideration and detailed documents will
come before the members in due course for further consideration.
It was also stated that detailed consultation would take place locally in areas considered for traveller
specific accommodation and there was no issue in meeting local residents and residents associations
as part of the process. Mr. Connell noted that a number of existing halting site facilities are
overcrowded and solutions are now required to address this. In response to queries, Mr. Connell
advised that detailed fire safety audits of existing facilities were completed and were issued to the
Department for publication.
Mr. Connell clarified the Council's land acquisition policy and requirements or otherwise to bring
proposals for the acquisition of lands before the Councillors. Mr. Connell also outlined the existing
provisions to deal with tenancy sustainment and anti-soclal behaviour issues pertaining to all local
authority tenants. Mr. Connell advised Councillors that the Department was notified of the
proposals contained in the Report and that further detailed documentation would be required in
advance of securing funding for delivery of the units, including Part 8 approval of the members.

Solas / Arthouse Cinema
Mr. McGrath, Chief Executive, provided a detailed overview of the current status of the project and
of the ongoing negotiations with all relevant stakeholders. Mr. McGrath advised the Councillors that
as outlined in previous reports, when completed, the Galway Picture Palace project will comprise a
three screen cinema, situated in the heart of Galway City. The cinema will form part of a cultural
cluster in this area of the City, including the City Museum, Druid Theatre, An Taibhdhearc, and a
number of other developments that are currently being planned. Work commenced on site in July,
Following a re-tendering
2009 but ceased in 2011 when the contractor went into liquidation.
process, work recommenced in March, 2012 with a new contractor and continued until Phase 1 was
complete. The project is 80% complete. Phase 2 is largely internal fit-out and final completion.
In August 2015, agreement was reached with the Department of Arts, Heritage and the Gaeltacht
which would see City Council, on behalf of all the funders, taking on the responsibility of project
managing the project to completion. At the Ordinary Meeting of October 2015, Council agreed to
allow a further €242,000 funding towards the completion of the project in conjunction with the
DAHG's commitment of an additional €735,000. It was also indicated at that time that Element
Pictures Ltd., the operators of the Light House Cinema in Smithfield, Dublin, would become, with the
DAHG's consent, the operators of the Galway Picture Palace. Element indicated at that time that
they would invest €850,000 to complete the fit-out.
Extensive meetings over an extended period took place with all of the key stakeholders including the
project promoters Solas Teo and the contractors J.J. Rhatigan & Co. Ltd. The process of re-starting
works at the site on Spanish Parade/Lr. Merchants' Road has proved problematic over the
intervening months. Mr. McGrath advised that following extensive negotiations involving all the
stakeholders including the Department, Börd Scannån na hÉireann, the Western Development
Commission, Element Pictures Ltd., Solas Teo and J.J. Rhatigan agreement has been reached which
will see Element Pictures taking on the task to complete the fit-out and commissioning of the Picture
Palace with the contractors expected to commence work in July, 2016. The Picture Palace will open
to the public in April 2017.
Mr. McGrath advised that this new arrangement will involve the assignment by Solas Teo of its lease
on the Picture Palace, its completion and operation, to Element Pictures. The existing lease will be
varied to reduce the period of the lease from 99 to 30 years, with provisions to renew, and all such
considerations around the operation and programming of the cinema will be captured by covenant
to ensure that the city of Galway and the region will have a functioning, successful cultural, specialist
art-house cinema at the heart of our cultural offering.
Mr. McGrath stated that he was satisfied, as are all the other funders both national and regional,
that this is a good news story for the Picture Palace and good for the city of Galway. This new
arrangement will have no further financial impact on Galway City Council as it will take place within
the existing funding package agreed by the Councillors of €242,000, for which provision has been
made in the 2016 Budget. Mr. McGrath conveyed his thanks to the board and staff of Solas Teo
who have brought this project from initiation to this point, often with considerable challenges but
with enormous determination. He also conveyed his appreciation and confidence in Element
Pictures and their capacity to complete and operate the facility as a flagship part of the cultural offer
in Galway and the region.

Councillors: P. Conneely, N. McNelis, B. Cameron, C. Connolly, MJ. Crowe, D. Lyons, T. O'Flaherty, C,

structures and future role of the Solas board in the project, extent of works remaining, items to be
finalised, the extent of the financial contribution by City Council and the lease being assigned to
Element. A number of Councillors expressed concern in relation to the overall viability of the project
and costs incurred to date. A number of Councillors also sated satisfaction with the report and the
detail provided by the Chief Executive.
