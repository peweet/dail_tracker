2. Housing Assistance Payment Report
Ms. L. Mitchell Senior Executive Officer referred Councillors to the report issued and noted the
following: The Housing Assistance Payment (HAP) scheme is now available in Galway City Council
and is part of the housing options being offered to the public who are seeking housing
accommodation. HAP commenced on February ISt 2016. The introduction of HAP means that
Galway City Council can now provide housing assistance for households to live in the private rented
sector who qualify for social housing support. HAP will replace rent supplement for those
households with a long term housing need. Short-term rent supplement will continue to be
managed by the Department of Social Protection.
Individual targets are being set by DECLG for each of the 19 local authorities that have to date
commenced the implementation of HAP in order to ensure delivery of the targets set nationally
under the Social Housing Strategy. The HAP delivery target for Galway City Council has been set at 14
10
HAP households to be set-up each week. These targets will commence on a phased basis to allow for
an implementation period. This target is based on the number of long-term Rent Supplement
recipients in the area, numbers of households on the housing list and on current delivery patterns.
The target is calculated on projected average weekly performance across the year that recognises
there will be periods in the year when the target will be exceeded and periods when the target will
not be met.
To-date 20 households have been housed under HAP by the City Council and the process of
transferring all long term recipients of rent supplement over to HAP has now commenced. The
commencement of larger scale Rent Supplement transfers will lay the foundations for achieving the
overall target and the continued success of the HAP scheme. The effectiveness of this programme
will be monitored on an ongoing basis.
HAP Authorities will be provided with a HAP administrative payment of €150 per HAP household on
the basis of the cumulative number of households (including closed tenancies) supported through
HAP in the LA area. Local authorities will be able to make claims for HAP administration costs at two
points in 2016, at mid-year and at end of 2016.
Ms. Mitchell referred to difficulties access private rented housing units in the city and stated that
HAP is a valid housing option.
At 7.45 pm Deputy Mayor Cllr. N. McNelis assumed the Chair until the return of the Mayor at 8.00
pm.
