ITEM NO. 1 
 
Section 140 motion, Local Government Act 2001, as amended, in the names of Cllr. G. 
O’Neill and Cllr. J. Behan seeking to withdraw the Blessington Greenway proposals from 
consideration by An Bord Pleanala 
 
Date: 25th February 2022 
To: Brian Gleeson, Chief Executive, Wicklow County Council 
In accordance with Section 140 of the Local Government Act, 2001, as amended by the Local 
Government Reform Act 2014, we, the undersigned Members of Wicklow County Council, 
direct the Chief Executive of Wicklow County Council to withdraw the Blessington Greenway 
proposals from consideration by An Bord Pleanala. 
This will allow for the removal of the elements of the Greenway Plan which will cause serious 
negative impacts on the lives of the people living in the areas surrounding the Blessington 
Lakes including the installation of traffic lights on Knockeiran, Baltyboys and Valleymount 
Bridges and the proposed new parking restrictions on Blessington Main Street which are 
widely opposed by the local community. 
The reason for the motion is the lack of meaningful consultation with Elected Members of 
Wicklow County Council and the general public before the matter was referred to An Bord 
Pleanala. 
We further direct that the matter be placed before the Members of Wicklow County Council 
at the forthcoming Council Meeting to be held at 2.00 p.m. on Monday 7th March 2022 in 
the Chamber of Wicklow County Council, County Buildings, Wicklow. 
Cllr. Gerry O'Neill 
Cllr. Joe Behan 
Ms. M. Hartnett, Senior Executive Engineer gave a presentation outlining the following: 
 
Blessington eGreenway – Project Description 
 
 Community led project with the section done already carried (6.5 km) out by the 
local community  

 
 The scheme is proposed to provide a predominately off-road shared use path for 
pedestrians and cyclists.  
 
 The scheme will cover approximately 33km and involve the provision and upgrading 
of a greenway adjacent to the shoreline of the Blessington Lake Special Protection 
Area (SPA)  
 
 Traffic lights are proposed at the three existing bridge crossings to manage a new 
shuttle system for vehicular traffic. 
 
Blessington eGreenway – Status 
 
 In July 2017, Wicklow County Council obtained Part 8 approval to construct a 
Greenway around the lakes. 
 
 The scope of works for the project triggered the need to review the environmental 
reports undertaken to date, which has led to the project requiring a Natura Impact 
Statement (NIS) and a planning submission to An Bord Pleanala.  
 
 Documents were lodged with An Bord Pleanala with public consultation commencing 
on the 20th of January 2022. 
 
Bridge Options 
 
 Option 1 – Shared footway and cycleway with 1 lane of carriageway.  
 
 Option 2- Segregated footway and cycleway with 1 lane of carriageway.  
 
 Option 3 – Extend out to accommodate a segregated footway, cycleway and 2 lanes 
of carriageway 
 
 Option 4 – Do nothing. Cyclists to be accommodated on the existing bridge layout. 
 
Other options considered 
 
Provide underpass at Knockieran Bridge - the installation of underpasses at the approach to 
the bridges to remove the crossing points will not change the situation. Lights will still be 
needed to be installed to control the traffic. It is envisaged that there would be a significant 
land take required to facilitate the greenway to access these under passes and this would 
need environmental assessments. 
 
Option 2 Traffic lights 
 
 In order to provide this infrastructure, the carriageway needs to be narrowed to a 
single traffic lane to be used in turn from each direction to cross the bridge. 
 

 In order to control when traffic can cross without conflicting with opposing traffic, 
traffic lights needed 24hours/7 days a week. 
 
 A cycle time of less than 90 seconds (i.e.  a driver will have to wait 90 second before 
getting a green light again). 
 
 Based on the current assessment it is anticipated that on average there will be 3 
vehicles waiting at the traffic lights at any one time. 
 
 The expert modelling results show that there are no capacity concerns with the 
introduction of this shuttle working on the bridges. 
 
 Just to note that the new traffic signals could operate using sensors to detect traffic 
(rather than timers) in order to ensure that waiting times are minimised i.e. when a 
vehicle arrives at the bridge, a sensor will detect it and cycle the lights to green to 
allow traffic to pass over the bridge. 
 
Safety 
 
 Safety for all road users - cyclists, pedestrians, people with young children, older 
visitors, people with disabilities.  
 
 Fáilte Ireland - ‘Segregation away from vehicular traffic is a priority for users. 
Segregation is one of Fáilte Ireland’s Goals for greenways - and is one of the 5 ’s’  - 
scenic, sustainable, lots to see and do, strategic, segregation. 
 
 
Potential consequences if the scheme does not proceed 
 
 No options at this moment in time. 
 
 Loss of €15 million , by not including the bridges and the traffic lights, the greenway 
would not be classed as a Greenway anymore and this would risk the TII funding  
 
 Loss of €1 million, Risk to Blessington RRDF as it is dependent on the greenway 
application included a “becoming Greenway ready” initiative. Funding received in     
the sum of over €1 million. 
 
 Loss of tourism and economic opportunity presented by the greenway   
 
 Detrimental effect to the Council’s reputation with the Department.  
 
 Risk to Department’s confidence in the Council’s ability to deliver a major greenway 
scheme and this could impact on all schemes in the County.  
 
 Expenditure to date in the region of €1 million, the department could seek monies 
claimed to be returned. 

 
Impact on local community 
 
 It is acknowledged that the lights will result in a short delay to the local community 
crossing the bridges on their daily commutes. 
 
 The scheme is hugely beneficial to the local community and the county at large on 
many levels including economic, health and welfare and improvement to the 
connectivity between the surrounding villages and town. 
 
 In terms of public consultation, this has continued with the An Bord Pleanala formal 
process which commenced 20th January 2022 and closed 4th March 2022. The 
submissions made will be received over the next coming weeks from An Bord 
Pleanala and will be available for public viewing. They can be discussed with the 
Project Team if required. 
 
 Moving forward, it is proposed to form a new Project Liaison Group. This group will 
assist with information sharing and engagement with all stakeholders in the local 
area such as:   
 
o Councillors  
o IFA  
o Local business Representatives  
o Wicklow County Council staff 
o Local Community Groups 
 
eGreenway Facts 
 This €15 million investment in the new greenway - with more to follow - will give a 
huge boost to the economy of Blessington and the villages around the lakes and 
throughout West Wicklow.  
 An economic study on the experience elsewhere shows that greenways bring 
significant opportunities for existing businesses to expand and for the creation of 
new ones. This will mean more sustainable jobs for local families throughout the 
lakeside area. 
 First funded major greenway loop in Ireland  
 This development has been over 10 years in the making and is a prime example of a 
community-led, ‘ground-up’ initiative. 
 It aims to enhance health, social/community interaction and mental well-being. 
 It has been designed to the highest safety and universally accessible standards. 
 In full compliance with all ecological, cultural and heritage requirements 
 Traffic lights with modern sensor systems will be installed on 3 main bridges to 
provide safe crossing for both greenway users and all types of road vehicles. Stop on 
red for vehicles will be no longer than 90 seconds. Detailed studies carried out by 
traffic experts show that these will not cause congestion. 
 Apart from the bridges, virtually the entire route is off-road and substantially on ESB-
owned land adjacent to the lakeshore. 

 Since the existing 6.5kms greenway - created by Blessington & District Community 
Forum - was opened over 8 years ago, it has proven to be successful with both users 
and local landowners. 
 A new greenway visitor hub will be created in Blessington at the former Health 
Board building on the Manor Kilbride road. 
 
Ms. H. Purcell, Meetings Administrator read the email received by Cllr. G. O’Neill from An 
Bord Pleanala. 
‘Further to your email below to my colleague Shaun McGee the Board has asked me to 
advise you it is open to Wicklow County Council at any stage of the application process to 
withdraw the application.  
  
To do this Wicklow County Council must send a letter to the Board stating they are 
unconditionally withdrawing the application’. 
 
The Chief Executive advised the Elected Members that there are three legal opinions on the 
matter, two Senior Counsels opinions and that of the Law Agent.  All three legal opinions 
concur that the resolution, Section 140 motion is void and the Chief Executive cannot 
lawfully be directed to withdraw this application.  In addition, the Council is under a 
mandatory obligation to lodge a Section 177 application to An Bord Pleanála and no legal 
and statutory provision is in place to withdraw an application. The Chief Executive said he 
was aware of the email circulated by Cllr. G. O’Neill from An Bord Pleanála confirming that 
the application could be withdrawn but this does not confer on the Council any right to do 
so.  Any attempt to withdraw the application would leave the Council exposed to a potential 
judicial review going forward. 
 
A broad discussion followed and it was agreed to vote on the motion proposed by Cllr. G. 
O’Neill, seconded by Cllr. J. Behan seeking to withdraw the Blessington Greenway proposals 
from consideration by An Bord Pleanala.  
 
A vote was taken and following a roll call was defeated by a margin of 5 votes for, 5 against,
