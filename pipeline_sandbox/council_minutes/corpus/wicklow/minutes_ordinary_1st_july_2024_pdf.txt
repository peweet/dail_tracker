Request for Suspension of Standing Orders:- The Meetings Administrator advised that a 
request for suspension of standing orders had been submitted in advance of the meeting.   
In accordance with the protocol adopted the request was read to the meeting as proposed 
and seconded:- 
 
1. Cllr. J. Behan, seconded by Cllr. P. Leonard and supported by Cllr. T. Fortune requested 
a suspension of standing orders to discuss the following motion:  
 
“That this Council requests the Chief Executive to contact the Taoiseach, Simon Harris TD, 
and the Minister for Health, Stephen Donnelly TD, to seek Government support for Wicklow 
County Council to purchase 400 acres of land at Old Fassaroe , Bray.  

  
This land is currently for sale and is zoned for mixed use development, including residential, 
education, recreation and employment and has the capacity to provide thousands of 
affordable, social and private homes as well as many other essential facilities.  
  
It is also the original location for an extension of the Green Luas line and has the capacity to 
provide a park and ride facility which would relieve some of the traffic delays on the N11 
currently being experienced by thousands of commuters form Greystones, Wicklow and 
Arklow Municipal Districts.  
  
This Council believes that this is a once in a lifetime opportunity for a publicly led initiative to 
design and develop the newest town in County Wicklow which has the capacity to make life 
better for countless thousands of residents from all over County Wicklow.” 
 
 
It was agreed by all to have a discussion on the topic at 4.30 p.m.

ITEM NO. 5 
 
To consider and adopt the following Resolution 
 
“That the Chief Executive has the express authorisation of the elected council within the 
meaning of section 153(2) of the Local Government Act 2001 as amended, to act in any 
action and/or other proceeding, now in existence and/or in the future, relating to the 
exercise or performance by the local authority of a reserved function, including but not 
limited to the exercise of reserved functions in the context of a development plan. Such 
authorisation is given to do all such acts, matters, and things as the Chief Executive considers 
necessary and/or appropriate. The Chief Executive shall, inform the elected members of any 
future action or other proceeding, within the scope of this resolution at the first Monthly 
meeting of the elected members, after any such action or proceedings are served on the 
Council (where practicable or if not practicable, at the next appropriate meeting 
thereafter)”. 
 

A broad discussion followed.  Some concerns raised by the Elected Members were: 
 
 Handing over to the Chief Executive the authorisation to respond to any legal action 
in the future by a third party with regard to reserved functions. 
 Key role of public representatives is to represent the public, have oversight and 
accountability which is not happening. 
 Being asked to relinquish a power that a previous legislature in 2001 gave to Elected 
Members. 
 Request to seek second legal opinion. 
 Right as an Elected Member to be consulted in respect of legal action regarding a 
decision made as a public representative. 
 County Development Plan – Elected Members have the right to decide how the 
county is going to be developed. 
 Request to raise the issue with AILG. 
 Eradication of powers of the Elected Members. 
 Chief Executive protecting the Elected Members by getting resolution put in place. 
 Concern that decision is being made too quickly – is there enough information to 
make an informed decision? 
 The Act is enough for the Chief Executive to defend the Elected Members. 
 List of live cases should be brought before the Council where express authorisation 
would be sought on a case-by-case basis. 
 What is the number of cases that are live at present? 
 Dissolution of powers – meant to strengthen powers of the Elected Members. 
 Request to be kept informed at all stages and have a veto on any particular direction 
the Chief Executive proposes. 
 What are the implications for past cases? 
 What are the risks factors if resolution is rejected? 
 The Cairn Homes case before court at the moment which disputes the population 
targets set out in the County Development Plan. 
 The wish that the Chief Executive defend the decision of the Elected Members in the 
County Development Plan.  
 
The following motions were proposed: 
 
1. Proposed by Cllr. J. Snell, seconded by Cllr. T. Fortune ‘that a copy of legal opinion be 
circulated, second legal opinion to be sought by the Chief Executive, AILG 
representatives to seek AILG opinion and LAMA representatives to seek LAMA opinion’. 
 
2. Proposed by Cllr. S. Bourke, seconded by Cllr. L. Fenelon Gaskin to insert the wording 
‘"that such authorisation given to do acts, matters and things as the chief executive 
considers necessary and/or appropriate in consultation with the Members" before the 
wording ‘the Chief Executive shall inform the Elected Members’. 
 
 

3. Proposed by Cllr. M. Duddy, seconded by Cllr. T. Fortune ‘that a list of existing live 
cases in which the Council are involved be brought before the Council to grant express 
authorisation to those proceedings rather than the adoption of a global-style 
resolution’. 
 
4. Proposed by Cllr. P. Glennon, seconded by Cllr. P. Stapleton to delete the words ‘but 
not limited to’. 
 
5. Proposed by Cllr. T. Fortune, seconded by Cllr. O. Finn to defer this item to a later date. 
 
Following further discussion, the Elected Members agreed to withdraw all five motions and 
it was agreed by all to move to Item No. 6 and return to this item later in the meeting, in 
order to allow the Chief Executive to revert with a re-worded resolution that deals 
specifically with the Cairn case.
