ITEM NO. 8 
 
To consider and adopt the draft Wicklow County Council Annual Service Delivery Plan 
2023. 
 
Ms. H. Purcell, Meetings Administrator advised the Elected Members that the purpose of 
the Annual Service Delivery Plan is to set out the objectives and the performance standards 
for the Council’s service delivery areas for 2023.  
 
Appendix 1 of the plan details the annual performance indicators which are monitored by 
the National Oversight and Audit Committee (NOAC) and appendix 2 sets out the key 
baseline data target outputs for 2022.  Attention was drawn to errors on page 61 relating to 
Goal TWES 3 Roads; 
 Number of safety measures at schools p/a 2023 target is 3 and; 
 Number of low cost safety improvement target is 10, not 123. 
 
The Elected Members requested a presentation from each Director be given outlining 
details of the plan for their directorate.   
 
It was proposed by Cllr. P. O’Brien, seconded by Cllr. P. Fitzgerald and agreed by all to adopt 
the draft Wicklow County Council Annual Service Delivery Plan 2023 subject to the 
amendment of figures on page 61. Ms. Gallagher advised the Elected Members that their 

proposal would be taken on board and discussed at the next Senior Management Team 
meeting.

Suspension of Standing Orders 
 
Cllr. D. O’Brien, seconded by Cllr. G. McManus requested a suspension of standing orders to 
discuss and receive updates from the Chief Executive and appropriate Directorates about 
the lifting of the Eviction Ban and the state of readiness in Wicklow County Council to 
respond to NTQs (Notices to Quit).   
 
A broad discussion followed.  The Elected Members highlighted the following: 
 
 The hard work and commitment of the Housing & Corporate Estate Director of 
Service and his staff. 
 Collective approach and responsibility to this matter. 
 Wicklow are the best performing county in terms of housing supply in the country. 
 Supply of housing key. 
 Housing is a collective issue. 
 Multi-strand approach required. 
 
Mr. J. Lane, Director of Services responded by stating that Wicklow County Council had 74 
NTQs at the start of the year with a further 3 in the last week, 70 new H.A.P. applications 
have been received.  A multi-team approach is being taken involving teams from the 
Homeless Unit, HAP and Allocations.  The purchase in lieu target is 30, currently 6 are 
agreed, 5 in progress, queries have been submitted on 14 and 18 will not be pursued.  
Wicklow’s target for 2023 is 226 units.  380 units will be delivered with an expression of 
interest option on further units.  Wicklow County Council is currently pursuing negotiations 
with key stakeholders on the supply of housing units, some of which can be earmarked for 
NTQs (Notices to Quit)
