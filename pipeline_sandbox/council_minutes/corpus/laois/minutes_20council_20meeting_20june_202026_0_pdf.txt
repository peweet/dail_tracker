88. 
PART VIII OF THE PLANNING & DEVELOPMENT REGULATIONS 2001 (AS 
AMENDED) IN RELATION TO THE PROPOSED DEVELOPMENT OF THE 
FORT MUSEUM & EVENT CENTRE  
 
The Members were circulated with the planning report on the proposed development of the 
Fort Museum & Event Centre.  It was noted that the site is located within Portlaoise Town at 
the corner of Millbrook and Church Avenue and is surrounded by a mix of commercial and 
residential properties.  Mr. Adrian Barrett, Senior Engineer delivered a presentation to the 
Members on the proposed development.  On the proposition of Councillor Caroline Dwane 
Stanley seconded by Councillor Marie Tuohy, the members agreed to the proposed 
development as outlined. 
 
89. 
PART VIII OF THE PLANNING & DEVELOPMENT 2001 (AS AMENDED) IN 
RELATION TO THE PROPOSED DEVELOPMENT OF NEW (NON-PUBLIC) 
ENTRANCE TO TIMAHOE CEMETERY  
 
The Members were circulated with the planning report on the proposed development of the 
new (non-public).  It was noted that the proposed development will involve the acquisition of 
land situated to the north of the existing cemetery.  This acquisition is for the purpose of 
establishing a new, non-public entrance onto the R426 which will afford access to the rear of 
the cemetery for approved contractors and Laois County Council personnel.  The scope of the 

 
works also includes the replacement of the current boundary wall, railings and entrance, in 
addition to necessary drainage and all associated site development works.  On the proposition 
of Councillor Paschal McEvoy seconded by Councillor Elva Kelly, the members agreed to the 
proposed development as outlined.

98. 
NOTICE OF MOTIONS  
Notice of Motion No: 30/2026 
 
Councillor James Kelly proposed the following Notie of Motion:- 
 
“That Laois County Council call on the Tánaiste and Minister for Finance, Mr. Simon 
Harris to include Laois towns and villages in “The Living City Initiative” tax relief scheme 
which is aimed at tackling vacancy and dereliction and that this Council submit maps to 
the Minister highlighting the Counties central urban areas in need of regeneration” 
This Notice of Motion was seconded by Councillor Aisling Moran.  The Members were advised 
that correspondence would issue to the Tánaiste and the Minister for Finance, Mr. Simon 
Harris in this regard. 
 
Notice of Motion No: 31/2026 
Councillor Tommy Mulligan proposed the following Notice of Motion:- 
"That Laois County Council requests that the Minister for Education, Ms. Hildegarde 
Naughton, T.D. and the Department of Education urgently assess the need for an 
additional special school in County Laois and commence planning for such a school as a 
matter of priority" 
This Notice of Motion was seconded by Councillor Marie Tuohy.  The Members were advised 
that correspondence would issue to the Department of Education in this regard. 
Notice of Motion No: 32/2026 
Councillor Tommy Mulligan proposed the following Notice of Motion:- 
"That Laois County Council calls upon the Departments of Transport and Finance to 
review the TaxSaver Commuter Ticket Scheme to ensure it better supports workers who 
commute to Dublin on a part-time or hybrid-working basis" 

 
This Notice of Motion was seconded by Councillor Caroline Dwane Stanley.  It was agreed 
that correspondence would issue to the Department of Transport & the Department of 
Finance in this regard.
