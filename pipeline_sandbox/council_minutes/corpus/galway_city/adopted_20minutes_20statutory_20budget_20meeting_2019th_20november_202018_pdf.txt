The Meeting commenced with a statement of intention to conduct the business of the Meeting
in line with Standing Orders followed by a moment of silent reflection. Mayor McNelis
welcomed all to the Meeting, including newly appointed Director of Services, Ms. R. McNally.
Ms N. Moggan, A/ Head of Finance, referred Councillors to the draft Budget 2019 Report, issued
in advance of the Meeting, and presented an overview of the process and the significant issues
for consideration.
Councillors were advised that the draft budget was for €87.7million, an increase on the adopted
budget for 2018, and a proposed increase of €9.6 million on the 2018 budgeted figure.
Consultations with members of the Corporate Policy Group on the preparation of the Draft
Budget, in accordance with the provisions of the Local Government Acts, took place on the 7th
September, 22nd October and the 8th November 2018.
The proposed Draft Budget for 2019 was compiled on the basis of no proposed increases in
Rates or Local Property Tax. Ms. N. Moggan outlined the sources of Revenue Income, including
Commercial Rates, LPT, Goods and Services, and Grants and Subsidies.
Ms. N. Moggan thanked the Councillors for their prudence in previous budgets, which had built
up the provision on rates. The boiler replacement programme now has provision up to 2021 set
aside, through part of the costs being recouped through a retrofitting programme based on a
Circular from the Department.

Ms. Moggan advised Councillors that decisions were now required on the determination of
percentage of refund to apply in respect of commercial rates on vacant properties for 2019,
Adoption of Annual Budget 2019, the determination of Annual Rate on Valuation to be levied
for 2019, and approval of Rates Payment Incentive Scheme for 2019.

Cllr. T O'Flaherty requested to submit a Motion that LIS Funding could be carried over into the
next year. This was seconded by Cllr. P. Conneely.
Ms. N. Moggan, Acting Head of Finance, indicated that spend on LIS must be incurred in the
year, as only Capital items are allowed to be carried over. This is in accordance with Local
Government funding rules, as set out by the Department. LIS would qualify as minor works, so
could not be considered as a Capital expenditure.
On this basis, Cllr T. O'Flaherty withdrew the Motion.
A discussion on LIS and expenditure over the year followed. Mr. U. Finn, Senior Engineer,
indicted that approximately 80% of the fund was spent as of the current date, on items
including footpaths and bike racks.
