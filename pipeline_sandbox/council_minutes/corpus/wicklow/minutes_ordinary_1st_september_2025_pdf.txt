Request for Suspension of Standing Orders:- The Meetings Administrator advised that a 
request for suspension of standing orders had been submitted in advance of the meeting.   
In accordance with the protocol adopted the request was read to the meeting as proposed 
and seconded:- 
 
1. Cllr. J. Behan, seconded by Cllr. P. Leonard requested a suspension of standing orders 
to discuss the following motion:  
 
‘That this Council is deeply concerned at the impact of the current industrial action by school 
secretaries and caretakers in our schools across the county. 
 
Furthermore, this Council wishes to express its total support for the secretaries and 
caretakers in their legitimate request for parity with other similar school employees 
regarding pension entitlement, critical illness and bereavement leave. 
 
The Council agrees to write to the Taoiseach, Tánaiste, Minister for Education, and the 
Minister for Public Expenditure requesting that this issue be resolved by the Government as a 
matter of urgency’. 
 
It was agreed by all to have a discussion on the topic at 4.30 p.m.

ITEM NO. 16 
 
To receive a presentation: The East Coast Railway Infrastructure Protection Project 
(ECRIPP) Public Consultation 2. 
 
Mr. C. Lynch, Iarnród Éireann and Mr. D. Keneghan, Senior Associate Director, Jacobs gave a 
presentation outlining the following: 
 
 5 key locations identified and assessed as particularly exposed to coastal erosion and 
climate change effects. 
 Project objectives. 
 Project benefits. 
 Preliminary design – Bray Head to Greystones North Beach. 
 Preferred scheme – Bray Head to Greystones North Beach. 
 Preliminary design – Kilcoole to Newcastle. 
 Preferred scheme – Kilcoole to Newcastle. 
 Newcastle to Wicklow Murrough. 
 Preferred scheme – Newcastle to Wicklow Murrough. 
 Programme. 
 
There followed a broad discussion in which the Elected Members raised queries and 
concerns which were responded to by Mr. Keneghan and Mr. Lynch. Some of the queries 
raised were as follows: 
 
 Concerns around timeline of project. 
 Coastal erosion on the North Beach, Greystones.
