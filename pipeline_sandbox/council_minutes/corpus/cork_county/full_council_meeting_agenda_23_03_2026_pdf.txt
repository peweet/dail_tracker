13. CLLR FINBARR HARRINGTON & CATHAL RASMUSSEN 
“That Cork County Council write to Alan Kelly TD, Chair of the Oireachtas Joint Committee 
on Environment and Climate Action and Communications, requesting that the Committee 
invite the Chief Executive of An Post and the Minister for Communications, Patrick 
O'Donovan, TD to appear before it to address the current situation in An Post sorting offices, 
which is reportedly leading to significant delays in the delivery of post and, in some cases, 
items going missing. Delays in An Post deliveries can disrupt business operations by slowing 
down the receipt of payments, contracts, and important documents, which may impact cash 
flow, customer satisfaction, and overall efficiency. These ongoing delays are also placing 
undue pressure on post men, post women and post office staff who are on the frontline and are 
often receiving the brunt of public frustration despite not being responsible for the issues. The 
Council therefore asks that the Committee seek clarity on the causes of the delays and what 
measures will be taken to restore a reliable and timely postal service.” 
         
           [09-03-2026]
