21.07.2025.02 To consider in accordance with Section 13 of the Planning and 
Development Act 2000 (as amended). 
 
a. Chief Executive’s Report on submissions/observations received in respect of 
the Material Alterations on proposed Variation No 1. (Tralee Municipal District 
Settlements’ Plan) of the County Development Plan 2022-2028. 
 
Mr. D. Ginty Senior Planner briefed the meeting on the Chief Executive report. He 
stated that the report set out the proposed variation of the County Development Plan 
to replace the Tralee Town Development plan as contained in the County 
Development Plan.  A Special Meeting was held on 23rd May to consider the 
submissions received during the consultation period of between 20th February 2025 
and 21st March 2025.  
 
b. The recommendations of the Committee established under Section 51 (1)(a) of 
the Local Government Act 2001 for this purpose. 
 
Mr. D. Ginty set out the recommendations of the Committee established under Section 
51 (1)(a) of the Local Government Act 2001 as follows. The Proposed Material 
Alterations to Proposed Variation No. 1 of the Kerry County Development Plan 2022-
2028 were placed on display from Thursday 5th June until Friday 4th July 2025 (both 
days inclusive) and submissions/observations were invited during this period.
