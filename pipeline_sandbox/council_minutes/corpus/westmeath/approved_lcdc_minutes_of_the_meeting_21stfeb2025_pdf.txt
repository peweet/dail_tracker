Ms Jacqueline Finney, acting as Chairperson of Westmeath LCDC opened the meeting and thanked members
in attendance. Barry Kehoe was not present at the beginning of the meeting.
1. Minutes of the meeting 6th December 2024
On the proposal of Tom Farrell and seconded by Alfie Devine, the minutes of the LCDC meeting held on
6th December 2024 were approved.
2. Matters Arising
None
3. SICAP
a) End of Year Annual Performance Review 2024
Annette Barr Jordan presented the SICAP LCDC Guidance Annual Performance Review 2024 carried out
through the LCDC SICAP subcommittee. All the relevant documents have been uploaded onto IRIS,
budget parameters have been adhered to with an underspend of €31,298. VAT for 2024 will be paid at
the end of 2025. KPI's have been exceeded. The additional target groups identified will be added to 2025
annual plan. There are ongoing challenges to establish a Traveller network in Westmeath — however it is
believed this will be accomplished by Q 3 - Q 4. All data follow ups have been dealt with. The LCDC SICAP
subcommittee review commented "Case Study was excellent, it was received well by all LCDC Members,
Age Friendly Groups and Westmeath Older Persons Council." Annette advised Pobal provided extremely
positive feedback, all of which could not be included in this review. Annette and Jacqueline thanked the
subcommittee for its work. Based on the outcomes of the annual performance review and Pobal feedback
it was recommended that the 2024 annual performance review be adopted by the LCDC.
Proposed by Cllr. Bill Collentine and seconded by Anne Drury.
b) Annual Plan Review 2025
Annette Barr Jordan presented the SICAP Annual Plan Review 2025. All items have been uploaded onto
IRIS except CVs for new staff members. The projected financial checks have been reviewed. Targets and
goals for 2025 have been identified. Delvin, Rathowen and Moate have been brought back into SICAP as
new disadvantaged areas requiring more intensive work under Action 1.1. The LCDC sub-committee
monitors spending during the year and may allocate funding to another area. All actions outlined in the
proposed annual plan are in line with the LECP objectives and requirements on the ground. Annette and
Jacqueline thanked the subcommittee for its work.
Maria Fox requested a full list of towns and villages in SICAP. The Annual Plan 2025 was recommended
for LCDC approval.
Proposed by Teresa Kennedy and seconded by Joe McEvoy.
4. Slåintecare Healthy Communities
a) Westmeath Traveller Interagency Group —funding application
Brigid Geoghegan gave a presentation on the Westmeath Traveller Interagency Group (WTIG) funding
application. She outlined the objectives of W TIG which include inter-agency co-ordination, effective
engagement with Travellers and better communications. Brigid highlighted the lower the socioeconomic
position, the worse the health. The funding application aligned with goals and actions of 11 national and
local organisations including SICAP. Areas that will be addressed are education/training, employment and
2
wellbeing. The presentation outlined the educational and employment challenges facing Travellers and
the significant negative impact on health and wellbeing. In a highly competitive funding process,
Westmeath is one of 5 successful applicants approved for €142,000 from the Department of Health for a
2-year non-capital expenditure programme. As part of the programme Slåintecare Healthy Communities,
WCD and Youth Work Ireland Midlands will deliver tailored education programmes, employment
support, wellbeing programmes, advocacy and community engagement.
Jacqueline and Annette thanked and congratulated Brigid and all the organisations involved on this huge
achievement.
Joe McEvoy drew attention to fishing within the Roma and Ukrainian communities in Athlone. He
highlighted the need for more communication with the IFI and NCFFI on fishing rules and regulations and
potential funding for fishing tackle.
Jacqueline and Annette said this is something Westmeath Sports Partnership could look at.
Cllr. Tom Farrell suggested an Open Day with fishing organisations present, could be a way to open
communication channels to include marginalized communities and new communities.
Frank Murtagh advised that despite attempts, it is hard to engage with the small number of Roma families
in Athlone. Of the individuals engaging with WCD in 2024, 20% secured employment. This percentage is
the same for Traveller/Roma and overall figures.
Barry Kehoe joined the meeting in person.
