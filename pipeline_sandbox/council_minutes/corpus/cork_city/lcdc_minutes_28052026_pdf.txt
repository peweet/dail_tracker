LCDC LEP Grants 2026.
a.
Martin Davoren gave an update to the LCDC. The LCDC LEP Grants are under
review by the Department of Rural, Community Development and the Gaeltacht
(DRCDG). Upon approval of the grants, applicants will be notified in due course.
b. DRCDG/P0baUOECD event in Cork-EU Presidency Event, 30.11.2026.
Kieran O'Connell updated the LCDC on the DRCDG/Pobal/OECD event which will
be held on the 30th November 2026.
PPN Report.
Katherine Harford presented the PPN report to the LCDC. The LCDC members
shared their suggestions and recommendations including engaging in other
European cities projects.
Strategic Items:
a. SICAP + ECP Programmes — Cork City Partnership Report.
Cllr. Ken Collins presented the report to the LCDC and outlined the ongoing

Sléintecare Healthy Fund.
