Minutes of the Meeting of  
Westmeath Local Community Development Committee, (LCDC)  
held on Friday 1st Dec 2023 at 11:00 am  
in Mullingar, Council Chamber (Hybrid Meeting) 

2 
 
 
 
Hugh Farrell opened the meeting and gave a special welcome to Jackie Finney as acting Director of 
Services. He also acknowledged Mark Keaveney’ s work and contribution to the LCDC over his years as 
Director. Jackie Finney will be representing Pat Gallagher at this meeting.  
 
Hugh Farrell thanked everyone for their help during his time as chairperson of the LCDC and wished 
Margaret Kiernan all the best on her retirement from the LCDC. Anne Drury will be the new PPN 
Representative on the LCDC.  
 
1. Consider Draft Minutes of LCDC Meeting held on Friday 20th October 2023 
On the proposal of Margaret Kiernan and seconded by Teresa Kennedy. The LCDC minutes held 20th 
October 2023 has been approved. 
 
2. Matters Arising 
None. 
 
3. Election of a new Chairperson 
On the proposal of Cllr. Tom Farrell and seconded by Margaret Kiernan and agreed. Cathryn Whelehan 
was elected as the new Chairperson of Westmeath LCDC and assumed the Chair for the meeting.  
 
4. Election of Vice- Chairperson 
There were no nominations put forward for the position of Vice-Chairperson. Pat Murtagh suggested that 
this position continues to be held by the WCC Chief Executive.  This was proposed by Frank Murtagh and 
seconded by Cllr. Tom Farrell and agreed.  
 
5. LEADER 
 
6. Local Economic and Community Plan - Draft Consultation Report  
Mary Goldsberry gave the LCDC members a presentation on the LECP Draft Consultation Report.

Pat Murtagh gave the members an update on in the SICAP Tender Process. Letter of Offer has issued to 
Westmeath Community Development and currently at the stage of contract signing. Awaiting guidance 
from Pobal.

Our next LCDC Meeting will take place on Friday 16th Feb 2024 at 10am in the Council Chamber,
