The Meeting commenced with a statement of intention to conduct the business of the
Meeting in line with Standing Orders followed by a moment of silent reflection.

that recording of the meeting was not permitted. Discussion took place regarding
speaking time in the Chamber for this special meeting.

Seconded: P. Conneely

Seconded: D. Lyons

In Favour: 6 Against: 9 Present: 15

As the substantive motion was passed, suspension of standing orders came into effect to
allow five minutes speaking time.
1
Mayor McNelis, Cllr. P. Flannery & Mr. B. McGrath, C.E. all declared their interest as
Directors in the Galway 2020 company.
Ms. Aideen McGinley, Chair of 2020 gave a presentation to the Chamber. Ms. McGinley
spoke ofthe valued friendship with the Chief Executive / City Council. The Galway 2020
project is now at the stage of implementation and consists of a very strong voluntary
Board including 3 experts on Capitals of Culture. She spoke about 'making waves' and
engaging people at the highest level. Announcements are expected in Autumn 2018
about launching and delivering on the project. Ms. McGinley spoke of the tourism
benefits which will spread throughout Galway City and County and the growing legacy
Galway 2020 will have.
Ms. Hannah Kiely, C.E.O. Galway 2020 also gave a presentation to the Chamber. She
welcomed Mayor McNelis to his new position & thanked former Mayors P. Flannery & N.
Larkin for their previous work. Ms. Kiely spoke about the following in her presentation:
•
Delivery is on target.
16 members of staff at present, intention to move to 30-40 staff.
Acknowledged the work carried out by Mr. Chris Baldwin.
To date €3.2 million in funding has been applied for, the remainder of the funding
will deliver & mature.
Strong relationship with RTÉ, slots across RTÉ media - €100,000 paid for 30
months of advertising - In partnership with TG4 - Engaged with Galway
Advertiser & Connacht Tribune.
Rolling out SME package as per the bid in July. In August regional engagement
will be delivered.
Up to 90% of programmes are going ahead. Essential checks taking place until
end of July, then moving onto contract stage.
The announcement for the opening & closing ceremonies is expected in
September. Anticipated one ceremony for Galway City & one ceremony for
Galway County.
Small Towns Big Ideas project. 140 applications have been received, €20,000 per
project.
24 projects across 3 wards of the City. Ms. Liz Kelly has been appointed to deliver
these projects.
Ms. Sinead O Hara has been appointed for 2020 funding.
N.U.I.G has put 20 people in place to work with 2020.
The need for branding in the City - marketing in place - flags, banners etc will be
in place in July & August 2018.
Roadshow to take place in September giving a flavour of what the programme is
about.
Working on sponsorship programme.
The unique conference 'Galway 2020 - Creating the Legacy' taking place between
18th - June 2018 in Cluain Mhuire, G.M.I.T.
The need for hubs in Galway County.
Three to four thousand volunteers in place by October 2018, training to be
provided.
The need for everyone to pull together both locally & nationally.
2
Councilors welcomed & thanked both Ms. McGinley & Ms. Kiely for their presentation.
Concerns raised by Councillors include: staffing structure of Galway 2020, funding
provided by Galway County Council, lack of knowledge re Galway 2020 on the streets,
lack of advertising, negative publicity, requirement for programme of events to be
publicised, the need for regular updates from the Board, lack of detail re sponsorship,
include Residents Associations, fear of funding shortfalls, duty of care to rate payers,
requirement for openness & transparency.
Ms. McGinley & Ms. Kiely answered questions raised & clarified matters.
Mr. B. McGrath, C.E. reminded Councillors of the reason the City embarked on European
Capital of Culture 2020. He spoke of his confidence in the Board of Galway 2020 & his
satisfaction with protocols / procedures in place. He thanked the Board for the work
being carried out.
Mr. McGrath accepted that communications could be improved but believes in the
enormous amount of work being done in the background.
Proposed: Cllr. D. Lyons
Seconded: Cllr. D. McDonnell
Cllr. D. Lyons proposed an extension of the meeting until 8.45pm. This was seconded
by Cllr. D. McDonnell.
The Motion was agreed.
Councillors again thanked Ms. Kiely & Ms. McGinley while outlining their concerns.
