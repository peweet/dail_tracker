3. Rural Urban Speed Limit Review
Thomas Fitzpatrick (Executive Engineer Roads) outlined Phase 2 of Offaly County
Council's 2026 Speed Limit Review, following national direction to introduce 30 km/h
limits across built-up and urban areas. It summarised the background to the national
Speed Limit Review, explains the creation of Urban Speed Limit Zones, and sets out
Comhairle Chontae Vibh Fhaili
Offaly County Council
proposed speed limits for urban cores, residential streets, schools, and arterial routes.
Three towns—Birr, Tullamore and Edenderry—have identified urban cores where 30
km/h will apply, and a full list of schools in Edenderry MD is included for similar
reductions. The report also notes the required timeline, with bye-law adoption due by
October 2026 and implementation by March 2027.
John Mitchell (A/Senior Engineer Roads), Thomas Fitzpatrick (Executive Engineer
Roads) and Carol Maloney (Executive Technician) addressed all queries raised by the
Members.
4. Housing Update
John Cunningham (Senior Engineer Housing), and Pauline Hoctor (Senior Executive
Officer Housing), delivered a comprehensive update to the Members in relation to
Housing in the County. Their presentation covered the following key areas:
•
•
2025 Allocations
Social Housing Supports & Housing Stock
Homeless Services & Estate Management
Disability Strategy & Domestic Violence Supports
Housing Loans & Grants
Traveller Accommodation Programme 2025-2029
Tenant Purchase Scheme
Housing for All - New Builds & Turnkey Developments
Part V Delivery
Housing Pipeline
Energy Retrofit Programme
Private Rental Inspections
Mr. Cunningham and Ms. Hoctor outlined the housing activity for 2025 and early 2026,
including allocations across Local Authority, Approved Housing Bodies, and leasing
schemes; updates on homelessness prevention, estate management initiatives,
disability supports, domestic violence safe homes, and grant funding.
They also provided detailed information on new housing delivery under Housing for All,
including turnkey schemes, Part V units completed or under construction, and projected
pipeline developments across the Municipal District. Updates were also provided on the
Energy Retrofit Programme and the ongoing private rental inspection targets and
achievements.
Mr. Cunningham and Ms. Hoctor addressed all queries raised by the Members and were
thanked for their very detailed and informative report.

5. Regeneration/Project Updates
District Director Andrew Murray informed Members that the Edenderry Library Project
is currently at the tender assessment stage, with work ongoing to address tender
queries.
District Director Andrew Murray informed the Members that Stage Two of the design
tender for the Edenderry Swimming Pool Project has been published. He further advised
that he would present an indicative timeline to the Members at the next meeting.
The Members thanked District Director Andrew Murray for the update.
6. 2026 Roads Programme
District Engineer Rory Moore and Executive Engineer Enda Daly presented the Roads
Programme for 2026 to the Members stating that the grant allocation had been received
from the Dept. of Transport since our last meeting. The Members thanked him and
Executive Engineer Enda Daly for the work, time and effort they put in to putting the
programme together but stated that overall, the grant amount is not sufficient for the
roads in North Offaly. The Programme was proposed by Cllr. Eddie Fitzpatrick and
seconded by Cllr. Noel Cribbin.
