Part 1 "That Wicklow County Council introduce a "rent payment" system similar to that used in Wexford
County Council. This would enable all Council tenants to pay their rent in their local Post Offices throughout
the county. The benefits of which would be as follows:
-Tenants will benefit from being able to pay rent in the Post Office the same day as they collect social welfare
payments if applicable.
-Post offices will benefit from the extra revenue and therefore more job security for P.O. employees.
-Shops and small businesses adjacent to the Post offices will benefit from increased footfall.
-Wicklow County Council will benefit from less arrears and a decreased workload on municipal staff".
Part 2 "That Wicklow County Council support an awareness campaign to reverse the transfer of social
welfare payments from Post Offices to banks during the COVID-19 Emergency period. Apart from
supporting the restoration of lost revenue to Post Offices the weekly visit for many constituents to the local
Post Office plays an integral part in their overall wellness and connectivity to the widerconmunity as well as
an economic support to local Main Street businesses".

"That Wicklow County Council endorse the recently launched Youth Manifesto on Climate Justice. As the
first Council to declare a climate and biodiversity crisis and the first Council to include a young person on an
SPC we join such eminent figures as Chair of the Elders and former President Mary Robinson in the
endorsement of this important Manifesto which calls for Climate Justice through:
unity
Pointing fingers at each other will not solved the climate crisis. Whether we are young or old, rich or poor,
from urban or rural areas, climate change will impact us all. Standing in unity while recognising our different
realities is how we must tackle this challenge.
A Green Check
How society functions in any given democracy is dictated by the priorities of its government. Make climate
change our priority. Deliver radical, people centred policies that would suppott the transition we need.
A Just Transition
It is normal to fear the unknown. However, we cannot afford to continue, as if our house is not on fire. We
need to transition for the better of all in society and to reach those that are furthest behind in our society first.
A Social Justice lens
It is those who are least responsible for climate change that will suffers its gravest consequences. We
cannot solve the climate crisis by ignoring Inequality and Human Rights. So, let's build global empathy and
leave no one behind".

"That this Council calls for a full and independent review of the Bray Fire Service as a matter of urgency.
The rapidly changing landscape of the greater Bray area both in terms of
commercial and residential development requires a Fire Service that is functioning at an appropriate and
sustainable operational capacity.
Wicklow County Council should commission a report to re-examine the requirement for a Full-time Fire
setvice for Bray which gives consideration to both the needs of the community and the wellbeing of the Fire
Service Staff.
On the 1st of December 2014 a report was issued on the provision ofa Full-time Fire Service for Bray. This
report was based on a number of data sources, including population data from the 2011 census. Giving
consideration to the population growth in Bray, Enniskerry, Kilmacanogue and surrounding areas, the
increased development both in terms of residential and commercial units and the length of time since the last
assessment was carried out, an independent review of the current services and future needs are now
required to ensure adequate setvice provision can be planned. "
