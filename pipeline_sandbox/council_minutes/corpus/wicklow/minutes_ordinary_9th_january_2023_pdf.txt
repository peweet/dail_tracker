ITEM NO. 13 
 
To note the County Wicklow Age Friendly Strategy 2023 – 2027. 
 
Mr. M. Nicholson, Director of Services gave a short overview of County Wicklow Age 
Friendly Strategy 2023 – 2027 outlining the following: 
 
 The programme has been operating since 2008 
 It is affiliated to the World Health Organisation and every county has an agency 
friendly programme, an Age Friendly Strategy and an Age Friendly Co-Ordinator. 
 
 The key to success is active involvement of all key stakeholders, including Age 
Friendly Programme Manager, Local Authority interdepartmental group, and local 
age friendly alliances. 
 
 There are age friendly parking, libraries, housing specialists and benches. 
 
 There are age friendly considerations, walkability surveys, talent initiatives, bus 
shelters, housing units, dementia garden etc. 
 
 Senator Pat Casey is Wicklow Age Friendly Ambassador. 
 
 The new plan has 72 actions over eight thematic groups. 
 
 Recommendation that there should be an Elected Member on the Age Friendly 
Alliance. 
 
A short discussion followed, the Elected Members congratulated the Age Friendly Alliance 
and the Administrative Team on their many achievements.

To consider the Chief Executive’s Monthly Management Report, December 2022.

Suspension of Standing Orders 
 
1. Cllr. P. O’Brien, seconded by Cllr. G. Dunne requested a suspension of standing 
orders to discuss the evolving situation in Direct Provision at the Grand Hotel which 
has been deemed necessary because of the failure of the Minister and his 
Department to engage with the Public Representatives in the Wicklow Municipal 
District and the dangerous living conditions the residents find themselves in. 
 
Following a broad discussion on the matter it was agreed to write to the relevant Minister 
and to IPAS as a matter of urgency and request a meeting with relevant officials and a cross 
party delegation from Wicklow County Council to discuss the issue further. 
 
 
2. Cllr. G. O’Neill, seconded by Cllr. J. Behan requested a suspension of standing orders 
to discuss the provision of modular housing in the Baltinglass Municipal area. 
 
A short discussion took place in which the Elected Members expressed their concerns. 
 
The Chief Executive advised the Elected Members that a list of possible sites were submitted 
to the Department for consideration in April.  Sites selected were based on technical criteria 
as well as key requirements. The Government has appointed OPW as the contracting 
authority with responsibility for managing the construction and installation of modular units 
on a range of state-owned properties around the country. Two sites in County Wicklow 
remain under active consideration on the list, to date OPW has only requested access to 
carry out a survey for Baltinglass, some initial surveys were done in December, and it is 
expected a full site investigation will be carried out in January. A request to access the 
Dunlavin land, has not been received as yet. The site in Baltinglass is the only one surveyed 
at the moment. If the site is deemed suitable, the OPW will revert to the local authority in 
relation to proceeding with the developmental works. The Department of children will 
arrange for presentations to the relevant Elected Members in the district supported by the 
OPW technical team as part of our communications process. With regard to the emergency 
accommodation programme, the OPW will arrange a community information meeting and a 
leaflet drop in the relevant area.
