1 
Election of An Cathaoirleach 
The Cathaoirleach opened the meeting and noted that it had been her honour to serve the people of 
Meath as Cathaoirleach of Meath County Council and she acknowledged the support of her family and 
friends, as well as the Elected Members and Management and Staff of Meath County Council.  
There were expressions of gratitude to the out-going Cathaoirleach from Councillors Sarah Reilly, 
Gerry O’Connor, Ronan Moore, Wayne Harding, Nick Killian, Yemi Adenuga, Maria White, Joe Fox, 
Suzanne Jamal, Eugene Cassidy and Emer Tóibín.  All referenced the professionalism and dignity of 
the Cathaoirleach with many paying tributes to how the Cathaoirleach chaired the meetings and dealt 
with all issues in a fair and open manner. 
 
The Chief Executive thanked Councillor Tolan for her work and dedication during her time as 
Cathaoirleach.  He highlighted the professional and dignified manner in which she carried out her

Result: Cllr. Wayne Harding – 28, Cllr. Eddie Fennessy – 8,  Abstain – 2, Absent – 2  
 
Councillor Wayne Harding was declared elected as Cathaoirleach of Meath County Council and 
assumed the Chair. 
 
The newly elected Cathaoirleach addressed the meeting, thanked his proposer and seconder and paid 
tribute to the out-going Cathaoirleach.  He acknowledged the support of his family, including his wife 
Yvette, his two children and his parents and noted that he was looking forward to the year ahead.  He 
also paid tribute to the other candidate, Councillor Eddie Fennessy.  Looking to the year ahead, An 
Cathaoirleach identified a number of priorities including the delivery of the Slane By-Pass and the 
Boyne Greenway.  There were expressions of congratulations from Councillors Padraig Fitzsimons, 
Gerry O’Connor, Ronan Moore, Mike Bray, Paddy Meade, Joe Fox, Sarah Reilly, Yemi Adenuga, Nick 
Killian, Sharon Tolan, Maria White, Sean Drew, Suzanne Jamal, Emer Tóibín and Padraig Coffey.  The 
Chief Executive congratulated the new Cathaoirleach and expressed his commitment to working 
closely with him in the year ahead.
