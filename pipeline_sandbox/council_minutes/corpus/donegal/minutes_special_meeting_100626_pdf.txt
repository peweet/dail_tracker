C/183/26 
PRESENTATION FROM COMHAIRLE NA NÓG – DONEGAL YOUTH 
COUNCIL 
The Cathaoirleach welcomed Mr Michael Mc Devitt, Foróige, Ms 
Cassie Lamb, Mayor, Donegal Youth Council and Ms Margaret Byrne, 
National Youth Executive to the meeting. 
 
The presentation delivered by Ms Lamb and Ms Byrne, gave an 
overview of the work and ethos of the Youth Council. The Council, they  
confirmed provided a voice for the young people of Donegal at both 
local and national level. Detail was provided in relation to the various 
national consultations that the Youth Council were involved in and 
acknowledged their efforts in raising issues nationally. They alluded to 
the fact that had collaborated with Jigsaw Donegal on topics such as 
bullying and school stress and had participated in events such as the 

 
4 
 
“Females in Politics” forum and the Donegal PPN event and consulted 
with a range of other stakeholders in relation to issues affecting young 
people today. 
 
It was noted that the main issues affecting young people today were 
vaping, school/exam stress and bullying and that these and other 
topics of interest would be discussed by the Youth Council going 
forward.  
 
Members welcomed the excellent and informative presentation and 
proceeded to raise a number of queries which were responded to by 
Ms Lamb, Ms Byrne and Mr Mc Devitt. They advised of the following :- 
 
➢ That social media and the use of same was of major importance to 
young people and that this was something that would be continued 
during the term of the next Youth Council.  
➢ The CSPE course as part of the school curriculum and its attraction 
for students, it was confirmed, depended on the manner in which it 
was taught. Education with regard to the operation of the PR 
System was, they confirmed,  an element of this course and that the 
Youth Council elections provided a first-hand insight into the 
workings of the electoral system.  
➢ Access onto the N56 was something that could be possibly debated 
as part of the consideration on housing and transport issues by the 
incoming Youth Council. 
➢ It was important that young people were listened to and that the 
issues they raised were taken on board by the elected members.  
➢ More education on the political system was needed to encourage 
women into politics. 
➢ Existing supports provided by the HSE and in school settings could 
be built upon to assist young people living in defective concrete 
block affected homes.  
➢ Social media was a powerful and beneficial tool with many positive 
impacts. Noted need for caution and control so that the best results 
could be obtained. Education on the dangers deemed to be the way 
forward. 
➢ Possible age limit for access to mobile phones and other elements 
of social media should be 11-12 years when students are moving 
on to secondary education.  
➢ Issues such as housing & transport, status of services on our 
islands etc are important issues which can be looked at going 
forward. 
➢ Considerable acknowledgement presently in relation to the 
promotion of the Irish language and this was something that can be 
built on. 
➢ Bullying both online and in-person an issue which requires attention 
and ongoing monitoring. 
➢ AI a great power tool which can be used to initiate the thinking 
process and to stimulate debate and discussion.  

 
5 
 
➢ Issues with vaping/alcopops are related to peer pressure and 
aggressive marketing campaigns. These need to be tackled 
nationally and consideration given to banning specific flavours, and 
packaging. This to be further debated through the National 
Executive and maintained as a recurring agenda item for the Youth 
Council.  
 
The Cathaoirleach thanked the delegation for their input, and the 
informative presentation and acknowledged their excellent debating 
skills.
