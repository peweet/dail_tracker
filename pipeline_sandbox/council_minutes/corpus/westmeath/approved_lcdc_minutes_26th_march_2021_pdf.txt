Draft Minutes of the Meeting of  
Westmeath Local Community Development Committee, (LCDC) held 
 on Friday 26th March 2021 at 10.00am via teleconference.   

Hugh Farrell, Chairperson of Westmeath LCDC declared the meeting open and formally welcomed all 
LCDC members, Westmeath Community Development staff and the staff of Westmeath County Council. 
Apologies were noted from Seamus McDermott, Valerie Hand, Margaret Kiernan, Cllr. Vinny McCormack, 
Hazel Gavigan and Peter Ormond. Hugh Farrell, on behalf of the LCDC members, wished to pass on his 
sympathy to Margaret Kiernan on the recent passing of her sister.  
 
Hugh Farrell reminded members that if they have a conflict of interest in any of the projects to declare 
same and reminded them of the confidentiality of the items on today’s agenda. Hugh thanked all 
members for attending today’s meeting.  
 
1.  
Draft Minutes of LCDC Meeting held Friday 26th February 2021 
 
On the proposal of John Geoghegan and seconded by Cllr. Bill Collintine the minutes of the previous LCDC 
meeting held on Friday 26th February 2021 were approved.   
 
2.  
Matters Arising 
 
No matters arising on this occasion.  
 
 
 
3.  
COVID 19 
 
a) Approval of draft allocations of COVID 19 Emergency Funding.  
Documentation on the draft allocations were circulated to the LCDC members in advance of the meeting. 
Cllr. Tom Farrell discussed the outline of the funding scheme with the members. 164 applications were 
received under this round of funding with 162 of these applicants meeting the criteria. Each group who 
met the criteria received a minimum of €200 in funding.  It was agreed that the draft allocation of Covid 
19 Emergency funding be approved by the members.  
 
Approved: Proposed by Cllr. Tom Farrell and seconded by Frank Murtagh.   
 
 
4.  
Correspondence  
a) Climate Action & Covid Vaccine Partner 
Annette Barr Jordan informed members that she had received correspondence in relation to climate 
action and she also received information on the Covid Vaccine Partner.  All correspondence received in 
relation to these two issues will be forwarded to all members after this morning’s meeting.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

5.  
AOB  
  
Bernie Leavy informed the committee members that since our last meeting there has been a revised 
decommittal amount which needs to be noted by the members. 32LDRWTM107145. 
 
 
6.  
Date of Next Meeting 
The next meeting will be held on 28th May 2021 at 10am via teleconference.  
 
 
Hugh Farrell formally declared the meeting closed. He thanked everyone for their cooperation and 
patience during the teleconference and wished everyone well.
