At the commencement of the meeting a roll call was taken.  The Meetings Administrator 
advised of meetings etiquette for online meetings and that the press and some members of 
the public were also present. 
 
 
Votes of Sympathy 
 
Elected Members passed a vote of sympathy to the families of the late Ms. Alvina Kelly, Mr. 
Tom Phelan, Mr. Kevin Roberts, Ms. Carmel Robinson, Liam McCord, Eilis Bassett and Ms. 
Evelyn Goodman.  A minutes silence was observed for the deceased. 
 
Request for Suspension of Standing Orders:- The Meetings Administrator advised that a 
request for suspension of standing orders had been submitted in advance of the meeting.   
In accordance with the protocol adopted the request was read to the meeting as proposed 
and seconded:- 
 
 
1. Cllr. M. Kavanagh, seconded by Cllr. P. Leonard, supported by Cllr. L. Scott, Cllr. R. 
O’Connor, Cllr. S. Stokes, Cllr. T. Fortune, Cllr. M. Barry and Cllr. D. O’Brien requested a 
suspension of standing orders to discuss the following: 
 
‘That Wicklow County Council write to Minister McConalogue requesting him to contact 
all Dáil party leaders & MEPs to remind them of their own declarations on the Climate 
and Biodiversity Emergency and their commitment to youth and community groups, 
made in 2019, and more recently to the 2 Citizens Assemblies (adult & youth), 
regarding the restoration of nature as a matter of urgency. Furthermore, that Wicklow 
County Council writes to all party leaders urging them to work together with their MEPs 
to pass a strong EU Nature Restoration Law without delay. If passed, we request that 
Wicklow County Council forwards this motion to all City & Co Councils without delay’. 
 
It was agreed by all to have a discussion on the topic at 4.30 p.m. 
 
Congratulations were extended to the County Wicklow U14 Ladies Gaelic Football Team 
who won the All-Ireland final against Clare and to the County Wicklow Men’s Senior Hurling 
team who beat Donegal in the Nickey Rackard Cup.

 Core Policies.

 51 Key Deliverables.

Suspension of Standing Orders 
 
Cllr. M. Kavanagh, seconded by Cllr. P. Leonard, supported Cllr. L. Scott, Cllr. R. O’Connor, 
Cllr. S. Stokes, Cllr. T. Fortune, Cllr. M. Barry and Cllr. D. O’Brien requested a suspension of 
standing orders to discuss the following: 
 
‘That Wicklow County Council write to Minister McConalogue requesting him to contact 
all Dáil party leaders & MEPs to remind them of their own declarations on the Climate 
and Biodiversity Emergency and their commitment to youth and community groups, 
made in 2019, and more recently to the 2 Citizens Assemblies (adult & youth), 
regarding the restoration of nature as a matter of urgency. Furthermore, that Wicklow 
County Council writes to all party leaders urging them to work together with their MEPs 
to pass a strong EU Nature Restoration Law without delay. If passed, we request that 
Wicklow County Council forwards this motion to all City & Co Councils without delay’. 
 
A broad discussion followed. 
It was proposed by Cllr. M. Kavanagh, seconded by Cllr. P. Leonard to including the wording 
‘and that consultation takes place with farmers, foresters, community groups and other 
stakeholders to discuss any issues relevant to them’ and following a roll call the motion ‘That 
Wicklow County Council write to Minister McConalogue requesting him to contact all Dáil 
party leaders & MEPs to remind them of their own declarations on the Climate and 
Biodiversity Emergency and their commitment to youth and community groups, made in 
2019, and more recently to the 2 Citizens Assemblies (adult & youth), regarding the 
restoration of nature as a matter of urgency and that consultation takes place with farmers, 
foresters, community groups and other stakeholders to discuss any issues relevant to them. 
Furthermore, that Wicklow County Council writes to all party leaders urging them to work 
together with their MEPs to pass a strong EU Nature Restoration Law without delay. If 
passed, we request that Wicklow County Council forwards this motion to all City & Co 
Councils without delay’ was passed by a margin of 19 for, 3 against and 10 not present viz:-
