community wellbeing and social inclusion. 
 

1. Provide a minimum level of core funding of €250,000 per annum to all Family Resource 
Centres nationally, ensuring sustainable and equitable service delivery. 
2. Introduce dedicated capital and stand-alone funding streams to enable Family Resource 
Centres to secure, develop, or upgrade premises that are fit for purpose. 
3. Support and promote the integration and co-location of Family Resource Centres with other 
key community and statutory services, including Citizens Information, youth services, and 
family supports, to enhance accessibility and outcomes for service users.”
