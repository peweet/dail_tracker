3. Consideration of Reports and Recommendations 
 
3.1  
Municipal District Engineer’s Report 
 
Mr. Pat Harrington, Senior Executive Engineer presented his report to the 
Members: 
• The Members were informed that part of the construction at Phelim 
Street, Rathvilly would be delayed until at least after Easter, 2026 as 
Uisce Éireann would be carrying out extra works and which would impact 
the timeframe for completion. 
• The completion of lighting in Myshall and on Mill Street, Tullow will 
commence when the contractor is available. 
• The Members were requested to submit requests for D.I. projects for the 
coming year so that a list could be collated. 
• The Members were informed that following seventeen weeks of rain, that 
potholes and some road repairs could not be completed until roads had 
dried out. 
 
The following was also discussed: 
• The Members were informed there were five locations where roads had 
failed due to saturation.  Climate change and drainage have had an 
impact on ground saturation. 
• The repair of roads at Tinryland following the completion of works by 
Uisce Éireann was raised.  The Members were advised that the cost of 
these repairs would fall to the Local Authority under the Road Opening 
Licence granted to Uisce Éireann.  He informed the Members that the 
Road Patching Unit had been on site today as the weather was dry to 
carry out repairs. 
• The Members complimented Mr. Harrington on the five-year turnaround 
programme which had been implemented and had led to a robust 
network.   
• The Members were advised that a week would be set aside during the 
summer months to deal with signage. 
• The Members welcomed the pedestrian crossing in Rathvilly. 
• Mr. Harrington informed the Members that the footpath around the 
lighting posts on the Tullow Road in Rathvilly would be replaced. 
 
The Members thanked Mr. Harrington, Senior Executive Engineer and team for 
their work particularly due to the recent bad weather.

3.5  
Community Section Update 
 
 
Mr. Barry Knowles, Senior Executive Officer, provided an update to the 
Members on the work of the Community Section which included the following: 
 
• Community Support Centres – The Members were informed that in 
response to recent severe weather events that designated centres were 
being activated throughout the county.  In the event of a severe weather 

4 
 
event these centres would be activated and would provide, power, phone 
charging, hygiene facilities etc to the local community.  Emergency 
generators will be provided at these community centre locations and 
electrical works carried out to ensure that the community centre can 
accept the generator.  The procurement of emergency generators is due 
to be completed by the end of March 2026.  The work of Mr. Ben 
Woodhouse, A/Chief Fire Officer was at the commencement of this 
project before it was handed over to the Community Section was noted. 
The Members were informed that the generators would be stored by the 
Fire Service until they were required for use. 
A discussion followed on the selection of centres and the geographical 
spread.  The Members were informed that there would be three centres 
in each Municipal District. 
 
• Local Community Safety Partnership – The Members were informed that 
the Local Community Safety Partnership had been formed and had met.  
Ms. Laura Kelly was the Acting Co-ordinator and Ms. Donna Humphreys 
was the Assistant Staff Officer for the Partnership. 
 
• The Local Enhancement Programme 2026 – The Members were 
informed that the closing date for receipt of completed applications is 
27th February, 2026. A total of €156,072.58 in capital funding is 
available to support necessary repairs and improvements to facilities, 
energy-efficiency projects, the purchase of equipment, and similar 
works. This funding is divided with €46,821.77 (30%) ring-fenced for 
small-scale grants of €1,000 or less and €109,250.81 (70%) designated 
as targeted funding for disadvantaged areas as identified in the County 
Carlow Local Economic and Community Plan 2023–2029. 
 
• The members were advised that Active Carlow and Healthy Carlow will 
make presentations on their work to the Members at meetings later this 
year. 
 
The Members thanked Mr. Knowles for his update and staff in the 
Community Section for their work.
