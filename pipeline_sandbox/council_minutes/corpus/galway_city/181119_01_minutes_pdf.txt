Item 4 (a) Féilte Ireland — to receive an update on Galway City Council's
City Museum
Mr. B. Barrett reported on the proposed extension and works to Galway City Council's City Museum. The
Mayor invited clarifications and questions.
31 Page
Comments from Members included:
Timeline for completion, funding required for the project, entry fee charge, inclusion of Irish Language, Illegal
parking at Museum, studio space, linkage with third level institutions, opening hours, disability proofing &
use by school children.
Mr. B. Barrett responded comprehensively to Members' queries, and advised that work was due to
commence on trial holes shortly which would give them more detailed information & costings which would
allow them to be more specific in the tender documents. Mr Barrett gave an 18 month build estimate with
an opening date of QI in 2022. He advised that there were challenges to keep museum open while works
were being carried out. He also confirmed that it was a condition of the grant from Failte Ireland that a
charged would be introduced for all the Museum but they would be examining this in the context of school
children in the City being allowed entry free of charge.
The Chief Executive stated that this was a good news story for Galway, the total value of the grant from Failte
Ireland and City Council was in excess of €10m. He refuted the suggestion of delays to the project informing
Members that Procurement processes had to be followed and the conditioning of a grant from any agency
was normal practise. Mr McGrath also confirmed that the building would fully comply with Part M obligations
and the opening hours and staffing would eventually be increased.
The Mayor welcomed Senator John Dolan to the Council Chamber.
Cllr. M. Crowe proposed to proceed in principle with acceptance of the grant from Failte Ireland, with the

Item 4 (b) Salmon Weir Pedestrian & Cycle Bridge.
Mr R. Ryan from ARUP and S. Harrington from Harrington & Associates gave a presentation to Members on
the proposed new Salmon Weir Pedestrian & Cycle Bridge.
Questions and comments from Members included: The level of investment required, consideration of the
existing bridge, protection of fig tree, footfall, height & distance, disability access, Value for money & green
friendly bridge.
Mr. Barrett & Mr. Harrington responded comprehensively to Members queries including the cost of €7.2m,
the distance and proposed height of the preferred option. Mr. U. Finn informed Members that the option
chosen was based on the Galway Transport Strategy and that a new traffic bridge would require significantly
more funding. Mr. Finn also stated that the scheme was subject to approval from An Bord Pleanala.
