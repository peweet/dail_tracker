Cllr. D. McDonnell 
Referred to minutes and that no comment had been recorded on remarks

Replies to queries and requests for clarification were provided by the Chief Executive and Mr. T. 
Connell, Director of Services.  Mr. Connell provided updates on queries raised across his Directorate 
including that if returning emigrants qualified for Social Housing then there was various options 
open to them. He also stated that Social Inclusion Grants were a possible source of funding for Ala 
but that they had not applied for this grant.   Mr Connell highlighted that he was meeting with the 
Area Councillors and residents in relation to the plaque at Mervue and he would circulate 
correspondence following those meetings.  
 
The Chief Executive advised Councillors that his position on the matter of the social media post had 
not changed from his response at previous meetings.  Mr. McGrath also referred to Eyre Square and 
stated that improvements were being made.  Litter Bins had been painted since the last meeting.  He 
acknowledged there are issues but some of these are beyond the Council’s control like recruitment 
of staff.  There is a national moratorium on recruitment and the cost of employing additional 
headcount are prohibiting factors.

Cllr. P. Flannery 
Stated his dissatisfaction that no reference made in relation to the 
issues he raised. He asked who had the ultimate authority in relation 
to water safety. 
Cllr. N. McNelis 
Asked if the OPW, the Harbour Board and other relevant groups had 
been written to re Irish Water Safety motion. 
Cllr. C. Connolly 
Under matters of accuracy she wished to have it recorded that no 
funding had been received by GCC for Social Housing since 2009. 
Cllr. P.  Keane 
Asked if Galway City Council had written to the Credit Union seeking 
funding in relation to housing.  
Replies to queries and requests for clarification were provided by Chief Executive Mr. McGrath and 
Mr. T. Connell Director of Services.  Mr. Connell provided updates, including formally writing to Irish 
Water Safety to carry out a water safety audit. He also highlighted that GCC would be going to 
tender at the end of the month for Phase I of Ballymoneen Road Development. There is now a 
requirement to procure consultants and carry out a feasibility study in relation to  Phase II, focusing 
on layout and density. 
 
The Chief Executive advised Councillors that the Council is awaiting approval from Department of 
Environment, Community & Local Government prior to meeting with the Credit Union as any 
borrowing of funding required their approval in the first instance. He also stated that Social Housing 
funding was received as a package which included RAS, Long Term Leasing and Voluntary Housing. 
 
The above replies led to further discussion among Councillors including clarity on the number of 
units being built in the Ballymoneen Road project, timeframe involved, consultation with residents 
of Maigh Burca and the BER rating.  
 
Discussion also arose in relation to the raft at Salthill.  Cllr. P. Flannery queried whether the authority 
of Irish Water Safety (IWS) or Galway City Council’s Health & Safety Officer takes precedence.  The 
Chief Executive advised Councillors that IWS act in an advisory capacity and the final decision 
remains with the Council. The Council’s has a duty of care and there are health and safety issues 
particularly when the raft is not attended.    Further discussion took place before Cllr P. Flannery 
proposed that Irish Water Safety attend the next ordinary meeting of GCC. 
 
That Irish Water Safety attend and make a presentation at the next Ordinary Council Meeting
