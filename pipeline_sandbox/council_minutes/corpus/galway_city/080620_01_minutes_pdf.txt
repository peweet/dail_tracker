4. CONSIDERATION OF REPORTS OF OFFICIALS
4(a) Covid-19 - Report on the emergency response to Covid-19 covering the period 12th March 2020
to 7th June 2020
Mr. D. Mahon, A/Director of Services presented the report and provided an overview of the evolving
COVID 19 world pandemic and the major emergency and public health crisis that has arisen in Ireland.
He gave a high level overview of how Galway City Council (GCC) responded to the major emergency.
He advised the meeting in chronological sequence some of the key dates and events during the crisis,
which continues to this day including: the work of the Galway Senior Response Team; Government
announcements; emergency measures and legislation passed; HSE Guidance; Briefings held with
Councillors; Service updates on the work of GCC on key service provisions during the different phases;
advertisements; National Roadmap to Reopening Society and Business; remote working and ICT;
National Return to Work Safety Protocol; Galway City Council Local Roadmap for Restarting Economy
and Business and Health and Safety.
4(b) Covid-19 Community Response Forum
Mr. B. Barrett, A/Director of Service gave an update to the meeting regarding the work of the Covid-
19 Community Response Forum which is chaired by Mr. B. McGrath, Chief Executive, and was set up
and co-ordinated by Galway City Council in March. The membership includes over twenty agencies,
organisations and community and voluntary groups across Galway City which led the co-ordination of
COVID-19 community supports ensuring a co-ordinated community response in support of our
communities and particularly the most vulnerable members.
Mr. Barrett elaborated on the Community Call helpline and COVID-19 Community Response Map; Stay
at Home resources and the second phase of the Community Call campaign titled 'In This Together'
which was launched on 24th April 2020.
4(c) Covid-19 Roadmap to reopening society & business (Nationwide & Local)
Ms. R. McNally, Director of Services gave a background to the meeting on the national and local
COVID-19 Roadmaps to reopening society and business. She updated the meeting on the
establishment and work of the Galway City Mobility team (CMT). She advised that over 800
submissions were received to date and that the team are assessing same to consider the
allocation/reallocation of road and other public spaces to facilitate social distancing requirements
associated with COVID-19 restrictions. She advised that a number of duplicate submissions were
received which created additional work for the team. She informed the meeting that surveys and
street audits are currently being carried out in the city and that further information is available on the
Galway City Council website at www.galwaycity.ie/mobilitvteam.
4(d) Covid-19 - City Mobility Framework
Mr. U. Finn, Senior Engineer referred to the five stages of recovery in the national and local COVID-19
Roadmaps to reopening society and business. Mr. Finn advised the meeting that the impact of Covid-
19 caused a reduction in transport movements in Galway city since March. He said that as the city's
economy and society reopens and restrictions are slowly lifted, travel patterns will also change. He
outlined the role, scope and objectives of the City Temporary Mobility Plan including the interactive
portal on the Council website which facilitates stakeholders engagement.
6 IP age
Mr. Finn further advised that the CMT will consider measures such as wider footpaths, removal of
parking; one-way streets; safer bus stops and cycling facilities, traffic restrictions; byelaws regarding
30km/h zones; pedestrianisation of certain streets and supporting businesses who may need the use
of public space for social distancing purposes. A weekly update will continue to be sent to Councillors.
4(e) Covid-19 Business Support Grant
Mr. L. Mitchell, Senior Executive Officer referred Councillors to the report issued and updated
members on the Restart Grant, which will give direct grant aid to micro and small businesses. She
advised that it is a contribution towards the cost of re-opening or keeping a business operational and
re-connecting with employees and customers. The grant can be used to defray ongoing fixed costs,
for replenishing stock and for measures needed to ensure employee and customer safety.
Ms. Mitchell outlined that any business that has a commercially rateable premises, or a business that
occupies a premises and where rates are paid on their behalf, can apply subject to the criteria for
qualifying for the Grant as outlined in the report. She advised that the amount of grant ranges from
€2,000 to €10,000.
Ms. Mitchell advised that to date Galway City Council have received 531 completed online
applications. The Finance Department have commenced a process of validating and processing
applications received and expect to make the first Restart Grant Payments on or before Friday 12th
June 2020, subject to receipt of first tranche of funding, in advance from Department of Business
Enterprise & Innovation this week. Applications for the Restart Grant will be accepted online only
at: www.galwaycity.ie/restart. She summarised the appeals mechanism and advised that all queries
must be directed to ReStartGrant@galwavcitv.ie. She also outlined businesses that are not eligible for
the grant and confirmed the closing date for receipt of applications is 31 August 2020.
4(f) Impact of Covid-19 on City Council finances
Ms. N. Moggan, Financial Management Accountant referred to the Report issued outlining the current
Impact of COVID-19 on City Council Finances. With reference to the adopted budget 2020 a review
by Income Source was given with particular detail being given to Commercial Rates which represents
38% of Income for Galway City Council.
Ms. Moggan outlined the actions taken to date to manage and protect the finances of Galway City
Council during this COVID-19 period as outlined in the Report. The Council were advised that 18% of
our Expenditure is discretionary and that many of these have been parked until the financial impact is
fully assessed. The Local Authority Sector awaits details of the funding towards the Government
Waiver of Commercial Rates for those businesses impacted by the closures and a Government
response to the Goods and Services Income Impact submission. The position in relation to anticipated
further losses from commercial rates income post 27th June has yet to be confirmed. A submissions
on behalf of the CCMA to DHP&LG has recommended that full compensation is paid to local authorities
for the loss of these key Income Sources which are required to deliver local services and to contribute
towards some level of financial stability to allow local government contribute effectively to local
economic recovery. She added that Councillors will continue to be updated accordingly.

Housing Acts 1966 to 2014 or Part V of the Planning & Development Act 2000 will be allocated through
Choice Based Letting."
Mr. Mahon advised that as there will be a significant number of AHB properties available for allocation
from 2020 onwards, for which Galway City Council has nomination rights, the SPC recommend that the
Scheme of Letting Priorities is amended to allow for inclusion of AHB properties in Galway City Council's
CBL system. Therefore it is proposed that the current wording contained in Galway City Council's
Scheme of Letting Priorities (July 2017) as outlined above is replaced as follows: "Dwellings owned by
Galway City Council and provided for under the Housing Acts 1966 to 2014 or Part V of the Planning &
Development Act 2000, and Approved Housing Body Stockfor which Galway City has nomination rights
may be allocated through Choice Based Letting."
Joint Tenancy
Mr. Mahon advised that Joint Tenancy requests are considered by Galway City Council on a case-by-
case basis. Previously, requests from a Tenant to allow an adult child join the Tenancy was not
permitted and the applicant had to remain as an occupant only in the house. It is proposed to change
this policy and to allow adult children become Joint Tenants only in cases where this joint tenancy
would facilitate the purchase of the property through the Tenant Incremental Purchase Scheme. The
SPC have recommended that the following text be added as point 12.4 to the Scheme of Letting
Priorities:
Galway City Council will consider Joint Tenancy requests from adult children residing with a parent for
the purposes of the Tenant Incremental Purchase Scheme, under the following circumstances:
A formal written application for Joint Tenancy must be made to Galway City Council.
The application must be made jointly by the Tenant and the proposed Tenant and
must coincide with a Tenant Incremental Purchase Scheme application. Applications
will not be considered where they are made in the absence of an application for
Tenant Purchase.
The Joint Tenancy application will be processed alongside the Tenant Purchase
application. If for some reason, the Tenant Purchase application does not proceed,
Joint Tenancy will not be granted.
Applications will only be accepted for properties eligible for sale under the Tenant
Purchase Scheme. The terms and conditions of the Tenant Purchase Scheme in place
at the time of application are applicable.

The applicant must demonstrate that:
a) they have been resident in the dwelling for at least two years
b) they are an approved member of the tenant's household and included on the rent
declaration form in respect of the dwelling for their period of residence at the
property.
c) they meet the eligibility criteria to qualify for social housing support in accordance
with Section 20 of the Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 2009 and the Social
Housing Assessment Regulations
d) they have not engaged in anti-social behaviour in accordance with the terms of
Galway City Councils Anti- Social Behaviour Policy
e) they have not caused any breaches to the Tenancy Agreement while residing in
the dwelling that necessitated the issue of a warning letter to the Tenant under
sections 7, 8 or 9 of the Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2014
In these circumstances, Galway City Council may grant a Joint Tenancy to facilitate the purchase
of a property under the Tenant Purchase Scheme. Mr. Mahon advised that the proposed
