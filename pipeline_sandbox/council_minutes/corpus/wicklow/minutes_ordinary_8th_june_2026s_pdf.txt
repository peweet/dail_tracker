Request for Suspension of Standing Orders: - The Meetings Administrator advised that two
requests for suspension of standing orders had been submitted in advance of the meeting.
In accordance with the protocol adopted the requests were read to the meeting as proposed
and seconded: -
Suspension No. 1
Cllr. G. O'Neill, seconded by Cllr. J. Behan requested a suspension of standing orders to discuss
the following motion:
'That the Members of Wicklow County Council express our concern regarding the increasing
prevalence of tuberculosis in humans and in livestock in the country and county and invites
representatives of the Department of Agriculture to attend a future meeting of the Council to
discuss the situation and how it can be dealt with'.
It was agreed by all to have a discussion on the topic at 4.30 p.m
Suspension No. 2
Cllr. L. Scott, seconded by Cllr. L. Fenelon Gaskin and supported by Cllrs. S. Stokes, T. Fortune,
