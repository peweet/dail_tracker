ITEM NO. 6 
 
To consider and to make, with or without amendment the County Wicklow Polling 
Scheme 2023 
 
Ms. L. Gallagher, Director of Services advised that the Elected Members were circulated 
with a copy of the Draft Polling Scheme and a letter from the Senior Executive Officer in 
Corporate Services, Helen Purcell.  Local authorities are obliged to make a polling scheme 
and review it at least once every ten years.  The last polling scheme was made in 2013, 
adopted by the Elected Members in 2014.  Wicklow County Council is obliged to put it out to 
public consultation, which has been carried out.  The Council has consulted with the 
Returning Officer of the county, Ms. Mary Delahunty.  There are no changes proposed to the 
previous scheme as the changes made to the Bray LEA were implemented by ministerial 
order in 2018.  Therefore, there are no changes to the previous scheme.  It is a reserved 
function of the Elected Members to adopt the polling scheme. 
 
It was proposed by Cllr. J. Behan, seconded by Cllr. P. Leonard and agreed by all to consider 
and to make, without amendment the County Wicklow Polling Scheme 2023.
